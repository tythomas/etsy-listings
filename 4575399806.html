Description

Vintage Franciscan Gold Band 301 Merced Rim salad plates in cream china with gold trim measure approx. 8 3/8" across, Sets of 4 with 12 total available 

The photographed Franciscan backstamp and number 301 correspond to the cataloged Gold Band 301 pattern on the Merced rim shape. This discontinued china pattern from the Franciscan Masterpiece China line was produced in California from approximately 1942 to 1981.

These china salad plates are offered as replacement china for collectors building or completing a service. Each glazed cream china plate has a broad gold-tone outer band with fine concentric pin lines. The restrained cream-and-gold design makes these gold rim plates beautiful additions to an existing Gold Band service or elegant dessert, salad, or luncheon plates.
