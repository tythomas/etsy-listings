Over 70 different vintage amber goblets available ~ create your own custom mismatched set. Perfect for eclectic tablescapes, vintage weddings, retro bar carts and collectors of unique colored stemware.

- Glasses range from approximately:
  - 3" tall to 7.5" tall
  - Hold approximately 4 - 12 ounces

- All glasses were manufactured during different time periods ranging from the 1950s onward ~

- Beautiful variety of:
  - Mid Century Modern glassware
  - Retro amber stemware
  - Pressed glass
  - Cut glass
  - Heavy textured goblets
  - Elegant delicate stemware
  - Farmhouse and cottagecore styles
  - Vintage wedding glasses
  - Bar cart glassware

- Perfect for:
  - Mismatched wedding tables
  - Thanksgiving and fall entertaining
  - Vintage collectors
  - Eclectic home decor
  - Retro barware displays
  - Layered amber tablescapes

- All pieces are in VERY GOOD vintage condition with no chips, cracks or rough edged rims unless specifically noted below. Glasses with imperfections are marked with ** in the pull down menu and are sold last.

- Glass is free from clouding and water scaling. Since this glassware is vintage, there may be visible air bubbles from manufacturing, straw marks, mold lines or light surface scratches from age and gentle use.

- NOTE ~ We recommend hand washing your vintage glassware to maintain its beauty and avoid damage.

- Many of these patterns are discontinued and increasingly difficult to source in matching quantities, making this collection perfect for creating a one-of-a-kind curated vintage set.

- PLEASE carefully review measurements before purchasing since these glasses vary greatly in size, weight, shape and capacity.
