Description

Vintage black amethyst glass 16-hole flower frog, attributed to L.E. Smith, measuring 4 1/4 inches wide x 1 1/4 inches high.

Beautiful vintage black amethyst pressed-glass flower frog from the late 1920s to 1930s, attributed to L.E. Smith Glass Company. The glass appears nearly black in normal light and reveals a deep purple/amethyst tone when strong light passes through it.

This low-profile floral arranger has 16 stem openings and was designed to sit in a flower or console bowl, helping hold cut flowers securely in place. It also makes a striking standalone piece of vintage floral, Art Deco, or black glass decor.
