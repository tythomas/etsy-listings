Description

Homer Laughlin Orbit Tulip Time dinnerware with turquoise accents and blue floral sprigs. A 36-piece service for six, with 10 1/4-inch dinner plates.

This early 1960s ceramic dinnerware set features blue sprigs and beige-gray leaves on an ivory background, paired with turquoise cups and bowls. The coupe shapes and square-handled cups give the set its distinctive mid-century look.

The entire 36-piece set is included: six complete six-piece place settings, sold together rather than individually.
