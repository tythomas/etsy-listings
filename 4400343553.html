Vintage 1930s–1940s Yo-Yo Quilt – Full Size 86” x 71”
Hand-stitched yo-yo quilt made from authentic feed sack and dress cotton fabrics from the 1930s–40s. This classic open-work style quilt is made from hundreds of individually gathered rosettes (“yo-yos”) in a beautiful scrappy pattern with florals, stripes, checks, and small-scale geometric prints typical of Depression-era textiles.

This quilt measures 86” long x 71” wide, making it a true full-size coverlet. Yo-yo quilts were often made small, so this larger size is especially desirable for display, layering, or draping across a bed.
