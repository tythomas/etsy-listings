Description

Vintage turquoise blue Kemple Wheaton Lace & Dewdrop pressed glass dinnerware, sold individually in plates, bowls, cups, saucers and goblets.

Choose your piece from this beautiful vintage turquoise blue Kemple Wheaton Lace & Dewdrop glassware. Pieces are sold individually — see the variation menu for current pricing and availability.

MAKER: Kemple / Wheaton Glass Company  
PATTERN: Lace & Dewdrop  
ERA: Believed to date to the 1970s  
COLOR: Turquoise / aqua blue  
MATERIAL: Pressed glass  
MARKINGS: Pieces are marked with “K” and “W” on the underside

Kemple Wheaton's Lace & Dewdrop is an ornate pressed glass pattern featuring a raised beaded design, decorative scalloped edges and wonderful texture. The brilliant turquoise-aqua blue glass catches the light beautifully and makes these pieces especially striking when mixed into a vintage table setting.

The pattern has the ornate look collectors associate with Depression-style glassware, but these pieces are later vintage production believed to date to the 1970s.

A wonderful choice for anyone building a Kemple Wheaton collection, replacing missing pieces from an existing Lace & Dewdrop set, or creating a colorful mix-and-match vintage table.
