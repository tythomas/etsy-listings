Description

Vintage ruby red glass brides basket with a ruffled scalloped rim, fluted pressed-glass design, ornate gold-tone metal fittings, and a twisted overhead handle. Approximately 6 1/4 inches in diameter and 5 3/4 inches tall overall.

The glass is attributed to Imperial Glass in the Pillar Flute pattern, associated with the mid-20th century (approximately 1945–1955). The attribution is based on similar documented baskets; this unmarked example has not been definitively confirmed as Imperial. The decorative metal mounts give the basket a Victorian Revival appearance.

CONDITION
Vintage condition with no damage. A fine, slightly curved line is visible on the inside bottom of the glass and can be felt on the interior surface. It cannot be felt on the exterior bottom and stops before the circular edge. Its origin is uncertain; it may be a manufacturing irregularity or a shallow hairline. Please review the close-up photographs. One metal prong shows localized greenish discoloration/age-related patina. No maker's marks were found.
