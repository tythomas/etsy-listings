Description

A set of four hand blown drinking glasses, each one wrapped in a raised web of red, orange, teal and chartreuse trailed glass.

These are made the way Mexican recycled glass has always been made: the body blown by hand from clear glass with a faint seafoam cast, then ribbons of molten color laid onto the hot surface by hand. That is why no two are alike. The web runs differently around every glass, and the walls dimple where the glass was worked.

The color sits proud of the surface, so you can feel the ribbons under your fingers. Small seed bubbles are caught throughout the clear glass and the rims are gently out of round, both marks of hand blowing rather than faults. The underside carries a rough, unground pontil scar where each glass was held while it was finished. There is no maker's mark, stamp or label anywhere on them, which is usual for this glass.
