Description

Homer Laughlin Brittany maroon band china  -  cream, with a burgundy band, gold rim line and floral sprays, made 1939 to 1941. Each piece is stamped on the base HOMER LAUGHLIN, MADE IN U.S.A., with a date code — F39, K40 and G41.  So pieces were not all manufactured during the same time frame. 

MAKER: Homer Laughlin China Company, Newell, West Virginia
SHAPE: Brittany
PATTERN: B1315 — maroon band with scroll cartouches and multicolor floral sprays, gold trim
CIRCA: 1939-1941
