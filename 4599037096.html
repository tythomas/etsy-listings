Description

Vintage Robinson-Ransbottom spongeware stoneware crock with reddish-brown sponge decoration over a warm beige ground. Measures approx. 6 1/4" in diameter and 4 1/8" tall.

Made in Roseville, Ohio, and marked on the underside with the R.P. Co. Roseville, O. U.S.A. stamp and model #700-A. Believed to date to the 1950s or early 1960s; the exact production year has not been documented.

The crock has straight sides, an open top, a glossy beige glazed interior, and irregular reddish-brown sponge decoration on the exterior. A versatile piece for farmhouse or country kitchen display, dried stems, or countertop utensil storage. Props shown in photographs are not included.

CONDITION: Vintage condition with no chips or cracks reported. Light, localized glaze crazing is present, on one small area, pictured. Please review all photographs for the finish, details, and condition.
