GORGEOUS rainbow / primary colored dishes 

Made by Anchor Hocking in the late 30's and 40's these dishes equaled Fiesta ware and Bauer Ringware
Discontinued pattern - pieces are hard to find in good condition 

~ Rainbow Tangerine 
~ Rainbow Yellow ( bright) 
~ Rainbow Green ( medium) 
~ Rainbow Cobalt Blue 

LOTS of styles to choose from!!! 

* Ridged Tumbler - 4 1/2" tall - 9 ounces 
* Tall Champagne / Low Sherbet - 3 3/8" tall 
* Juice Tumbler - 3 1/2" tall - holds 5 ounces 
* Iced Tea Glasses / Parfait - 5 1/4" tall -  holds 10 ounces 

* Bread Plate - 6 1/4" in diameter
* Salad Plate 7 1/2"   diameter 
* Dinner plate 9 1/4" in diameter

*Cereal Bowl - 2 3/4" diameter x 5 3/8" tall 

All items in the listing are considered in VERY GOOD vintage condition with NO chips or cracks.  Most pieces have no scratching of the coloring with the exception of the plates which scratch more easily because they are typically stacked.  Plates were also the pieces that were used more for serving. Plate with the Orange and blue coloring show more loss than the yellow and green.  Light scratching in the coloring is noted on the plates mainly when the pieces are held up to direct lighting.  I tried to capture the pieces that were most scratched in the photos.  Pricing as been adjusted for the plates. 

This pattern is very hard to find in general, but even harder to find pieces that are in great condition with little scratching of the coloring!!!!
