Description

Set of six vintage Milano “Lava” Honey Gold tumblers by Anchor Hocking. Heavy textured 8 oz glasses from the late 1960s–70s—excellent condition.

Set of six vintage Milano (also known as Lava Glass) tumblers in the rich Honey Gold color by Anchor Hocking, produced between 1967–1978. This line is loved for its thick, sculptural texture and warm amber coloring—perfect for vintage barware collectors and anyone building a retro glass collection.

These 8 oz tumblers have a heavy, solid feel in the hand, making them ideal for cocktails, juice, iced tea, or bar cart display. The textured surface catches the light beautifully and adds mid-century charm to any table setting.

Details:

• Color: Honey Gold

• Quantity: Set of 6
