Vintage Monterey’s California speckled pottery serving pieces in light chocolate brown, pale green, and buttery yellow. Choose one individual piece from the options below; this listing is NOT for all five pieces together.

OPTIONS — EACH SOLD INDIVIDUALLY
• 1 Chocolate Platter — 11 x 8"; marked “Monterey’s Made in California U.S.A.”; excellent vintage condition, no reported chips or cracks.
• 1 Green Platter — 13" diameter; marked “Monterey’s Made in California U.S.A.”; excellent vintage condition, no reported chips or cracks.
• 1 Chocolate Bowl — 11 1/4" diameter x 3 3/4" tall; unmarked; excellent vintage condition, no reported chips or cracks. Three original firing support marks on the underside have small areas of exposed pottery; see photographs.
• 1 Yellow Divided Dish — 11 x 8 3/4" x 3" tall; marked “Monterey’s Made in California U.S.A.”; excellent vintage condition, no reported chips or cracks. Distinctive divided, leaflike form.
• 1 Chocolate Server — 10 1/4 x 4" x 2 1/2" tall; unmarked; small chip on the inside rim, shown in photographs. This piece has a glossier glaze than the other four; its maker and intended use are unconfirmed.

The five pieces came together from one individual’s collection. The two marked pieces carry a California-outline Monterey’s backstamp. The unmarked brown bowl coordinates closely in finish and speckled glaze and is believed to be from the same line, but  maker is not confirmed. The brown sauce server is included as a coordinating piece only, not as a verified Monterey’s product. Glaze is more glossy than the other pieces. 

Mid-century California pottery, tentatively circa 1950s–1960s. Collector sources associate Monterey dinnerware with Metlox, but that attribution and the exact production dates have not been independently verified for these examples.
