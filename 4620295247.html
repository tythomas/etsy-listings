Description

Original 1950s Samsonite hard-sided suitcase in deep navy blue with contrasting ivory trim, an ivory-colored handle, and brass-tone hardware. A striking midcentury piece for vintage luggage collectors, decorative storage, travel-themed displays, or photography styling. The original Shwayder Bros. manufacturer label identifies Style 4721.

The case opens to two compartments: one with tie-down ribbons and the other with a secured storage flap. The contrasting trim and original hardware give it wonderful vintage character.
