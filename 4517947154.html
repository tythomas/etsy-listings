Description

Glowing uranium glass from the late 1920s. This set of four Imperial Twisted Optic Green luncheon plates features the beautiful swirling optic pattern collectors love, with bright UV reactivity and exceptional vintage condition.

Produced by Imperial Glass of Ohio between 1927 and 1930, these Twisted Optic Green luncheon plates showcase one of the most striking swirl patterns of the early Depression-era glass period.

This listing is for ONE set of four matching plates. Three sets are currently available.  The 12 plates in this listing came from one owner and were stored for the majority of their life.  For a set of almost 100 year old plates, they are in fantastic condition!

Under UV blacklight, these plates glow a vivid bright green, confirming their uranium glass content. The swirling optic pattern catches and reflects light beautifully, making these equally appealing for display, collecting, entertaining, or period table settings.
