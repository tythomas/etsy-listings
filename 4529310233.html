Description

Set of five vintage Flintridge China gray salad plates measuring 8 1/2 inches in diameter. Made in California, these fine porcelain plates feature a wide dove gray rim with platinum trim and a clean Mid-Century Modern design that works equally well as replacement china or for an elegant vintage table setting.

Flintridge China was produced in Pasadena, California and became known during the 1950s and 1960s for its lightweight porcelain, quality craftsmanship, and contemporary designs. Today, Flintridge remains popular with collectors and buyers searching for replacement dinnerware.
