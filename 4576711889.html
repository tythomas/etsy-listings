Description

This attributed 19th-century stoneware vessel is a Korean Joseon jar and faceted storage jar base, approx. 7 3/4" tall with a 3 1/8" opening.

This unmarked stoneware jar has the tall, many-sided body, angular shoulder, recessed circular lid seat, glossy iron-brown glaze, and unglazed foot associated with late Joseon covered storage jars. Closely corresponding museum examples are identified as Korean Namwon'gun ware or iron-brown-glazed stoneware from the 19th century. The fitted lid is not present.

As a historic storage crock and kitchen storage jar, its form reflects household storage traditions. Collectors of brown glaze pottery may appreciate this brown pottery jar for its broad vertical facets and warm brown tonal variation, which give the vessel a strong sculptural profile for open shelving, a primitive decor collection, or a dried arrangement. One jar base is included; faux greenery and other staging pieces are not included.
