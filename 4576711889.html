Description

This attributed 19th-century stoneware vessel is a Korean Joseon jar and faceted storage jar base, approx. 7 3/4" tall with a 3 1/8" opening.

An unusual vintage pottery storage jar featuring a 16-sided faceted body, narrow opening and wonderfully uneven amber-to-deep-brown glaze. Variations in the glaze give the surface a warm, timeworn appearance that changes noticeably around the jar.

The vessel appears to have been made as a utilitarian food or pantry storage jar. Its faceted form and glaze resemble examples identified as Korean storage or honey jars; however, this piece is unmarked and has no known provenance. Its country of origin, maker and exact age cannot be confirmed. It is therefore being offered simply as an unmarked vintage pottery storage jar, possibly Korean.

Its narrow opening, deep interior and sturdy form make it an interesting piece for a pottery collection or primitive, rustic and wabi-sabi display.

CONDITION
Vintage condition with an aged, irregular surface. The brown glaze varies considerably in tone, ranging from lighter amber-brown areas to deeper brown, with visible glaze irregularities, crazing and firing character.

There is a chip along the bottom foot and a small area of glaze loss or flaking inside the jar, both shown in the photographs. Additional minor surface wear and imperfections are consistent with age and utilitarian pottery. No lid is included.
