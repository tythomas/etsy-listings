Description

Vintage ribbed glass Hoosier cabinet jars from the 1920s–1930s, sold individually so you can choose the Coffee, Tea, spice jar or shaker jar you need.

CHOOSE YOUR PIECE
• Coffee — 8 1/8" tall × 3 1/4" diameter
• Tea — 5" tall × 3" diameter
• Spice Jar — small ribbed jar with regular solid lid
• Shaker Jar — small ribbed jar with perforated shaker lid
• Rough Rim Jar — small ribbed jar with regular solid lid and roughness at the glass rim

The small jars measure approximately 4 1/2" tall with a 1 3/4" rim diameter.

These clear ribbed glass jars are the type made for early Hoosier-style kitchen cabinets and pantry storage. The larger jars have molded COFFEE and TEA lettering, while the smaller jars are useful as spice, seasoning or replacement cabinet jars.

All glass has been hand washed and is clean.

Coffee: Glass has no chips, cracks or other damage. The metal lid shows denting and age-related wear. A previous owner cut a floral-patterned plastic piece to fit inside the metal lid as a liner.

Spice Jar: Two available with regular solid lids and good glass rims. No chips, cracks or other glass damage.

Shaker Jar: Three available with perforated shaker lids. No chips, cracks or other glass damage.
