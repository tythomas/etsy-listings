Description

Vintage brown leather suitcase, Airess by Platt Luggage of Chicago — hard sided, brass hardware, about 1940s.

A small brass badge on the front reads Airess / Platt luggage with an airplane logo, and three applied brass letters spell the original owner's monogram, C.F.A.

Measurements:
• Width: 21"
• Height: 6.5"
• Depth: 15"

CONDITION
• Sold as decor — the leather is worn and has damage, described below

• Dark spotting and water marking across the top and along one lower panel
• Scratches and pale scuffs over the front, with an evenly darkened patina overall
• Wear along the edges, and rubbed dark corner caps
• Brass hardware is dull and pitted but all present — both Yale locks, both latches, the handle, the corner caps, the hinges, the maker's badge and the three applied monogram letters

• The lid pocket, the base pocket and the tie straps are all present; the elastic gathers are slack and lightly frayed
• The handle is solid and the case closes and latches
