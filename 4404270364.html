Description

Gorgeous large antique yellow ware mixing bowl with brown slip bands and feather-molded design. Light rim wear, no cracks, and classic firing marks. Dates to the 1880s–1910 era—perfect for farmhouse and primitive décor.

This large yellow ware mixing bowl is an authentic late-19th- to early-20th-century American stoneware piece, made circa 1880–1910. It features the warm mustard clay typical of early U.S. potteries, triple brown slip bands, and a molded feather/arc design around the shoulder—classic traits seen in early McCoy (pre-1911), Robinson Ransbottom, Monmouth, Western Stoneware, and other Midwestern producers of utilitarian kitchen wares.

The interior shows the potter’s wheel swirl, and the bowl has a beautiful aged patina from over a century of use. Large 14.5" yellow ware bowls are very desirable and make wonderful display pieces in farmhouse, primitive, and cottage-style kitchens.
