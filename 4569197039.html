Description

Set of two vintage Tiara Indiana Glass amber Sandwich pattern bowls, each approximately 6 3/8 inches across and 2 inches tall.

These matching pressed-glass bowls feature the Sandwich pattern’s molded flowers, curling scrolls, stippled panels, and scalloped rim. The 6 3/8-inch round-bowl form appears in the replacement pieces catalog for Tiara Sandwich Amber. Tiara marketed amber Sandwich glass from 1971 through 1989, placing this pair most likely around the late 1970s to early 1980s.

Their warm golden-amber color and detailed floral surface make them useful as dessert, fruit, berry, or small serving bowls. They also offer an attractive replacement opportunity for an existing Tiara Sandwich collection.

This listing is for one matching set of two bowls.
