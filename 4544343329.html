Description

Hand quilted Basket quilt measuring 66 x 78 inches with 42 uniquely pieced basket blocks, beautiful Baptist Fan quilting, and authentic vintage Midwestern charm.

A wonderful example of authentic American quiltmaking, this hand quilted Basket quilt features 42 beautifully pieced basket blocks, each created from a unique combination of vintage cotton fabrics. Acquired from an Illinois estate, the quilt was likely made during the late 1940s to early 1950s using treasured sewing scraps gathered over many years.

No two baskets are exactly alike. The maker thoughtfully combined probable feed sack fabrics, shirting fabrics, dress prints, plaids, florals, novelty prints and geometric cottons into one-of-a-kind basket blocks, while repeating warm mustard-gold triangles throughout the design to create visual harmony.

The quilt is entirely hand quilted in the classic Baptist Fan pattern, producing beautiful texture, soft drape, and the puckered appearance collectors appreciate. The original red floral border and binding provide a cheerful frame that complements the colorful basket blocks.
