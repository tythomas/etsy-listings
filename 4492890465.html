Description

Vintage Luminarc 1970s Wine Glasses w/ emerald green stems, French crystal liquor cordials, France bar cart glasses, vintage green stemware - perfect for any entertaining!

These wine glasses originate from the Alsace region of France and have a straight emerald green stem and a smaller size clear glass goblet. Originally made and designed by J. G. Durand Luminarc, cerca the Verrerie Cristallerie in d'Arques of France. 

The bottom of the glasses have the raised letting "FRANCE".

Each glass is in VERY GOOD vintage condition with no chips or  cracks. As with any gently used vintage item, there may be some slight surface scratches on the glass that can be seen when held to light - clear bowls appear to be free from clouding.  Rims are smooth to the touch !  Beautiful glassware!
