Description

Antique Noritake N1285 porcelain dinnerware, offered as one complete six-piece place setting. This beautiful early-20th-century Japanese china features delicate pink roses, blue and yellow flowers, ornate green scrollwork against a yellow and cream border, and elegant gold trim. Replacements Ltd. identifies N1285 as a pattern dating to circa 1918. The green Morimura M-in-wreath Noritake Hand Painted Japan backstamp is consistent with early Noritake production.

Each purchase includes ONE six-piece place setting:
• Dinner Plate — 10" diameter
• Bread Plate — 6½" diameter
• Salad Bowl — 7½" diameter
• Sauce Bowl — 5¼" diameter
• Teacup — 3¾" diameter × 2⅛" tall; holds 6 ounces
• Saucer — 5⅝" diameter

Condition: The five place settings currently offered have no chips, cracks or visible crazing. Please review the photographs for the pattern and finish of this antique china.

Price is for one six-piece place setting, not the entire collection. Select the quantity of place settings you wish to purchase. Matching individual replacement and serving pieces are offered separately in our shop, subject to availability.

Hand washing is recommended to help preserve the decoration and gold trim. Do not microwave.
