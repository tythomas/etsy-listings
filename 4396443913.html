Description

Beautiful 1940s–50s hand-embroidered quilt with blue floral blocks and blue backing. Fully hand quilted and stitched — fits Full bed or Queen topper

This charming hand-embroidered quilt dates from the late 1940s to early 1950s, when embroidery block kits were a favorite pastime for home quilters. Each block features a Colonial Flower or Floral Medallion motif — a pre-stamped embroidery pattern sold by Bucilla and Herrschner’s during the post-war years.

The quilt is crafted from white cotton muslin blocks embroidered in blue and golden-yellow cotton floss, bordered with vivid blue cotton sashing and finished with a solid blue backing. Every stitch — from embroidery to quilting — was done by hand, showing beautiful workmanship with small, slightly varied quilting stitches and gentle texture.
