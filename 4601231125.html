Description

Set of 6 vintage Fostoria Meadow Rose etched champagne coupes / tall sherbet glasses in clear glass, each measuring 5 5/8" tall with a 3 3/4" rim and approximately 5-ounce capacity.

These elegant glasses feature delicate floral sprays and scrolling etched decoration on a wide, flared bowl, paired with an ornate molded Wilma stem (Fostoria No. 6016). The floral etching is identified as Meadow Rose, No. 328. Fostoria produced Meadow Rose during approximately 1936–1975. Beautiful for champagne, cocktails, desserts, vintage table settings, or wedding celebrations.
