Description

Elegant Williams-Sonoma martini glasses with wide silver band, 7.5” tall. Discontinued Turkish-made barware, minimal silver wear.

Add timeless elegance to your barware with this set of two Williams-Sonoma “Silver Banded” martini glasses, crafted in Turkey and now discontinued.

Each glass measures 7½ inches tall, with a 5-inch rim diameter and a 3⅜-inch base. The design features a striking ¾-inch mirrored silver band encircling the rim — hand-applied for a sleek, modern look that reflects Williams-Sonoma’s quality craftsmanship.

These glasses are in very good vintage condition. Light silver wear is visible only when held to direct light, one glass showing slightly more than the other — still minimal overall. No chips or cracks. Heavy, clear glass construction with excellent balance.

Perfect for serving martinis, cocktails, or displaying on a bar cart.
