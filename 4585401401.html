Description

Antique 1930s scrappy Nine Patch quilt, hand pieced and extensively hand quilted in a wonderful mix of period cotton prints and woven fabrics. Pale green printed sashing and yellow floral cornerstones organize the colorful blocks of navy, burgundy, pink, blue, checks, stripes, shirtings and small florals.

Measures approximately 67 x 77 inches.

The quilt has cotton batting and an off-white cotton backing. The hand quilting creates lovely texture throughout, with diagonal quilting across the patchwork and sashing. The varied fabrics give this quilt especially strong display presence while retaining the practical, collected-scrap character of an early 20th-century handmade quilt.

Dating: Based on the fabric assortment, construction and quilting, this quilt is attributed to the 1930s. Some individual scraps may predate the finished quilt.

Condition: Good antique condition with limited localized wear. There is one area of fabric loss/wear on a navy blue patch, shown in the close-up photos. One small area along the green binding is worn/frayed with a little cotton batting visible. The off-white backing has a few small areas of age-related discoloration/spotting. No widespread fabric deterioration has been noted. Please review all photographs closely for condition and the individual fabric details.

The quilt has been gently hand soaked and smells clean, with no musty or storage odor.

A beautiful authentic antique textile for a bed layer, quilt rack, folded display, farmhouse interior, cottage decor or vintage textile collection.
