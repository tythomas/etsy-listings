Description

Vintage Fostoria Engagement champagne coupes / tall sherbet glasses with platinum rims, measuring approximately 5½" tall and 4" across the bowl. Made during the discontinued Engagement pattern's 1960–1982 production period and available by condition as singles or matching sets.

Fostoria's Engagement pattern features clear blown glass with a clean platinum band at the rim and an elegant mid-century stem. These glasses work beautifully as champagne coupes, cocktail glasses or tall sherbet/dessert glasses and are especially useful for replacing missing pieces from an existing Engagement service.
