Description

Authentic 1930s Dresden Plate quilt made with colorful feed sack cotton fabrics, orange sashing, and classic Depression-era charm.

This full size 72" x 86" antique quilt is a beautiful statement piece for collectors, cottage bedrooms, farmhouse decor, or vintage textile display.

Bring a piece of Depression-era charm into your home with this stunning vintage 1930s Dresden Plate quilt. Hand-stitched from authentic feed sack and calico cotton fabrics, this quilt features the classic Dresden Plate pattern—each “petal” cut from colorful prints and appliquéd onto a crisp white background. Bold pumpkin-orange sashing and borders frame each circular plate, creating a warm and cheerful statement piece.

Every patch tells a story—tiny florals, ginghams, and geometric prints typical of the 1935–1940 period reflect the resourcefulness and artistry of early 20th-century American quilters. This quilt is both a collectible textile and a beautiful heirloom accent for cottage, farmhouse, or vintage-inspired décor.
