Description

Set of four vintage Dolch First Reading Book clothbound hardcovers with illustrated animal stories and former-school-library markings.

This matched reading-book group includes Some Are Small, In the Woods, Once There Was a Bear, and Once There Was a Monkey. Edward W. and Marguerite P. Dolch titles published by Garrard Press, the books span works originally issued from 1958 through 1962. Their repeated El Paso Elementary Schools stamps include a 1967 Title I ESEA date.

Each hardcover has a colored cloth binding, pictorial two-color cover, paper text block, and limited-color interior illustrations. The blue, green, yellow-green, pink, and aqua palette makes the four-book group appealing for a vintage classroom display, children's-book collection, or replacement library.

Sold together as one set of four books. The wooden stand and other items shown in images are display props and are not included.
