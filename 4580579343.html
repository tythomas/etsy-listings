Description

Vintage yellow Coca-Cola wooden bottle crate with bold red advertising graphics, galvanized metal reinforcement and an uncommon three-cell divided interior. Dating evidence from comparable yellow Coca-Cola wooden carriers and this crate’s construction supports a circa 1960s to early 1970s date.

The long sides read “Drink Coca-Cola in Bottles,” while the end features “Have a Coke” and “3 Cell.” The interior retains additional Coca-Cola graphics and a Sturdi-Bilt beverage case marking. Its low three-section form makes an especially attractive piece for vintage advertising display, farmhouse storage, bottle collecting or soda fountain decor.

MEASUREMENTS
18 1/2" long x 12" wide x 4 1/2" high
Interior depth: approximately 3 7/8"

CONDITION
Authentic used vintage condition with age-related wear throughout. The original yellow painted finish and red Coca-Cola advertising remain bright and highly visible, with paint loss, fading, scratches, scuffs, discoloration and surface wear from use. The galvanized metal edging and divider hardware show oxidation, darkening and age wear.

The wood has marks, scratches, small nicks and areas of discoloration. The underside shows heavier expected wear and soiling, including splitting/cracking to portions of the bottom slats. One section of the metal reinforcement along the bottom edge is bent/separated at the joint, as pictured. Small edge losses/splintering are also present around the worn bottom boards. The three-cell dividers remain present and both hand openings are intact.

Please review all photographs closely, especially the underside, as they are an important part of the condition description.
