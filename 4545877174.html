Description

Mid-century bread and butter plates in Creative Fine China's Platinum Starburst pattern, number 1014, made in Japan and measuring 6 3/8 inches across.

These date to the 1960s, when Japanese factories were putting spare, modern decoration on plain white porcelain for the American table. The starburst blossoms sit off to one side rather than in the middle, leaving most of the face open white.

The backstamp reads Creative / FINE CHINA / 1014 / JAPAN with a small circled S, so the pattern number is confirmed rather than guessed. The blossoms are transfer printed, each with a small red dot at its center and thin gray stems drawn between them, and the rim carries a continuous platinum band rather than gold. The plates are a true coupe shape - no separate rim or well, just one shallow curve from the center out to the edge.
