Description

Authentic handmade Ohio Star variation quilt measuring 72" x 64", hand pieced and hand quilted in Illinois circa 1945–1955.

Bring authentic mid-century American craftsmanship into your home with this beautiful handmade Ohio Star variation quilt. Family made in Illinois, this vintage quilt dates to approximately 1945–1955 and features colorful hand-pieced star blocks joined by bold red, green, and yellow sashing that creates a striking graphic design.

Each block is made from a wonderful assortment of vintage cotton prints including florals, gingham, novelty prints, geometric patterns, and solids, making every star unique. Fine hand quilting throughout adds wonderful texture while the original yellow fabric binding completes the quilt beautifully.

Measuring approximately 72 inches by 64 inches, this quilt works well as a throw quilt, twin bed cover, wall display, quilt ladder accent, or farmhouse and cottage home décor. The cheerful colors and classic Ohio Star variation make it equally at home in traditional, primitive, country, or cottagecore interiors.

This quilt has not been laundered and remains in found condition. The front displays beautifully with vibrant colors and no significant holes or major structural damage noted.
