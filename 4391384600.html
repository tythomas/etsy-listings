Gorham Fine China USA Norman Rockwell American Landscape – Four Seasons plates: Spring Recess, Summer Respite, Autumn Reflections, Winter Delight. 10⅞", gold floral trim. Excellent

Bring classic Americana to your table or gallery wall with this vintage set from Norman Rockwell’s “American Landscape – Four Seasons.” Produced by Gorham Fine China, USA as a Limited Annual Edition. The scenes depict life in 1959; Gorham issued its Four Seasons plates later (early 1970s) under its limited-edition program.
