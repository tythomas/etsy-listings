Description

Vintage State Farm Insurance Companies brown briefcase with gold company lettering, brass-tone locking hardware, two working locks and two included keys. Measures approximately 17 inches wide × 11½ inches high × 3 inches deep; 14 inches tall when standing to the top of the handle.

This handsome vintage document case has a slim structured exterior, carrying handle, fabric-lined interior and accordion-style file compartments with a retaining strap. An inspector’s slip is included. The interior label bears a Globe-style logo and model marking 21-DL-120; the exact manufacturer and production date have not been independently confirmed. The exterior has a brown leather-look finish; genuine leather composition is not confirmed.

HISTORICAL CONNECTION
State Farm was founded in Bloomington, Illinois, in 1922, and maintains its headquarters there. This briefcase was purchased at an estate sale in Bloomington, Illinois. Its State Farm branding and local discovery suggest a possible connection to an employee or insurance representative, but the original owner and any use at company headquarters are not documented.
