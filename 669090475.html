Description

Listing cost is for a set of TWO light olive green roly poly tumblers in Libbey's "Tempo" pattern ~ 

Originally launched in the 1950s, the TEMPO  pattern stayed in production for several decades. The  simple lines of this pattern along with a variety of colors to choose from helped make this pattern popular. Roly poly lines make these glasses a great addition to any retro bar or breakfast table! 

Each glass measures approximately 4 1/2" tall, with a 2  1/2" top diameter and a 1  1/4"" base.    Capacity (when filled at a normal distance from rim) is approximately 10 oz. 
Bottoms are marked with the cursive Libbey  L 

VERY GOOD vintage condition with no chips or cracks ~ some slight surface scratching from gentle use can be seen if you hold the glasses to direct light. Nice shine- no cloudiness - rims are smooth to the touch with no chips or fleabites.  Some have original sticker on them

Perfect addition to your retro bar!!
