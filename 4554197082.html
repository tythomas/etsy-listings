Description

Noritake "Painted Desert" stoneware, pattern 8603, made in Japan. Pieces are sold individually here, so you can fill one gap or build up a whole setting.

Noritake made Painted Desert as part of its Stoneware line in the late 1970s and early 1980s, back when heavy, oven-to-table dishes were what people wanted for every day. The rim carries a speckled sandy brown band over a soft slate blue one, on a warm cream body. Desert colors, which is where the name comes from.

The stoneware is thick and opaque, and a gentle ridged throwing line runs around the plate wells and the sides of the bowls. Every piece is marked underneath: Noritake, STONEWARE, 8603, PAINTED DESERT, MADE IN JAPAN, along with oven, microwave and detergent safe. That mark is what confirms the pattern and dates the line.
