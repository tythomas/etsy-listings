Description

A wonderfully aged vintage wood jewelry or keepsake box dating to approximately the 1940s–1950s. The box features an inset scenic landscape print surrounded by ornate pressed floral and scroll decoration, dovetailed corner construction, four small block feet and a mirror mounted inside the lid.

Boxes of this general form were produced during the mid-20th century as decorative gift and keepsake boxes, with similar examples associated with candy gift packaging and later reused for jewelry, letters and personal treasures. This example is unmarked, so no specific manufacturer is attributed.

The front wooden knob provides a convenient place to lift the lid. Inside is a simple unfinished wood compartment approximately 2½" deep. The original mirror remains mounted beneath the lid.

Condition

This box is being offered in its authentically aged vintage condition and has not been restored or refinished.

The overall construction remains solid. The scenic image on the lid shows considerable age-related wear, including cracking, lifting/flaking and areas of image loss. The mirror is not cracked or chipped but has substantial clouding and deterioration/loss of the reflective silvering.

One dovetailed corner of the lid has a visible crack/separation. The hinges remain attached but have some wobble. The original metal lid stay is present; however, the small nail/pin that secured its lower end is missing. The stay is therefore disconnected and does not currently prevent the lid from opening too far. The lid should be supported when opened.

Additional scratches, finish wear and surface marks are present from age and use. The interior is free of staining and odors, with some dust remaining in the corners.

Please review all photographs closely, particularly the scenic image, mirror, dovetailed corner and lid-stay hardware, as they are an important part of the condition description.
