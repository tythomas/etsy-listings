Description

Vintage 1970s Design Gifts International serving tray featuring a large woven rattan flower and wood-veneer botanical motifs embedded beneath thick clear acrylic. Two raised silver-tone metal handles, a black outer rim and warm natural tones give this round tray a bold bohemian look for serving drinks or styling a coffee table or home bar.

MEASUREMENTS
Approximately 14 inches in diameter.

MATERIALS AND CONSTRUCTION
Thick clear acrylic with embedded woven cane or rattan and wood-veneer decoration, black outer rim and silver-tone metal handles. A portion of the original Design Gifts label remains on the underside. The exact wood and cane species are unknown.

CONDITION
Good vintage condition with visible surface scratching and scuffing across the clear acrylic, particularly over the center design. The silver-tone handles show light surface wear, and only part of the original paper label remains on the underside. No cracks, chips or structural damage were observed.
