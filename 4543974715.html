Description

Pink Depression-glass stemware by Central Glass Works, pattern #1426 ~ elegant twisted 'rope' stems with wheel-cut floral bowls, offered in two forms: tall water goblets and wide champagne / tall sherbet coupes. Choose your set from the dropdown.

Central Glass Works of Wheeling, West Virginia was one of the fine 'elegant glass' houses of the Depression era; the #1426 twisted-stem line in soft rose pink dates to roughly the 1920s to 1930s. (Identified by pattern match; like most Depression glass, the pieces are unmarked.)

Each piece has a beautifully twisted spiral stem, a light optic-paneled bowl, and a delicate wheel-cut floral and leaf spray, with the naturally warm pink glow collectors love.

• Maker / pattern: Central Glass Works, #1426, Pink
• Stem: twisted 'rope' spiral
• Decoration: wheel-cut floral and leaf, optic-paneled bowl
• Two set options (select in the dropdown):
   - Water Goblets ~ set of 4
   - Champagne / Tall Sherbet Coupes ~ set of 6

CONDITION 
Excellent  No chips, cracks, hairlines, or flakes; no cloudiness, the pieces ring clear. Bright, clean, display-ready stemware with only the faintest wear consistent with careful use and age.
