Description

This Adams Cattle Scenery 9-inch transferware plate has a pastoral center scene and molded basketweave rim. Its deep purple transferware print rests on an ivory china ground, with a scalloped edge framing the scene. 

Sold individually, this china dinner plate is Staffordshire china and 1900s china for an antique table or collection. The pictured Adams Est. 1657 Cattle Scenery England mark was used from 1914–1940, supporting an early-20th-century attribution without claiming an exact production year.
