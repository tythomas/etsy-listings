Four authentic pink Depression Glass sherbet cups featuring four different collectible patterns from the 1920s, 1930s, and 1940s, beautifully curated into one charming mismatched dessert set.

Four authentic pink Depression Glass sherbet cups featuring four different collectible patterns from the 1920s, 1930s, and 1940s, beautifully curated into one charming mismatched dessert set.

Whether displayed in a Depression Glass collection or used to serve ice cream, berries, pudding, mousse, or parfaits, this set offers the collected-over-time character that vintage glass enthusiasts love. Each piece represents a different iconic pattern and manufacturer, creating a unique grouping that spans nearly two decades of American glassmaking history.

Included in this set ~ 
- Holiday Pink by Jeannette Glass
- Produced 1947-1949
- Approximately 2 3/4" tall
- Approximately 3 1/2" top diameter
- Footed sherbet dessert cup

- Mayfair Pink (Open Rose) by Anchor Hocking
- Produced 1931-1937
- Approximately 3 3/8" tall
- Approximately 3 5/8" top diameter
- Holds approximately 4 ounces

- Normandie Pink by Federal Glass
- Produced 1933-1940
- Approximately 2 7/8" tall
- Approximately 3 5/8" top diameter
- Footed sherbet dessert cup

- Block Optic Pink by Anchor Hocking
- Produced 1929-1933
- Approximately 3 1/4" tall
- Approximately 3 1/2" top diameter
- Holds approximately 4 ounces
