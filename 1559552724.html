Beautifully crafted Large vintage 1980s hand crocheted afghan blanket with a rippled pattern in of shades of blue

Measures approximately: 58" long x 55" wide - material has some stretch to it.  These measurements are when the blanket is not pulled tight. 

Made of an easy care type of yarn but I'm not sure of the fiber content - I am guessing it is a polyester/cotton/rayon mix. Blanket is lightweight and stitches are more loopy than tight. 

GOOD preowned condition- no stains, tears or fading.  Light piling / pulling of the yarn noted in places, primarily on the edges.  This appears as stray yarn - see photos. Blanket has the original maker's name sewn into the corner of the piece - see photos. 

This piece has been laundered and air dried. Comes from a smoke free home. There are a remaining couple stray hairs noted in the yarn - unsure of these are human or animal.
