These elegant white milk glass goblets would be perfect to add to a collection or to start a new one!  
The goblets have a gracefully curved stem and square foot with a carved design on the bottom.  This thumb print or polka dot design of the body has a cross hatch border.  The McKee Company produced this as the Opal Line in the early 50s and they evoke the mid-century style perfectly. 
Manufactured from 1950 - 52 and now discontinued make these a highly collectible pattern! 

Goblets could be used for small floral display as a  make up brush holder ~ or the finishing touch to your holiday table !
