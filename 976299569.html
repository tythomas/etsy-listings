Description

38-piece vintage hand painted china set with blue floral detail. Includes 12 cups, 12 saucers, 12 luncheon plates, and 2 serving plates.

Vintage hand painted china set with blue floral and scroll border detail.

This is a 38-piece luncheon / tea party set, hand painted in 1963 and signed with the initials EH. The china appears to be from the era when plain white raised-pattern china blanks were purchased and painted by hand. Maker of the original blank china is unknown.

Set includes:
* 12 flat cups
* 12 saucers
* 12 luncheon plates
* 2 round serving plates

Measurements:
* Cups: approx. 2 1/4” tall, 4” across top, hold approx. 6 oz
* Saucers: approx. 5 3/4” diameter
* Luncheon plates: approx. 8 1/4” diameter
* Serving plates: approx. 10 1/4” diameter

Condition:
Very good vintage condition with no chips, cracks, or discoloration noted. Glaze has a nice shine. Light visible surface scratching noted on the plates. One cup has a small chip on the base. Blue accent color may vary slightly from piece to piece.

Listing is for the full 38-piece set.
