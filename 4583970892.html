Description

A visually powerful antique Pineapple Log Cabin quilt with exceptional display presence, intricate hand piecing, saturated jewel-tone solids, and an unusual pieced triangle border. Approx. 81" x 84".

Likely dating to circa 1900-1920, approximately c.1910, this quilt is hand pieced and hand quilted in the Pineapple Log Cabin pattern. Small multicolor centers radiate into sharply pointed cream sections surrounded by rich purple, cobalt blue, teal, red, tan and brown solids. The repeating geometry creates a bold, almost modern effect from across the room.

The quilt is finished with narrow blue and cream borders and a striking outer border of alternating red and blue triangles. The fabrics are very soft and supple from age and use. Cotton batting. The reverse is a solid off-white fabric with a few areas of light discoloration.

CONDITION
This is an authentic, well-used antique textile and is not a pristine quilt. It remains visually striking and displays beautifully, but age and wear are present.

There is one small tear in a tan/beige fabric with batting visible, several small/open seams, and notable fraying/open seams along portions of the pieced outer border, particularly in some red triangle sections. There are scattered spots and light age discoloration on the quilt top and a few light discoloration areas on the backing.

Several purple fabric pieces show pronounced irregular color variation, ranging from deep violet to lavender, blue-gray and pinkish-purple. The exact cause of this mottling/color change is unknown and it should not be assumed to be an intentional fabric design. Please review the close-up photographs for representative examples.

The quilt is being sold unwashed. No attempt has been made to treat the spots, discoloration, or color variation.

Despite its age-related wear, this is an unusually graphic antique quilt with tremendous character and strong display presence. Its complex Pineapple geometry, saturated colors and hand workmanship make it especially appealing as a bed quilt, wall display, or statement textile in an antique or folk-art collection.
