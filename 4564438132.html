Description

Antique Haviland Ranson (Schleiger No. 1) white Limoges porcelain serving pieces, made in France circa 1893–1931 and sold individually by variation.

Choose from a handled vegetable dish, a gravy boat with attached underplate, or an oval vegetable bowl. Each piece has Ranson’s all-white scalloped form with raised molded leaf and ribbon details. The green Haviland France backstamps are consistent with this production period. Each variation purchases one individual piece; the group photo shows all available choices.

These useful serving forms mix naturally with an existing Ranson service or add sculptural white French porcelain to a collected table. The price shown changes with the selected piece.

• Gravy boat with attached underplate: approximately 9" long × 4 1/4" wide × 3" high
