Description

One antique charcoal box iron, also known as a coal iron, with a worn wooden handle and pointed soleplate, offered as a decorative laundry collectible.

This unmarked coal-burning clothes iron has the hollow, boat-shaped body and sliding knobbed top control associated with charcoal box irons. Its construction is consistent with examples used around the turn of the 20th century, although no maker or patent mark is visible.

The dark cast iron body retains extensive oxidation and a deeply aged surface. A broad arched wooden handle and turned wooden control knob add warm contrast to the pointed industrial form. Its metal and wood construction makes compelling primitive decor, sewing room decor, farmhouse decor, or an old fashioned addition to a historical household-tool collection.
