Description

Matched set of three 1960s Scott, Foresman The New Basic Readers hardcovers: one Primer and Book 2 Parts 1 and 2.

This complete three-volume progression includes Fun with Our Friends (Primer), Friends Old and New (Book 2, Part 1), and More Friends Old and New (Book 2, Part 2). These familiar Dick and Jane readers also feature Sally, Susan, Pete, Billy, Spot, and Puff.

The illustrated yellow, green, and gray cloth-over-board bindings contain color illustrations and early elementary reading text. A photographed copyright page reads © 1963 by Scott, Foresman and Company. Contemporary catalog records identify Fun with Our Friends as the 1962 Primer and both Book 2 parts as 1963 titles; individual printing statements for all three books are not shown.

Sold together as one set of three books. Their colorful period covers and sequential reading levels make the group appealing for a vintage classroom collection, nostalgic reading display, or Dick and Jane collection.
