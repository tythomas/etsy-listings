Antique 1930s postage stamp quilt with colorful feed sack fabrics, hand quilting, and a soft pink border.

Antique Depression-era postage stamp quilt with beautiful color, hand quilting, and wonderful display appeal.

This antique postage stamp quilt dates to approximately the 1930s to early 1940s and features a striking concentric scrap patchwork design made from small fabric squares. The quilt includes a wonderful mix of Depression-era and feed sack style fabrics, including florals, stripes, shirtings, dots, checks, and colorful novelty-style prints.

The design radiates from a red and white center medallion and is framed with multiple colorful scrap borders, finishing with a soft pink outer border. It has strong visual appeal and displays beautifully folded over a chair, across the end of a bed, on a quilt ladder, or as part of a vintage farmhouse collection.
