Description

Calumet double-acting baking powder tin — 1 lb size, red lithographed metal with the chief portrait, 5 3/8" tall. The lettering is printed straight onto the metal rather than on a paper label, and the front carries the Combination Type wording Calumet used on its mid-century cans.

Measurements:
• Height: 5 3/8"
• Diameter: 3"

CONDITION
• Red paint is rubbed and scuffed, with fine scratches
