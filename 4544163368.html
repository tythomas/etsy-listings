Description

Vintage 1930s to 1940s scrappy Wild Goose Chase quilt, hand-pieced and hand-quilted, with dozens of scrappy pieced blocks set on point against a warm dusty-rose setting. Bright, clean condition. Measures approximately 66 x 78 inches.

A cheerful vintage scrap quilt that brings real Depression-era charm to a farmhouse, cottage, or cottagecore bedroom. Each block is a flying-geese Wild Goose Chase block - also known as a star, pinwheel, or windmill block - pieced from a different vintage print (feed-sack florals, plaids, gingham, and bold Art Deco geometrics) and set on point, framed by a soft dusty-rose (double-pink) setting, so every block is its own little fabric time capsule. Beautiful on a twin or guest bed, folded on a quilt ladder, draped over a chair, or hung as textile art.

The fabrics date it to approximately the 1930s to 1940s. It is entirely hand-pieced and hand-quilted with fine running stitches - close diagonal and cable quilting across the rose setting - over a soft cotton batting, with a plain cream cotton back and a rose floral binding.
