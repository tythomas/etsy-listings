Description

Vintage Johnson Brothers Rock City Gardens souvenir plate, featuring detailed blue-and-white scenic transferware illustrations of Lookout Mountain attractions. Measures 10" in diameter.

Made in Staffordshire, England, this decorative plate bears the Old English Staffordshire Ware Johnson Brothers backstamp and was imported exclusively for Rock City Gardens, Lookout Mountain. The central scene depicts Rock City, surrounded by vignettes including Sky Bridge, Balance Rock, Observation Point, Moonshine Still, and the See Seven States overlook. Traditional floral ornamentation frames the scenes.
