Description

Vintage Anchor Hocking Rainbow color-fired glass from the late 1930s-40s, sold individually in colorful serving and decorative styles.

Vintage Anchor Hocking Rainbow glassware dating to the late 1930s-1940s, offered individually by style.

These collectible pieces feature Anchor Hocking’s bright fired-on colors over clear glass, including Rainbow Tangerine / Orange, Bright Yellow, Medium Green and Cobalt Blue.

Choose from an assortment of serving and accent pieces including bowls, cups and saucers, cream and sugar pieces, candlesticks, salt and pepper shakers and vases.

A wonderful addition to an Art Deco kitchen, colorful vintage table setting or collection of period Fiesta go-along glassware.

CONDITION
• Good vintage condition overall.
• The glass pieces are free of cracks. Except where specifically noted below, no chips are present.

• Because Rainbow glassware has fired-on color over clear glass, some scratching and wear to the colored surface is expected with age and use. Most pieces show little to light surface wear, primarily noticeable when held toward direct light.

• The larger ribbed bowls show more color wear, most likely from use and stacking.
