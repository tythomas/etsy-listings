Description

Matching vintage Deep-Tones by Crown luggage set in rich burgundy with contrasting cream leather trim, aged brass-toned hardware, and sturdy original handles. This coordinated suitcase and train case pair makes an eye-catching stacked display for mid-century interiors, vintage-themed events, photography props, or shop decor. Sold together as the two pieces shown; intended primarily for decorative use rather than modern travel.

The larger suitcase has a burgundy-lined interior with gathered fabric pockets and tie-down ribbons. The smaller train case retains its interior organizer loops. Both cases carry the Deep-Tones by Crown badge. Estimated mid-20th century, likely 1940s–1950s; exact manufacture year has not been verified.

MEASUREMENTS (approximate)
Large suitcase: 21" wide x 18" tall (excluding handle) x 8 1/4" front to back across top; 5" interior depth.
Train case: 13 1/2" side to side x 9" front to back x 7 3/4" tall (excluding handle); 5" interior depth.

CONDITION
Vintage condition with exterior scuffs, scratches, aged patina on metal hardware, and discoloration, creasing, and wear on the cream leather trim. Both handles are secure and sturdy; the smaller train case handle has more pronounced yellowing. On both cases the right-hand latch does not flip automatically and must be moved manually into place, but it does secure. The large suitcase has interior wear and localized discoloration. The train case interior has substantial cosmetic wear, scuffing, and discoloration; its original mirror is almost entirely missing, with small cracked glass fragments remaining in the corners. Handle and latch function does not make these suitable for regular travel. Both cases have a light old-storage smell. Please examine all photographs carefully for condition and details.
