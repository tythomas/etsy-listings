Vintage 1970s turquoise enamelware colander with black trim, side handles and a footed base. Bold teal-blue color for farmhouse, country or primitive kitchen display.

This vintage enamelware colander has a saturated turquoise/teal-blue finish accented by black trim, two side handles and a raised footed base. The strong color and classic enamelware construction make it a useful vintage kitchen piece as well as an attractive display piece for open shelving, a farmhouse kitchen, pantry or country-style collection.

This is a genuinely used vintage piece. There are two areas of enamel loss on the underside/bottom where the metal is exposed, with light surface rust in one of those areas. There is also roughness beneath the rim. The black trim remains intact. Please review the listing photos closely for the condition and enamel wear.

A distinctive piece of vintage turquoise enamelware for collectors, rustic kitchen displays or anyone adding color to a vintage-inspired kitchen.
