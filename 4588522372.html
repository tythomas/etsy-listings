Description

Vintage Kentucky Club sampler with six empty tobacco tins, original plastic holder and federal tax stamp. Holder measures 13 1/2" x 2 1/2" x 2 1/2".

A coordinated six-tin pipe tobacco sampler from the Kentucky Club product family, made by Mail Pouch Tobacco Co. of Wheeling, West Virginia. The cream, red, yellow and burgundy tins retain their vintage lettering and advertising graphics, along with the original clear plastic presentation holder and surviving 6-ounce U.S. Internal Revenue tobacco tax stamp.

The six blends included are:
• Brush Creek
• Whitehall
• Donniford
• Crosby Square
• London Dock
• Peper's Pouch Mixture

Circa 1950s, likely 1952–1959. The “Act of February 26, 1926” wording on the tax stamp refers to the revenue act, not the manufacturing year. Each tin was originally labeled for 1 ounce; these are empty collectible tins, with minor residual tobacco residue from prior use.

MEASUREMENTS
Original holder: approximately 13 1/2" long x 2 1/2" high x 2 1/2" deep.
Individual tins: approximately 2 1/4" square each.

CONDITION
Age-related surface scratches, spotting, soiling and wear to the printed tin graphics. Interiors are clean overall, with minor rusting and residue. The original plastic holder shows surface wear, scratching, cloudiness and yellowing from age. The surviving tax stamp is separated/loose and is included - no tears or rips.  Please review the photos for the visible wear and condition of the tins, holder and stamp.

Sold together as one set of six empty tins with the original holder and tax stamp. A colorful display for a tobacciana, vintage advertising or collectible tin collection.
