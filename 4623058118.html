Description

Antique Dietz Vesta railroad lantern marked NY NH & H for the New York, New Haven & Hartford Railroad, featuring a striking red glass globe. A wonderful piece of American railroad history for a train collection, industrial display, or vintage railroad-themed room.

The metal lantern carries a Dietz New York manufacturer's marking and the NY NH & H railroad initials. The red globe has embossed lettering identified as “Dietz Vesta” and “New York USA,” along with a visible “U.S.” marking. The globe is a Vesta-branded - marked - see photos
