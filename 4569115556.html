Description

A striking set of seven vintage Anchor Hocking Gay Nineties tall tumblers in rich forest green glass with crisp white transportation graphics.

Made by Anchor Hocking from approximately 1950–1965, these are the large 16 oz flat tumblers from the Gay Nineties line. Their tall shape works beautifully for highball drinks, Tom Collins cocktails, iced tea, lemonade and everyday beverages.

This set includes:
• 7 Gas Buggy tumblers

The deep forest green glass paired with the whimsical white mid-century transportation graphics makes this a particularly fun set for a vintage bar, bar cart or retro kitchen.
