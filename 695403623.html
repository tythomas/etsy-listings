Description

Cambridge Caprice Clear Stem 300 replacement glassware, made by Cambridge Glass Company from 1936–1958. Choose from four classic stem forms: Water Goblet, Iced Tea Goblet, Champagne / Tall Sherbet, or Liquor Cocktail. Available as a single glass or set of 4.

CAMBRIDGE CAPRICE STEM 300 OPTIONS

• Water Goblet — 7 5/8" tall; holds approximately 9 oz.
• Iced Tea Goblet — 6 1/4" tall; holds approximately 12 oz.
• Champagne / Tall Sherbet — 5 3/4" tall; holds approximately 6 oz.
• Liquor Cocktail — 5 3/8" tall x 2 3/4" diameter at rim x 2 1/4" base; holds approximately 2 oz.

Select the exact style and quantity you need from the variation menu. Each style is offered individually or as a set of 4.

ABOUT THE PATTERN

Caprice is one of Cambridge Glass Company's best-known elegant glass patterns, with a flowing optic drape / swirl design that catches the light beautifully while remaining easy to mix with both formal and everyday table settings. These are Clear Caprice pieces on Stem 300 and are especially useful for collectors replacing missing pieces or building a coordinated vintage stemware service.

CONDITION

All pieces are in very good vintage condition with no chips or cracks. Glass is clear with no clouding or discoloration, and rims are smooth. As with vintage pressed and blown glassware, minor manufacturing variations may be present from piece to piece. Please review the listing photos as part of the condition description.
