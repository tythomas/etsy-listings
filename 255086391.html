Vintage American made pottery produced by Harker Pottery for their "Harkerware" line.
Harker Pottery was one of the oldest pottery manufacturing companies in the U.S. 

Pattern is called  "Ivy Wreath" 
Discontinued pattern
Circa - 1965 

6 1/4" Bread and Butter plates  feature a creamy white outer surface with a sage green interior. Last photo shows the plate with salad plates in this pattern that have the ivy pattern. 

ALL plates are in VERY GOOD vintage condition- no chips, cracks.  Rims are chip free and glaze is shiny and bright - no staining or discoloration. There are some very slight surface scratches that can be seen from gentle utensil use over the years! Glaze shows little to no crackling.
