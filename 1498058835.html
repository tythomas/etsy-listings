Maker : Indiana Glass
Pattern: Recollection amber (Madrid) 
Circa : 1976   
NOTE- This is a recollection piece from Indiana Glass manufactured a line in the 70's that was called Recollection.  This collection featured pieces that replicated the early Federal Glass depression era pieces. 

ALL pieces are in EXCELLENT vintage condition - no chips or cracks, glass is bright with no clouding. There are some straw marks that can be seen on the bottoms of some of the cups from manufacturing. Small air bubbles are noted on a few of the pieces - these bubbles are within the glass and do come through to the surface of the pieces. 

A seam can be seen on both sides 
Rims of the pieces are thick

Gorgeous bread / dessert  plates & cup / saucer…. great addition for anyone who has a collection of Madrid pieces !
