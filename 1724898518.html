Antique Ivory china bowls - 4 different sizes  ~  1930's antique ivory oval china 12" platter  - perfect to go with any dishes - Pieces are all ivory.  Edges have a subtle design and are slightly scalloped  . Pieces resemble Steubenville, but are not marked as such. 

Pieces have no back stamp except the platter which has imprinted  USA on bottom

Measurements-
~ Small dessert bowl - 5 1/4" diameter x 1. 1/4" tall 
~ Medium bowl -  6 1/4" diameter x 1 1/2" tall
~ Large Coupe / shallow bowl - 8 1/2" diameter x 1 1/2" tall 
~ XL vegetable serving bowl - 8 1/2" diameter x 2 1/2" tall * some nicking on the underside of bowls ( not all)
~ oval platter - 12" x 9" 

VERY GOOD vintage condition - no chips or cracks.  Piece surfaces are in good condition for age.  Some do show light visible scratching on the inside surface  ~ no crackling of the glaze noted.

NOTE - Single medium bowls also have some very tiny fleabites near the rim.
