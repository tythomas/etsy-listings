Description

Knapp-Monarch Therm-A-Jug — a 1 gallon insulated picnic jug in hammered silver-grey painted steel with a red spigot. The front label reads "KNAPP-MONARCH CO., ST. LOUIS, MO." with the K-M shield logo, and the base is stamped 154. The red cap unscrews and turns over into a drinking cup.

Measurements:
• Height: 13.5" ( with handle extended)

Hand wash the cap and rinse the liner. Not for the dishwasher, and do not submerge the jug.
