Description

A curated collection of 10 antique and vintage blue and white transferware pieces selected for a layered, collected plate wall. Unlike a grouping of all round plates, this collection combines varied sizes and shapes, including an 11 5/8" oval Staffordshire Asiatic Pheasant platter and an unusual 10 1/2" double-handled Blue Onion serving plate. The assortment also includes 1850s English ironstone, Spode Blue Italian, Blue Willow and Staffordshire transferware, creating a one-of-a-kind display with the character and dimension of a collection assembled over time.

This one-of-a-kind grouping combines English and Japanese blue and white china in different ages, sizes, shapes, patterns and shades of blue. The collection includes antique English ironstone dating to the 1850s, Spode Blue Italian, Blue Willow and Staffordshire transferware.

Rather than ten matching plates, the collection was assembled for the layered, collected-over-time look of an English country or grandmillennial plate wall. Scenic, floral, Blue Willow and Asiatic Pheasant patterns range from soft timeworn blue to rich cobalt. An oval platter and double-handled serving plate add shape and dimension among the traditional round plates.

**CONDITION**
This is an intentionally mixed collection of antique and vintage china from different makers and periods. Individual pieces show varying degrees of age and previous use, including crazing, surface and utensil wear, discoloration and variations in the transfer decoration. There are NO chips or cracks. 

The differences in color and age are part of the collected look of the set, ranging from the softly aged blue of the mid-19th-century Clementson ironstone to deep cobalt Blue Willow.
