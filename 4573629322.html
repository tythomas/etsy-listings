Queen's Garden is a discontinued Aynsley English bone china pattern, numbered 7614/1, with a pale blue ground, detailed garden landscape, colorful enameled floral accents, scalloped borders and gold trim.

CONDITION:
Exceptional vintage condition and remarkably well preserved. These serving pieces are from the same large service as 16 matching place settings that appear to have seen little, if any, use. No chips, cracks or other damage noted on these two serving pieces.

Made in England by Aynsley / John Aynsley & Sons. Pattern 7614/1. English bone china. Based on the backstamp and pattern evidence researched for this service, circa late 1930s–1940s, most likely 1940s.

This listing is for ONE serving piece. Select either Oval Bowl or Gravy Underplate. The gravy underplate is the plate only; no gravy boat is included.
