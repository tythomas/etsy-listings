Description

Vintage 1973 Holly Hobbie pot is a porcelain coffee pot with its fitted lid, approx. 7 1/2" tall with a cataloged 5-cup capacity.

This vintage coffee pot is Japanese porcelain marked Made in Japan. It features the Holly Hobbie girl carrying a cat among orange and yellow flowers, along with the quote “Happiness is having someone to care for.” The underside is marked HOLLY HOBBIE, © WWA INC., MCMLXXIII and MADE IN JAPAN. replacement pieces catalogs this lidded form as the 7 1/2", 5-cup coffee pot in the discontinued Holly Hobbie collection.

Glossy white porcelain is finished with a colorful printed illustration and gold trim at the lid, rim, handle, spout and pedestal foot. Its nostalgic character makes it appealing for a 1970s kitchen, while the complete lidded form works equally well as a display collectible or replacement piece for an existing Holly Hobbie collection.

The listing includes one coffee pot and one fitted vented porcelain lid, sold together as a single complete unit.
