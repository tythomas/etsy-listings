Description

1930s Rock Sharpe Frontenac cut crystal champagne or tall sherbet glasses, featuring the distinctive Stem 1015 with elaborate floral and foliate cutting. Each glass measures approximately 6 1/4 inches tall, 3 5/8 inches across the rim, with a 3 1/8 inch foot and approximately 4 inch stem, and holds about 6 ounces.

Frontenac is a named Rock Sharpe pattern introduced circa 1937. Rock Sharpe was associated with Cataract-Sharpe Manufacturing Co. of Buffalo, New York, a firm known for decorating and cutting quality American glassware. The Frontenac design combines two decorative bands near the upper bowl with detailed floral and foliate cutting, an architectural faceted Stem 1015, and additional cutting on the foot.

Eighteen matching glasses from the same estate are available at the time of listing. They are offered as matched sets of four, with one remaining matched set of two. Buyers wishing to purchase the entire 18-piece service may purchase all available sets while the full group remains together.
