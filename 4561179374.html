Description

1847 Rogers Bros Marquise silverplate flatware in its original fitted wood chest, with nine serving pieces. Each piece is struck 1847 ROGERS BROS with the boxed I S, and the original paper card in the chest names the pattern as Marquise, introduced 1933. ***78 piece set***

MAKER: 1847 Rogers Bros, International Silver Co.
PATTERN: Marquise
CIRCA: 1933
MATERIAL: Silverplate, hollow-handle dinner knives
CHEST: Original fitted "Tarnish Proof Chest" — burgundy velvet tray, slotted knife rack in the lid

Set contents:
• 12 hollow-handle dinner knives in the lid rack ( plus 2 more) 
• 14 Dinner forks 
•  5 Salad forks 
• 15 Soup spoons 
• 22 Teaspoons  pieces total
• 8 serving and specialty pieces: two butter spreaders, a two-tine pickle fork, a sugar shell, a gravy ladle, a tablespoon, a large serving spoon and a cold meat fork
• 78 pieces total 

Measurements:
• Chest: 16" x 11 1/4" x 3 1/2"
• Dinner knife: 8 1/2" 
• Dinner Forks - 7 3/4" 
• Salad forks - 6 3/4" 
• Soup Spoons - 7 1/2" 
• Teaspoons - 6 1/4" 

Care: hand wash and dry. Silverplate should not go in the dishwasher.

CONDITION
• Heavy dark tarnish on the serving pieces, pictured
• The cold meat fork has dense black pitting across the face and between the tines
• Black speckling on the large serving spoon and on a spoon bowl
• A white crusty deposit on one spoon bowl, pictured
• A warm copper tone low on that same bowl, where the silver plate has worn through to the base metal
• The knife blades show grey clouding and dark streaking where they sit in the velvet slots, and one blade has a rust-colored spot
• Grey haze settled into the pattern grooves on the handles, with green-tinged discoloration in the recesses of a couple of them
• Fine surface scratching on the flat handle shafts
• No monogram on any piece
• No bends, cracks, loose handles or repairs
• The chest shows its age: the satin lid liner is yellowed and creased, the velvet is worn flat with rubbed patches, the inner left wall is chipped, and the front edge has finish wear
• The paper pattern card is toned amber with a torn corner
