Description

Vintage round Christmas decorative tin featuring an elaborate raised evergreen wreath, gold-tone hunting horn, and ribbon on its lid. The substantial applied metal decoration gives the lid a notably heavy feel, while the matching metal tin itself is lightweight. A distinctive piece for traditional holiday decorating, woodland displays, or keepsake storage.

The dark-finished wreath has finely textured evergreen branches surrounding the lid, with a contrasting gold-tone horn and patterned bow. The applied metalwork is firmly attached. This design is attributed to Designs by Metzke based on comparable examples; no maker's mark has been confirmed. The precise alloy of the applied decoration has not been verified.
