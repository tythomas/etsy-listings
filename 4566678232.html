Description

A wonderfully aged circa 1940s Stratosphere suitcase by Rappeport of Chicago, featuring a distinctive brown and golden tan striped woven exterior, genuine leather trim, heavy metal corner protectors and the original Stratosphere globe badge.

Rappeport was a Chicago luggage maker documented in business by at least 1936, and the construction and fittings of this case are consistent with approximately the 1940s. The textured striped exterior, substantial leather edging, riveted hardware and original fitted interior give it an especially strong early travel-luggage look.

This is a compact suitcase or overnight-size travel case rather than a large full-size suitcase. Its warm neutral coloring and authentic age wear make it particularly appealing for vintage luggage collections, stacked suitcase displays, photography props, period interiors or decorative storage.

CONDITION
This suitcase shows authentic wear from decades of age and use and is being sold with that history intact.

The leather handle shows significant age-related wear, including deterioration and loss near the ends. Leather edging has scuffing and surface loss, and there is an area along the lower edge where the trim has pulled away/separated from the body.

The woven exterior has scattered marks, discoloration, rubbing and areas of fraying/separation. Metal hardware has age-related patina and oxidation.

The interior remains substantially intact but shows age wear, including wear and tearing to portions of the gathered lining/pocket material. The upper interior panel has snaps, but no corresponding removable accessory or panel is present.

Both front latches are present but do not spring open on their own. They must be manually pulled upward to release. Sliding key-lock mechanisms are present, but no key is included, so the locks have not been tested.
