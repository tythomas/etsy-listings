Description

Antique silver plate double condiment caddy — footed chased tray with two glass liners, domed lids and two spoons. The underside is stamped with a crown between two stars over the letters C&G; Makers markings for Crown Silver Company. England. Chased all over with roses and acanthus, on four cast acanthus feet.

Measurements:
• Tray length: 8.5" 
• Tray depth: 6"
• Height to top of handle: 6"
• Glass liner diameter: 3 1/4"
• Glass liner height: 2 7/8"
• Spoon length: 4 1/4" 

What is included:
• One footed tray with two mounted holders and a central carrying handle
• Two clear glass liners
• Two domed lids with urn finials and a notch for the spoon handle
• Two spoons

Gently hand washed and air dried. Hand wash only.
