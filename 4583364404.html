Description

Antique 1930s Grandmother's Flower Garden quilt, hand pieced and hand quilted in a colorful assortment of period prints and solids. Approximately 68" x 91".

This beautiful Depression-era quilt features large double-ring hexagon flowers set into a cream hexagon field, with green three-hexagon leaf accents throughout. The maker carried the green into the perimeter with a distinctive shaped/scalloped edge finished in narrow green binding.
