Maker, Pattern & Date Details

🇬🇧 Spode – “Italian Red” – Wide Rim Pasta Bowl
Origin: Made in England
Size: 11 ¾" diameter × 1 ¾" tall
Type: Wide Rim Pasta / Rimmed Soup Bowl
Production Period: c. 1980s–1990s

Notes: Adapted from Spode’s iconic 1816 blue “Italian” pattern; this red version was introduced later in the 20th century. A versatile shallow bowl ideal for pasta, salad, or display. Dishwasher and microwave safe.

🇬🇧 Adams (Jonroth Import) – “Mount Vernon”
Origin: Staffordshire, England; imported exclusively for Mount Vernon Shops, Virginia
Size: 7 ½" plate
Production Period: c. 1940s–1950s

Notes: A mid-century piece commemorating George Washington’s Mount Vernon estate. Crisp architectural engraving with floral border and Staffordshire backstamp.

🇬🇧 Royal Staffordshire – “Jenny Lind 1795”
Origin: Staffordshire, England
Pieces: (2) Bread Plates – 6" diameter • (1) Saucer – 6" diameter
Production Period: c. 1940s–1960s

Notes: Named after famed Swedish soprano Jenny Lind, this romantic pastoral pattern reflects post-war English design. Lovely for display or everyday use.

🇨🇴 Corona – Red Transferware Plate
Origin: Colombia
Size: 10 ⅞" dinner plate
Production Period: c. 1980s–1990s

Notes: Later 20th-century production inspired by English toile patterns. Excellent for mixing with authentic Staffordshire pieces.
