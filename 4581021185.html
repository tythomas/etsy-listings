Description

Antique folding quiet slate with four writing surfaces; each panel measures 12 3/4 × 8 3/4 inches.

This authentic school writing board is also known as a silent slate, double school slate, or book slate. Two framed slate panels fold together along cord-laced hinges, creating four writing surfaces. Cloth-wrapped frame edges helped soften the clatter of slates against wooden classroom desks.

The dark writing panels appear to be natural slate set within wooden frames. Woven tan textile wraps the frames, with braided hinge cords and faded red fabric tabs adding to its wonderfully timeworn schoolhouse character. The maker is unknown. Based on its materials, construction, and comparable documented examples, it is conservatively dated to the late 1800s or early 1900s, circa 1900.

MEASUREMENTS
- Each framed panel: approximately 12 3/4" × 8 3/4"
- Sold as one complete folding double-panel slate

CONDITION
This antique slate shows substantial, authentic wear from age and use. The writing surfaces have scratches, scuffs, chalk-like residue, and other old classroom marks. The woven textile binding and edging have fraying, loose threads, worn areas, and localized separation or loss, particularly around the corners and closed spine/end. The red fabric tabs are faded and worn. The cord-laced hinges remain present, keeping the two panels together.

Because of the aged textile binding and hinge components, this piece is best suited for collection and display rather than frequent classroom handling. Please review every photograph closely; the photographs are part of the condition description.
