Build your own vintage red goblet set from a curated collection of ruby red, cranberry, amberina, and deep red glassware.

 Styles may include wine glasses, water goblets, champagne glasses, sherbets, coupes, tumblers, iced tea glasses, and specialty stems.

Mix and match your favorites to create a custom red goblet set, holiday table setting, wedding glassware collection, vintage bar cart display, or collected-over-time glassware assortment.

- Listing is for ONE glass unless multiple quantities are selected
- Choose your preferred glass from the pull-down menu
- Includes a mix of ruby red, cranberry red, amberina, red flash, and deep red glass
- Styles may include wine glasses, water goblets, champagne glasses, sherbets, coupes, tumblers, iced tea glasses, and specialty stems
- Patterns and makers may include Anchor Hocking, Viking Glass, Fostoria, Imperial, Fenton, Noritake, Cambridge, LG Wright, Morgantown, and others
- Glasses range from approximately 2 3/4" to 9 1/4" tall
- Glasses hold approximately 4 to 16 ounces, depending on the style selected
- All pieces are vintage and were manufactured during different time periods
- Glassware may be pressed glass, cut glass, molded glass, flashed glass, or hand-blown depending on the individual piece

All pieces are in VERY GOOD vintage condition with no chips, cracks, or rough-edged rims unless specifically noted in the pull-down menu.
Please note that vintage glassware may show light signs of age and use, including minor surface scratches visible when held to direct light, small air bubbles from manufacturing, mold lines, slight color variations, or light wear to flashed finishes on select pieces.

Vintage red glassware adds instant color and warmth to a table, bar cart, open shelf, china cabinet, or holiday display. These pieces are especially striking mixed with clear crystal, white ironstone, silverplate, brass candlesticks, dark wood, plaid linens, Christmas china, or colorful vintage dinnerware.

A wonderful option for anyone who loves collected vintage glassware and wants to build a custom red glass set one piece at a time.

NOTE ~ We recommend hand washing your vintage items to maintain their beauty and not cause damage.
