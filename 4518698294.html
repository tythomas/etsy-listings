Description

A beautiful and increasingly difficult-to-find set of twelve matching Tiffin-Franciscan Lynwood champagne coupe glasses with elegant platinum rims, produced from 1957-1966 and preserved in exceptional vintage condition.

Whether displayed on a holiday table, used for champagne toasts, served with desserts, or added to a vintage crystal collection, this complete set of twelve Tiffin-Franciscan Lynwood coupe glasses offers the elegance and quality that made Tiffin crystal famous.

Produced between 1957 and 1966, the discontinued Lynwood pattern features a graceful wide coupe bowl, delicate platinum trim, and a beautifully molded stem with ribbed optic detailing rising from the foot. The platinum rims remain remarkably intact throughout the set, making this an especially desirable group for collectors and entertainers alike.
