Description

Vintage WEBCO dustless blackboard crayon tin in two-tone green, made in the USA by Weber Costello Company of Chicago Heights, Illinois.

This large classroom supply tin originally held one gross—144 pieces—of dustless blackboard crayon, the period term for blackboard chalk. Its streamlined WEBCO lettering and graphic green design date it approximately to the late 1940s, circa 1945–1950.

The fitted lid and empty interior make it a distinctive display piece or useful storage container for a vintage office, studio, classroom collection, or industrial-inspired space.
