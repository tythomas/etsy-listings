Description

Authentic 1930s Variable Star quilt in lavender and white. Hand quilted, all cotton—true Depression Era craftsmanship and cottage charm.

This vintage Variable Star quilt (also known as a LeMoyne Star Variation) dates to the 1930s–1940s, showcasing classic hand-pieced craftsmanship in a soft lavender and white two-tone design. Each block features an eight-pointed star center framed by connecting diamond shapes that form a beautiful secondary ring pattern.

Expertly hand-quilted through cotton batting and backed with natural muslin, this quilt is a true reflection of Depression Era artistry. The hand stitching and solid pastel fabrics are hallmarks of quilts made during this period of thrift and creativity.

Measuring approximately 74" x 72", it’s ideal for display, layering, or as a decorative coverlet for a twin or full-size bed. Its timeless color and pattern make it perfect for both collectors and farmhouse or cottage-style interiors.
