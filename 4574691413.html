Description

Dated 1961 hand-quilted scrappy Bow Tie quilt, measuring approximately 80" x 90". This wonderful mid-century quilt is filled with a large variety of colorful scrap fabrics—florals, ginghams, plaids, stripes, dots, geometrics and novelty prints—set against a cream ground with a beige border and binding.

The date “1961” is hand embroidered into the quilt, giving this otherwise anonymous handmade textile an unusually specific date. Acquired from an Illinois family estate.

The quilt is thin and lightweight, with no batting apparent. It has wonderful hand-quilted texture and displays beautifully on a bed, folded on a quilt rack, or draped over furniture.

CONDITION
Displays beautifully despite localized condition issues. Please review all photographs closely.

This quilt is being sold in an unwashed condition. It does have a slight  odor from storage. Because several of the vintage fabrics are fragile, we chose not to wash or soak the quilt and risk causing additional deterioration.

Multiple individual Bow Tie fabrics have age-related deterioration with splitting, shredding and areas of fabric loss, as photographed. The photographed examples include substantial deterioration to a red/pink gingham fabric, a lavender/blue floral fabric, and a pink/yellow/brown floral fabric.

There is also a section of the beige binding that has separated/opened along the edge. Scattered pink/red discoloration or color transfer is present in areas; the source of this coloring is unknown. Light surface soiling is present near the edges and is particularly noticeable on the backing.

The photographs show the condition issues we found; otherwise the quilt remains intact and presents very well for its age. Please consider the unwashed condition, storage odor and fragile fabrics when deciding how you would clean or use this vintage textile.
