Description

Set of four vintage Fostoria Sorrento Blue stemmed goblets with textured bowls and sculpted twist stems, approximately 5 1/2 inches tall.

Strongly attributed to Fostoria’s Sorrento Blue pattern, stem #2832, produced from 1971 to 1974. The heavy pressed glass design combines smoky blue-gray bowls, vertical texture, raised geometric lower bands, sculpted twist stems, and round feet.

The textured glass bowls and twisted stem design give this blue stemware a distinctive architectural presence. This water goblet set is useful as replacement stemware for collectors completing a service, and its substantial construction will appeal to buyers seeking fostoria stemware.
