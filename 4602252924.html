Description

Vintage 9" Jonroth The Lincoln Plate in blue and white English Staffordshire transferware, featuring Abraham Lincoln and landmarks associated with his life and birthplace in Hodgenville, Kentucky.

The center shows a Lincoln Memorial scene, surrounded by a portrait of Abraham Lincoln, Lincoln Spring, Lincoln Monument, and Lincoln's birthplace cabin. An ornate blue floral border completes the design. The reverse is marked “The Lincoln Plate,” with the Jonroth England mark and an importer inscription for Nancy Lincoln Inn, Hodgenville, Kentucky.
