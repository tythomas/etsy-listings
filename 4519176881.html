Description

Colorful vintage tea cups and saucers by Homer Laughlin Harlequin in four sought-after original colors—turquoise, yellow, green, and maroon.
Bring the cheerful mix-and-match spirit of classic Harlequin dinnerware to your table with this set of four vintage cup and saucer sets. Introduced by Homer Laughlin as a colorful alternative to traditional matching china, Harlequin became one of America's most beloved dinnerware patterns and remains highly collectible today.

This listing includes four cup and saucer sets in turquoise, yellow, green, and maroon. The vibrant colors, iconic concentric ring design, and Art Deco-inspired styling make these pieces equally at home in a collector's cabinet, a vintage kitchen, or a colorful tea service.

Unlike traditional dinnerware patterns, Harlequin was intentionally designed to be mixed and matched. This four-color grouping beautifully captures the original concept and creates the striking rainbow presentation that collectors and decorators love.
