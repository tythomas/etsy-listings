Description

Listing cost is for one XL handcrafted wooden dough bowl ~ note this piece is not food safe, it is for decor only! 

This piece comes from my own collection of many dough bowls. It is the perfect piece for any rustic / primitive kitchen. Note this is a reproduction of early 19th-century European dough bowls that were essential in everyday life rather than an actual piece from that time period. 

Measures approximately ~  27” W x 12” W x 4” H 

Bowl is handmade from real wood with natural distresses such as carving marks and natural wood features.  Inside of bowl is smooth and has a finish of some kind. It is not food safe - if you were to use it for that purpose, you would need to line the bowl with some type of barrier. 

This wooden dough bowl fits beautifully in any kitchen, bathroom, living room or bedroom. Dress it up by laying flower stems, herb stems or cotton stems in it. Add greenery and texture to your home by filling it with boxwood balls or twine balls.  Great piece for holiday decorating ~  add pinecones  or poinsettias for the winter holidays or mini pumpkins for the fall.  So many great ideas for this fantastic bowl!
