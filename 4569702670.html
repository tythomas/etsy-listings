Description

Vintage 1960s hand-carved wood Holy Family statue depicting Mary, St. Joseph and the young Child Jesus. 
Measures 11 1/4" tall with documented family provenance from  early 60's Jerusalem.

A beautifully detailed vintage hand-carved and hand-painted wood Holy Family sculpture depicting Mary, St. Joseph and Jesus as a young child.

Unlike the more commonly seen Nativity portrayal of the Holy Family with the infant Jesus, this carving shows Jesus as a young boy standing beside his seated mother. Mary holds an open book on her lap while Jesus reaches toward it, and Joseph stands protectively behind them holding his staff. The expressive faces, individually carved fingers and toes, deeply folded garments and subtle hand-applied coloring give this substantial carving wonderful dimension and character.

PROVENANCE
This piece has remained in our family since the early 1960s. My great-aunt acquired it in Jerusalem during 1964 and brought it home with her to the United States. See photo with vintage picture in back- this is my great aunt.

The handwriting visible on the underside is her writing, including references to Mamiee ( her sister), and is not a maker's mark.

The carving has been stored in a cedar chest for more than 30 years, prior to that it was displayed on my great Aunt’s curio shelf. 

Research into the carving found a much smaller example of this same sculptural composition described in the secondary market as an Alpine-style carving. The carving and hand-colored finish of this example are also consistent with mid-century European religious woodcarving traditions. No maker's mark or original label remains, however, so the maker and country of manufacture cannot be confirmed. Although it was acquired in Jerusalem, we are not representing it as having been made there.
