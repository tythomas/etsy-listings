Description

Homer Laughlin Cavalier Eggshell CV55 china — turquoise band, platinum trim, grey leaf and red berry center. Sold by the piece.

Cavalier Eggshell was Homer Laughlin's last eggshell-weight line, introduced in 1953. CV55 is the pattern's own code, and it has been out of production for decades.

Order as many as you like and combine any of the options in one order. Best condition ships first.
