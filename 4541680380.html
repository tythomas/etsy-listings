Description

Vintage Dresner Genuine Rawhide suitcase with original key, three original period travel decals, burgundy rayon interior, and leather handle. Measures 18 inches wide.

Vintage Genuine Rawhide suitcase by Dresner, dating to approximately the late 1940s to early 1950s.

This compact hard-sided suitcase retains its original light tan rawhide exterior, rich burgundy rayon-lined interior with gathered storage pockets, leather-wrapped handle, original working lock, and the original key.

One of its most desirable features is the collection of original period travel decals that remain attached to the exterior, documenting a lifetime of American travel:

• The Challenger Inn – Sun Valley, Idaho
• Soboba Mineral Hot Springs – San Jacinto, California
• St. Augustine Alligator Farm – Florida

The suitcase is also personalized with the original owner's initials, "E.S.," stenciled beneath the handle, adding to its authentic history and character.

Approximate measurements:
• 18 inches wide
• 12.5 inches tall when standing on end (handle not included)
• 4.25 inches deep


Condition: 
Structurally sound and displays beautifully. The rawhide exterior shows expected age-related patina, scuffs, light discoloration, and handling wear from decades of travel. The leather handle remains securely attached with age-appropriate wear. The burgundy rayon interior is clean overall with light wear and minor seam wear in a few areas. One latch requires a gentle lift by hand after unlocking rather than springing open automatically. It still closes securely. The original working key is included. The travel decals exhibit expected age, light edge wear, and remain firmly attached.


A wonderful addition to a vintage luggage collection or travel memorabilia display. It also makes an attractive stacking suitcase, photography prop, cabin décor piece, or accent for home, office, or retail displays.
