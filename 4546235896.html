Description

A set of six tall champagne coupes in Rock Sharpe cut crystal, each 6 inches tall and holding 6 ounces.

Rock Sharpe was Libbey's hand-cut crystal line, made in Ohio through the 1940s and 50s. These are the Acadia cutting on Libbey's 2007 stem.

The band around the top of each bowl is wheel cut, not printed on. Hold one up to the light and you can see the fine tool marks inside every petal and leaf. The design is a drooping bellflower with fern sprays on either side, repeating around the bowl. Below the cut band the bowl steps in to a stacked ring stem, and every glass sits on a plain domed foot. Rock Sharpe did not sign its stemware, so there is no mark underneath; the stem shape and the cutting are what identify these.
