This listing is for ONE PIECE OF GLASSWARE ~ you choose which patterns to add to your cart to  build your own mixed color wine glass / water glass / champagne glass collection. 

All glasses are genuine vintage manufactured during different time periods (these are not new glassware made to look vintage like other vendors). The glassware we are offering is either pressed or cut glass manufactured from 1950s on ~ 

All pieces are in VERY GOOD vintage condition with no chips, cracking or rough edged rims. Glass is free from clouding and water scaling. Please note that since this glassware is vintage,  there may be visible air bubbles from manufacturing or slight visible surface scratches from aging and gentle use. 

Glasses range from 4 1/4" in height to 8 1/4" tall and hold approximately 4 - 12 ounces

NOTE ~ We recommend hand washing your vintage items to maintain their beauty and not cause damage.
