Description

This antique Way Rite scale is a 25-pound household kitchen scale made in Chicago during the 1940s–1950s.

The cream painted-metal body, gray and cream round dial, and removable red platform give this piece of vintage kitchenware the worn character associated with primitive decor. Collectors of kitchenalia will appreciate this vintage scale as a functional household object that can also serve as vintage pantry decor. Its rusty metal surfaces and mechanical construction complement an industrial farmhouse setting.

The dial is marked “WAY RITE,” “HOUSEHOLD SCALE,” “MADE IN CHICAGO 22, U.S.A.,” “CAPACITY 25 POUNDS,” and “NOT LEGAL IN TRADE.” The scale is in working condition.
