Description

This is a unique listing for mismatched vintage glassware that was created at the request of event planners that we work with to provide unique, quality glassware for their events ~  

The process is simple: 
A.    Choose the TYPE of glass you want  (wine, water or champagne flute - see photos for examples)
B.    Choose the quantity of glasses you want in that style of glass. 
C.    Our glass specialist will curate a set of glassware based on your choices. 

PLEASE NOTE- you are not ordering a specific glass. 1350 North Vintage will be choosing the glasses to send to you based on your selection choices.  We will make every effort to pair glasses that are visually appealing together! We will also not send duplicates of glass pattern unless you are ordering a large quantity of glassware.  The glasses you are choosing will be mismatched maker's / patterns of actual vintage clear glassware. We have tried to photograph a representation of the types of glasses you will receive. 

Glasses range from 5" in height to 8 1/2" tall and hold approximately 6 - 16 ounces
*champagne flute heights range from 7" - 11" tall

All glasses are genuine vintage manufactured during different time periods (these are not new glassware made to look vintage like other vendors). The glassware we are offering is either pressed or cut glass manufactured from 1950s to some current patterns. 

All pieces are in VERY GOOD vintage condition with no chips, cracking or rough edged rims. Glass is free from clouding and water scaling. Please note that since this glassware is vintage,  there may be visible air bubbles from manufacturing or slight visible surface scratches from aging and gentle use.
