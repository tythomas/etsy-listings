Description

TWO heavy oval serving platters in blue on white, made by the Sterling China Company of East Liverpool, Ohio, and date-coded to early 1965. Each is 12 7/8 inches long ~  8 total available 

Sterling was one of the great American hotel-china makers, and these were built for daily service in a dining room -- vitrified through and through, and thick enough in the hand that they feel like something else entirely next to modern dinnerware. Sixty years on, that weight is why they have survived this well.

The border is a double row of small blue bells and diamonds under a dotted scalloped swag, printed under the glaze on a wide flange rim with a gently scalloped, embossed edge. All eight came out of the same mould and stack evenly. Sterling never printed a pattern name on these, and the border does not turn up in the usual pattern catalogs.

Eight platters are available at the time of listing. Variations can be combined to reach the quantity you need. Best condition ships first.
