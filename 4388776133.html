Description

This exquisite set of 7 Duncan & Miller “Governor Clinton Cut” glass dates to circa 1953 and showcases the timeless elegance of mid-century American crystal craftsmanship.

The bowl features a hand-etched leaf and laurel motif, perfectly complemented by the frosted Greek key design on the square stem block — a hallmark of this refined pattern. The form is both graceful and bold, making it an ideal addition to any vintage barware, crystal collection, or mid-century dining display.
