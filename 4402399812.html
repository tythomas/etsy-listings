These beautiful Currier & Ives dishes were produced by Homer Laughlin during the early years of the “An American Subject” series. The deep red transfer scenes are crisp and eye-catching, and each piece showcases one of the classic designs collectors seek out. The fronts look fantastic, while the backs show light, stable age crackling typical of midcentury Homer Laughlin pottery. These display beautifully and mix seamlessly into farmhouse, cottage, or Americana-style décor.

Scenes included:
- “Home Sweet Home” on the dinner plate  
- “Clipper Ship, Great Republic” on the salad plate  
- “Fox Hunting, Full Cry” on the soup bowl
