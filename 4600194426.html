Description

Vintage brown leather-covered suitcase with original cream-and-blue checked lining, leather buckle straps, brass-tone hardware, and an attached vintage W. B. Hayes Druggist luggage tag from Main and Miller Streets in Bloomington, Illinois. approx. 24" wide, 6 1/2" deep, 7" high when lying flat, and 14" tall when standing to the top of the handle.

A handsome old travel case with a leather-covered cardboard body, reinforced corners, adjustable exterior straps, original interior retaining straps, two side latches, and a central Corbin-marked lock. All three latches open and close properly. The handle is securely attached; the lock key is not included. The luggage tag provides an appealing Bloomington, Illinois, historical connection, but does not establish the manufacturer or precise age.

Estimated early-to-mid-20th century, possibly 1920s–1940s; exact date and suitcase maker are unverified.

CONDITION: Substantial age-related scratches, scuffing, surface cracking, dryness, and abrasion to the leather, particularly at the reinforced corners, exterior straps, and handle attachments. The checked fabric lining has scattered spotting and discoloration and a tear with fraying along an upper interior seam. Interior structure is cardboard beneath the covering. No odors noted. Please examine all photographs closely for wear and details. Given the age and fragile leather, recommended primarily for decorative display, photo styling, or a vintage luggage collection rather than regular travel or carrying heavy contents.

One suitcase is included, with its attached vintage luggage tag. No key included. Shipping charged separately.
