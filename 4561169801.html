Description

Golden Dawn all-wool camp blanket — cream ground with charcoal, gold, coral and green stripe bands at each end, 70" x 66". The woven label reads Golden Dawn / All Wool Blanket / Processed with Amunon to Resist Moth Damage, a moth-proofing claim that dates it to the 1940s or 1950s.

Measurements:
• Length: 70"
• Width: 66"

Construction:
• 100% wool, napped on both sides
• Twill weave, stripe bands woven in rather than printed
• Ends finished with narrow cream cotton binding tape
• Long sides are the woven selvedge, unbound
• Original Golden Dawn woven label still attached

Gently cleaned and aired. Hand wash cold or dry clean.

CONDITION
• Sold as a well-used vintage blanket — please read the flaws below and look at every photo
• Two small holes in the cream ground, about 1/4" each, one with a tan margin around it, pictured
• One open worn spot about 1/2" in a charcoal stripe, with a couple of smaller broken spots nearby, pictured
• One corner has the nap worn down to bare twill with loose threads, pictured
• The cotton binding is frayed along its length and one section is torn open with the stitching pulled, pictured
• Grey smudging and scattered dark specks on the cream ground, and the cream has yellowed a little overall — permanent, not surface dirt
• No fading — the charcoal, gold, coral and green are all still strong 
• No repairs or patches -  the weave itself is tight and sound - some thinning areas noted
