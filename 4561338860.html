Description

Antique English side plates in the Brier pattern by E. Farmiloe — teal transfer-printed wild roses on ivory semi-porcelain, 6 3/4" across.

Each back carries a crown-and-wreath mark reading Royal Semi-Porcelain, E. Farmiloe, England, with the pattern name Brier above it.

MAKER: E. Farmiloe, Soho Pottery Works, Tunstall, Staffordshire
PATTERN: Brier
BODY: Royal Semi-Porcelain (earthenware)
CIRCA: 1900–1906

Measurements:
• Diameter: 6 3/4"

Hand wash only.
