Description

Choose your own antique figural Christmas light bulb from a family collection: Santa Claus, a musical monkey holding a stringed instrument, or a snow-covered cottage. Each is an individually priced, molded and painted glass collectible with an aged metal screw base. Select one figure from the variation menu; the price is for ONE bulb, not all three.

These early-style Christmas bulbs belonged to my grandmother and have been kept in our family collection. Their exact manufacturing date and country are unconfirmed; a circa 1920s–1940s date is an estimate, not a verified attribution.
