Description

Antique brown stoneware crock — Albany slip glaze, wheel thrown, no lid.

The base is unglazed and unmarked, with kiln grit still stuck around the foot. You can see the throwing rings inside and the dip line low on the body where the glaze stopped.

Measurements:
• Height: 10 1/4"
• Widest diameter: 5 5/8"
• Opening across: 4"

CONDITION
• No chips or nicks on the rim
• No cracks or hairlines
• Interior glaze is sound and clean
• Small dark oval firing mark low on the body, pictured
• A few small pits and popped spots  with open holes in the glaze, some with yellow or rust colored specks, pictured
• One small glaze nick mid body, pictured
• Light surface scratching in the glaze here and there
• Gritty tan and dark deposits on the top of the rim and the inner ledge, left from the kiln and from use

• The dark gritty ring around the bottom edge is kiln residue, not chipping
