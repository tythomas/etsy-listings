Description

Signed Stonehenge Pottery Co. stoneware crock from Farmington, Missouri, featuring a rich cobalt-blue band, a rolled rim, and the pottery's blue circular maker's stamp. The underside is incised “Aubuchon,” connecting this piece to the pottery founded by Ronald Keith Aubuchon. An appealing piece of American studio stoneware for a country kitchen, primitive display, or regional pottery collection.

This handsome cylindrical crock has a warm neutral glaze with natural variation and an unglazed clay base. The cobalt-blue band circles the upper body, while the stamped front makes an especially attractive display side.
