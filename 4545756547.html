Description

A set of four wide gold band cocktail coupes matching Golden by Avitra, circa 1968, with mirror-bright bands, faceted baluster stems and clear blown bowls ~ 6" tall.

These belong to the golden age of the home bar, when a wide metallic band around the rim was the entire design and the glass beneath was left plain to show it off. Avitra's Golden pattern ran from about 1968 and was made in the wide band alone; a later version of the same pattern added a thin second gold line below the band, and these have none, which is how the earlier band-only version is told apart. The gold was applied by hand: the band's lower edge shifts slightly from glass to glass, and a faint brushed grain runs through it where the banding wheel laid it down.

Each bowl is a shallow conical coupe, blown thin and even, with the gold carried over the lip and a short way down the inside so the band reads as a solid ring from every angle. The stems are faceted balusters with a swelled knop, cut flat so they catch light against the plain glass, and the feet are thin and slightly domed, finished with a polished pontil rather than a rough scar. No makers markings noted.
