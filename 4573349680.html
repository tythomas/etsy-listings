Description

Matching set of two antique Fostoria Sorrento tall tumblers in the Sorrento Blue pattern features heavy pressed glass with bark-textured sides and geometric footed bases.

Fostoria produced its Sorrento Blue pattern, stem number 2832, from 1971 through 1974. These substantial drinking glasses have flared rims, irregular vertical bark-like ribs, pebble-textured geometric bands, and broad circular feet. The molded design and smoky blue-gray color give the pair a wonderfully sculptural presence while remaining useful as antique replacement drinkware.

MEASUREMENTS: 
• 5 5/8" tall 
• 3 1/8" Diameter at rim
• 2 3/4" Base 
• Holds 12 ounces 

These footed tumblers pair textured glass with the substantial weight of heavy pressed glass. They are distinctive 1970s glasses for bar cart decor or for anyone seeking a replacement glass.
