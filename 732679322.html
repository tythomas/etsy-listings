According to another seller, the following is history regarding this pattern ~ 
"The Sweet Pear pattern was originally released by Indiana Glass as the Avocado Pattern #601 in 1923. It was reissued by Indiana Glass in 1973 and the name was changed to Sweet Pear and it was sold through Tiara. It is considered one of the very first Depression glass patterns and was produced from 1973 to 1988. This particular color, Lemon, was first produced in 1974."
