Vintage FEDERAL GLASS Smoke Grey 12 Oz Pilsner Beer Glass FOOTED ROLY POLY Barware TUMBLERS 
CIRCA: 1971 
Discontinued 

Part of the Home Entertainment Set Sundown (Smoke) 

These cool retro tumblers feature a round roly poly style bowl and a weighted mushroom base. 
The unique color of these glasses is very subtle and shifts beautifully in different qualities of light. At first appearance, you notice a smokey gray tinting , however, depending on the lighting a blue hue on the bottoms can be detected! 

Though unmarked, these ware believed to have been part of the hard to find discontinued line by Federal known as "THE ENTERTAINMENT SET".   Circa - 1960s 1970s  OR clarion by Lenox - circa 1975 

Measures approximately : 
5 1/5" tall
2 1/4" base
3 1/4" diameter opening at mouth

Holds approximately 14 ounces 

VERY GOOD vintage condition - rims smooth to the touch, great shine - light use shown. Some light surface scratches are visible when glasses are held directly to the light. Glass is bright and free from clouding 

Great additions to any man cave or retro bar cart!
