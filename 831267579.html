Description

This hand tinted actual photo dates from the 1930s in original frame with plastic covering protecting the photo - frame has a kick stand to allow the photo to stand upright.

The photo is of a young boy ( all smiles) with stringer of fish standing in the woods ~ name is on the back of photo ( Bobby Eaton- 7 years old 1931) . This is an actual photo and not a post card or reproduction. It comes from my personal collection of family photos!!  

Adding color to black and white photographs was popular from the beginning of photography until the 1930s. Once color film became the norm, hand tinting started to wane.
