Description

An extraordinary oversized antique Grandmother’s Flower Garden quilt, measuring approximately 94 x 104", with an intricate arrangement of colorful hand-pieced hexagon flowers and cheerful yellow centers. This beautifully detailed quilt is estimated to date to the 1930s–1940s and has a lovely scalloped edge finished in yellow.

The design includes 182 complete flowers and 14 half flowers, with seven half flowers at each end. Each complete flower is constructed from 19 hexagons: one yellow center, six matching inner petals, and twelve contrasting outer petals. That is 3,458 hexagons in the complete flowers alone, not counting the half flowers or white connecting hexagons. The outer rings showcase an especially wide variety of period-style cotton prints, with no obvious repetition of complete fabric combinations. Individual flower blocks measure approx. 7 1/8" across.
