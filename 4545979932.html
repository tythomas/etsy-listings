Description

A 13 inch oval serving platter in white Polish porcelain, made by Karolina, printed with a lavender and crimson rose bouquet and finished with a thin gold line along a scalloped rim.

Karolina worked in Jaworzyna Slaska, in Lower Silesia, and this platter carries their export mark - a small crown beside a script cartouche with MADE IN POLAND beneath, printed in green on the back.

The bouquet in the well pairs a soft lavender cabbage rose with a deep crimson one, ringed by rosebuds, small purple bellflowers and grey-green leaves. Four smaller sprays from the same design sit on the rim, so the pattern shows from every side, and a fine gold line follows each curve of the wide scalloped edge. No pattern name appears in the mark - only Karolina, MADE IN POLAND, and a printed 1.

MAKER: Karolina (Zaklady Porcelany Karolina), Jaworzyna Slaska, Poland
PATTERN: unnamed rose transfer - the backstamp names no pattern
CIRCA: mid-20th century
