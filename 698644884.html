Description

A striking vintage silver roly poly barware set from the mid century era, complete with eight rounded cocktail glasses and their metal carrying caddy.

The highly reflective silver finish and classic rounded roly poly shape give this set the glamorous cocktail-lounge look associated with 1950s and 1960s entertaining. A fantastic addition to an MCM bar cart, home cocktail bar, or vintage glassware collection.

The exact maker has not been confirmed. The style is reminiscent of Dorothy Thorpe-era silver barware, but these are not being represented as documented Dorothy Thorpe pieces.
