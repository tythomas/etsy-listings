Description

Paragon Queen Mary 8902 Tulip dinner plate with a cobalt blue ground, ornate gold decoration and colorful floral center. Measures 10 1/4" in diameter. ONLY ONE AVAILABLE 

This striking English fine bone china plate is commonly cataloged as Paragon's Queen Mary pattern, number 8902, in the Tulip floral variation. A large purple tulip and mixed garden flowers fill the center, surrounded by a cream-and-gold border, deep cobalt blue band, floral reserves and elaborate gold scrollwork. The gently scalloped rim is finished in gold.

The backstamp reads By Appointment to Her Majesty the Queen, China Potters, Paragon, Fine Bone China, England Regd. This single royal-warrant mark dates the plate to approximately 1952–1960. The design reproduces a service associated with Queen Mary, but this plate is a later 1950s production.
