Description

One turquoise-blue pressed glass Peek-A-Boo cherub barrel toothpick holder on a pedestal base, approximately 3 5/8" tall.

This collectible holder features two rounded cherub figures supporting an eight-panel barrel cup above a circular pedestal foot. The form is strongly attributable to the McKee Peek-A-Boo or Cherub pattern introduced in 1904, while the cupped, stippled underside indicates this colored example is likely a later production rather than an original 1904 issue. 

The translucent turquoise blue pressed glass has a beaded rim, molded barrel bands, bubbles or seeds, and textured mold details. This compact blue glass vessel displays beautifully with aqua glass and mid century style décor and works well for toothpicks, wrapped cocktail picks, or a collection of vintage novelty glass.

The price is for the single holder shown. Surrounding glassware, tray, paper, and tape are props and are not included.
