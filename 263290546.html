Listing cost is for 2  snack sets ( 2 trays and 2 cups) ~ SIMPLY GORGEOUS ~  Mid Century Sip and Snack Luncheon Trays 

VERY GOOD vintage condition **!  Glass is BRIGHT and clear - no staining or clouding!  Some cups and trays show a bit of yellowing of the glass causing a vintage hue which is common in glass pieces of this era with some age on them! There are some sets that have markings on the bottom of the cups and trays to identify who they belonged to ( initial - R and some B ).  Often women marked these so they could identify their plates and cups when sharing them at showers and card parties!  The plates also have some markings in the glass on the underside that appears to be from manufacturing. The surface is free from utensil scratches .  

Hazel Atlas  produced this very popular snack Party Set during the 1950's~  it is called the "Orchard Crystal" line 
Snack plates have three areas to hold the matching  cup, a small snack AND a little ashtray and holder for a cigarette ( see last photo). Cant you just imagine the housewives of the 50s sitting around gossiping with these adorable trays on their laps while smoking?!?

Plates are made of HEAVY GLASS and can be used for SOOO many events~ showers, brunches, card parties,  luncheons or bakery plates!
