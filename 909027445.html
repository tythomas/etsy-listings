These pieces have beautiful turquoise blue coloring with a beaded design raised on surface ~ the beauty is hard to capture in photos and describe !

Manufacturer: Kemple 
Pattern: Lace and Dewdrop Blue 

Unsure of exact manufacturing date ~ I believe the compote is an original piece and not a 1970s reproduction since there are no markings on this piece. The saucers however, have the KW marking and are likely from the 70's  
(The 1970s reproduction pieces have a KW imprinted on the bottom )

Kemple glass was in business from 1945 to 1970 . Wheaton bought this mould afterwards. This original pattern was made in the late 1800's and was used by Phoenix Glass

VERY GOOD vintage condition - no chips, cracks or rough spots. Pieces are heavy! Pitcher does have some air bubbles and a manufacture line in the handle.
