Description

Hand-crocheted 1970s ripple afghan in mustard gold and warm cream — 58" x 69", worsted acrylic.

The zigzag is worked in back loops, which gives each row its raised ridge. There is no fringe and no added border; the side edges are finished in mustard, and the top and bottom end in the zigzag itself.

Measurements: approximate
• Width: 58"
• Length: 69"

Construction:
• Hand crocheted, single/half-double crochet worked in the back loops
• Chevron ripple stitch, roughly 10-11 peaks across the width
• Two colors only, alternating in narrow bands of about 4-5 rows each
• Worsted-weight acrylic yarn, soft and matte
• Plain worked selvedge down both sides; no separate edging

Gently hand washed and air dried.

CONDITION
• No holes — nothing shows through when it is held up to the light
• No stains or discoloration
• No repairs, and no mismatched yarn anywhere in the field
• Side edges and corners intact, with no unraveling or thinning
• Hangs flat and even, no stretched or wavy edges
• Light surface fuzz/piling on both colors, the normal soft halo an acrylic afghan gets with age
• A few dark fiber flecks are caught in the mustard rows — these are part of the yarn, not soiling
