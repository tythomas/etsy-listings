Description

Vintage Dresden Plate appliqué quilt, approximately 94 × 76 inches, featuring 20 carefully coordinated floral blocks, hand quilting, pink borders, and scalloped edges. Estimated circa 1930s–1940s.

This family-owned vintage quilt has 20 Dresden Plate blocks arranged in five rows of four. The maker deliberately repeated and balanced colorful floral, polka-dot, and geometric cotton prints within the blocks rather than placing fabrics randomly. Every plate has a dusty-rose pink circular center surrounded by a separate cream fabric ring. Prominent, heavier black hand-appliqué stitching outlines the petals and centers, adding bold definition. Elaborate hand quilting in the cream ground includes curved, feather-like decorative motifs. Pink border strips and scalloped edges finished with pink binding complete the design.
