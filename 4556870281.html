Description

Los Angeles Potteries salad bowl — chartreuse green, hand painted grapes and leaves, 11" across and 5" deep.

The base is impressed LOS ANGELES POTTERIES / CALIF / U.S.A. with a mold number. The grapes and tendrils are brushed on by hand, so the pattern is a little different on every bowl.

Measurements:
• Across the rim: 11"
• Across the narrower axis: 10 3/4"
• Height: 5"

MAKER: Los Angeles Potteries, California
CIRCA: 1960s
MATERIAL: Glazed ceramic
DECORATION: Hand painted grape and leaf motif on the outside; plain green inside

Hand wash only — a crazed glaze should not go in the dishwasher.

CONDITION
• Crazing over the whole bowl, inside and out — shown up close in the photos - crackling does not go through to the raw pottery beneath when immersed in water
