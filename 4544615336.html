Set of six 1960s Style House Rhythm dinner plates made in Japan. Mid-Century Modern black scroll design with platinum rim. Each measures 10 3/4 inches.

Bring authentic Mid-Century Modern style to your table with this beautiful set of six Style House Fine China "Rhythm" dinner plates.

Often sought by collectors of Mid-Century Modern and Atomic-era Japanese dinnerware, Style House's Rhythm pattern features a clean abstract design that pairs beautifully with contemporary and vintage table settings.  Produced in Japan during the 1960s, the Rhythm pattern features elegant black freeform scrolls floating across a crisp white background, finished with a sophisticated platinum rim. The clean, atomic-inspired design complements both modern and vintage interiors while remaining timeless enough for everyday dining or entertaining.
