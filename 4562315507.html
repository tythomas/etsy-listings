Description

Westmoreland Beaded Edge milk glass plate — hand painted songbird on a berry branch, 7 1/2" across.

The beading around the rim is Westmoreland's Beaded Edge line, and the bird is painted freehand — you can see the brush strokes in the wing and the tail.

MAKER: Westmoreland Glass Company, Grapeville, Pennsylvania
PATTERN: Beaded Edge
CIRCA: 1950s-1960s

Measurements:
• Diameter: 7 1/2"

Hand wash only.

CONDITION
