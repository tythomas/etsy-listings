Description

A striking antique crazy quilt measuring 84 x 71", filled with richly colored irregular patchwork and decorative embroidery stitches. Burgundy, navy, purple, brown, black, plaid, and lighter fabrics create a beautifully varied surface with excellent display appeal. This substantial, very heavy quilt displays beautifully across a bed and would also make an appealing addition to an antique textile collection or Victorian-inspired room.

The pieced top combines a variety of fabrics and textures, with contrasting decorative stitching along the patchwork seams. The reverse has a brown-and-dark striped backing secured with burgundy yarn ties. The quilt appears to contain batting in the quilted body, but not in the edging. The wide brown edging is a distinctive feature of the construction.
