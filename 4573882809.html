Description

Antique three-piece West German vanity perfume vanity set in green satin glass, with two stoppered perfume bottles and one lidded powder jar.

This coordinated dresser set includes two matching footed scent bottles and a round covered vanity jar. An original paper label reads “MADE IN WESTERN-GERMANY.” The mark establishes manufacture between 1949 and 1990; the forms and decoration most likely date circa 1960s. 

The olive green glass has a soft satin or frosted surface, translucent green feet and finials, gold-painted bands, and an abstract scroll design with dot, crosshatch, and outlined geometric decoration. This circa 1960s glass set has an understated olive to gray-green color and warm gold details, making it inviting for a collected vintage vanity display, while the covered jar can hold jewelry or other small keepsakes.

Sold as one complete three-piece set: two perfume bottles with fitted ground-glass stoppers and one powder or trinket jar with its fitted lid. Pieces are not offered separately.
