Description

Six Morgantown Crinkle sherbets in Shamrock Green — the heavy bark-textured glass Morgantown made in its final decades. Each stands 2¾ inches tall, measures 3½ inches across the rim, and holds 6 ounces.

Morgantown Glass Works worked in Morgantown, West Virginia until it closed in 1971, so every piece of Crinkle in circulation was made before then. The line was formed against a deliberately irregular mould, and the texture carries through to the inside of the bowl rather than sitting only on the outside — run a finger around the inner wall and you feel the same crumpled surface you see.
