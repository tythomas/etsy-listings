Description

Vintage red Dietz Junior kerosene lantern with clear embossed Dietz Junior glass globe, original paper hangtag, and surviving Dietz and distributor decals. Measures 16 1/4" tall with the carrying handle extended and 5 1/4" in diameter at the base.

This cold-blast metal lantern has a Hong Kong marking on the metal burner assembly beneath the globe. The World Famous Sales Company of Chicago decal, Dietz of America flag decal, yellow Dietz hangtag, and CAUTION USE KEROSENE ONLY label remain attached. Tentatively dated to the 1960s–1970s; an exact manufacturing year has not been verified.

CONDITION: The wick is unburned and raises and lowers when the adjustment knob is turned. This lantern appears unused. It has not been lit or tested for fuel-tightness or safe operation. Scattered minor paint wear, small chips/scratches, age-related wear to the paper labels, and dust inside the glass globe are present. The globe appears intact, but is dusty.  Please examine all photographs for condition and original markings.

Sold as one vintage lantern, as pictured. For decorative/collectible display; no guarantee of safe working operation. If intending to operate it, have it inspected and follow appropriate kerosene lantern safety practices.
