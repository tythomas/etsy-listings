Description

A one-of-a-kind vintage Samsonite suitcase decorated with a collage of genuine antique and vintage paper ephemera, including handwritten correspondence, historical advertising, an old bank check, postal materials, and illustrated prints. A distinctive piece for eclectic interiors, a study, library, vintage display, or decorative storage.

The brown suitcase bears a Shwayder Bros. Inc. Samsonite maker's plate marked Denver and Detroit. Its exterior has been decorated at a later date with old papers, including an envelope postmarked Chicago in 1896, an E. Litchfield & Co. bank check dated 1904, Post Tavern correspondence dated 1922, agricultural advertising, and colorful illustrated labels. These document dates refer to the papers, not the date of suitcase manufacture. The exact date the collage was assembled is unknown.

CONDITION
The interior is exceptionally clean for its age, odor-free, with intact pockets and functional elastic. The exterior shows vintage scuffs, chipped finish, age wear, and areas of discoloration and wear to the applied paper collage. The original Samsonite handle has extensive cracking, peeling, and loss of its outer covering, particularly at the ends. The handle remains attached, but this suitcase is recommended for decorative display or light storage rather than carrying heavy contents or regular travel.  Cute little size!
