### DESIGN

The dinnerware has a warm cream-to-beige background with a raised scalloped border and hand-painted orange and white floral decoration.

The combination of earthy beige, orange and white gives the pattern a classic 1970s look while also making it especially appealing for autumn and Thanksgiving table settings.

Some of the dinner plates have a handwritten signature on the back that appears to read **“Marielli.”** The exact maker, pattern name and country of manufacture have not been confirmed.
