WOODLAND GOLD BY METLOX
- Maker: Metlox
- Line: Poppytrail / Vernon
- Pattern: Woodland Gold
- Made in California
- Circa: 1959–1979
- Discontinued pattern
- Heavy ceramic / stoneware construction
- Speckled cream background
- Brown, orange, gold and green autumn leaf design

Woodland Gold is a classic mid-century California pottery pattern with an especially warm, earthy palette that works beautifully with vintage, rustic and autumn table settings.
