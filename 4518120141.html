Description

Striking blue appliqué and extraordinary hand quilting make this vintage quilt a true statement piece, beautifully preserved with remarkable craftsmanship throughout.

This vintage handmade quilt features a bold blue appliqué design set against a soft antique white background, creating a striking graphic pattern that displays beautifully on a bed, sofa, or quilt rack.

The quilt showcases extensive hand quilting throughout, including feather, floral, vine, and geometric quilting motifs that cover nearly the entire surface. The workmanship is especially impressive in the wide border areas where elaborate quilting patterns create texture and depth beyond the appliqué design itself.

The blue appliqué fabric remains vibrant and consistent in color, providing beautiful contrast against the aged white background fabric. The quilt has a lightweight feel with no batting, allowing the quilting details to remain clearly visible across the entire surface.

Condition is exceptional for a vintage quilt. No tears, holes, repairs, fabric breakdown, or significant staining were found during inspection. The background fabric retains a soft antique white coloration rather than bright modern white, with light age-related toning consistent with a well-preserved vintage textile. A few very minor areas of discoloration are present, including one visible spot, but they are difficult to notice during display and do not detract from the overall presentation.

This quilt displays beautifully on a queen-size bed and would make a stunning addition to farmhouse, cottage, coastal, traditional, or antique-inspired interiors.
