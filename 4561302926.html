Description

Matched set of five antique McKee & Brothers clear pressed glass plates in the IHC / Teutonic pattern family, dating to the late 19th century. These substantial 9 1/8-inch plates feature an elaborate imitation-cut design of diamonds, fans, stars/rosettes and hobstar-style motifs.

McKee introduced the earlier IHC (“Imitation Heavy Cut”) design in the 1880s; portions of the line were later reissued/redesigned as Teutonic in 1894. Because clear IHC and Teutonic forms can be difficult to distinguish, these are conservatively identified as McKee IHC / Teutonic, circa 1880s–1890s.

SET
• 5 matching plates
• Approximately 9 1/8 inches in diameter
• Heavy clear pressed glass
• Sold together as one set

CONDITION
Four plates are in the better overall condition. One plate has considerably more pronounced factory edge/mold irregularity along portions of the scalloped rim. The plates show characteristics of early pressed-glass manufacture, including trapped air bubbles/seeds, mold seams and rough or irregular areas along portions of the scalloped edges where the glass did not finish cleanly in the mold. These manufacturing characteristics are visible in the photographs and should not be confused with ordinary later edge chipping. Please review all photographs closely for the individual plates and edge details.

A scarce opportunity to acquire five matching examples of this elaborate late-Victorian McKee pattern together—well suited to an EAPG collection, antique table setting or display.
