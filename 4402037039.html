Description

Beautiful and rare opalescent Paneled Grape goblets by Indiana Glass. A glowing pearl-white finish, offered in sets of 2.

These rare vintage goblets feature Indiana Glass’s opalescent white Paneled Grape pattern, a scarce and highly collectible variation of the classic grape motif. Unlike standard opaque milk glass, this version is crafted in a glowing opal-white glass with soft translucence and blue-gold fire when held to the light. These pieces display exceptional clarity and pattern detail, making them a standout for cottage-style décor, vintage barware, or special-occasion table settings.

This opalescent variation was produced in far smaller quantities than traditional milk glass Paneled Grape, making matching sets especially hard to find. Yours are a beautiful, well-matched set offered as a set of 2 or a full set of 4, each sold as its own variation.
