Description

Taylor Smith & Taylor Pebbleford speckled ceramic dinnerware ~ 1950s kitchen ~ Granite, Sand, Teal and Sunburst Yellow ~ Dinner plates ~ dessert plates ~ bowls 

These vintage Pebbleford replacement pieces have simple coupe shapes and brown-speckled glazes in Granite, Teal, Sand, and Sunburst Yellow.  Specialist references date these solid-color Pebbleford lines primarily to the 1950s, with production continuing into the early to mid-1960s for portions of the line.

The assortment includes dinner plates, bread-and-butter plates, and shallow coupe soup bowls. Each non-overlapping variation is priced per individual piece, and buyers may purchase multiple pieces while quantities last.
