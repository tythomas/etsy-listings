Description

Antique Noritake N1285 porcelain replacement dinnerware and serving pieces from an early-20th-century Japanese china collection. The pattern is cataloged by Replacements Ltd. as circa 1918 and features delicate pink roses, blue and yellow flowers, ornate green scrollwork on a yellow and cream border, and elegant gold trim. The green Morimura M-in-wreath Noritake Hand Painted Japan backstamp is consistent with early Noritake production.

Choose the individual piece you wish to purchase from the variation menu. Each selection is priced separately; this is not a listing for the entire collection.
