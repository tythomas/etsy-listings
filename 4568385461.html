Description

Vintage Kemple Yutek Dark Blue turquoise glass creamer and open sugar bowl with a beautiful raised pressed star and fan design.

The rich turquoise blue coloring is especially striking, and the beauty of the glass is difficult to fully capture in photographs.

Included in this listing are two pieces:
• Creamer — 4 7/8" tall to the highest point; holds approximately 12 ounces
• Open sugar bowl — 3 5/8" tall with a 4 1/4" diameter at the top

Manufacturer: Kemple
Pattern: Yutek Dark Blue
Material: Pressed glass


CONDITION
The open sugar bowl has a large chip on the outside of the top flared rim. Because of the cut pattern, the chip is very hard to see, but it is present and should be considered when purchasing. Please review the photographs carefully.

Otherwise, both pieces are in very good vintage condition.

A beautiful pair for a Kemple collection, colorful vintage table setting, or turquoise glass display.
