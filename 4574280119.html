Description

One vintage silver-tone metal jumping fish mold is a fish shaped mold for gelatin and aspic, featuring high-relief scales and an integrated hanging tab.

This deep food mold depicts a curved fish with a round eye, overlapping scales, radiating fins, fan-shaped tail, open center, and pierced hanging tab. It closely resembles documented 1960s jumping-fish molds, including a 1963 A.H.C. form, but no reverse mark is shown.  The silver-gray cast metal appears likely to be aluminum, with warm oxidation and age patina.

As an aspic mold or jello mold, this metal bakeware piece also suits nautical decor, a kitchen wall hanging, or retro kitchen decor.
