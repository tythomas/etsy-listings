Description

A round vegetable bowl and an oval one, both in Homer Laughlin's Priscilla pattern and both backstamped 1947. Sold together as a pair.

Priscilla was decorated for Household Institute, a mail-order line Homer Laughlin supplied through the 1940s, and both bowls carry that mark beside the Homer Laughlin one. The date codes read E 47 N 8 on the round bowl and F 47 N 8 on the oval — consecutive months of 1947.

The ivory body is warmer than white, and the scalloped piecrust rim has a thin gold line run along its edge. A full bouquet sits in the well of each bowl, built on a pink cabbage rose with a red and yellow parrot tulip beside it, and eight or so smaller sprigs are scattered around the rim shelf — yellow-edged roses with blue forget-me-nots, mauve tulips, pink asters. The colours are still strong and no decal has faded or lifted.

MAKER: Homer Laughlin China Company, USA
PATTERN: Priscilla, decorated for Household Institute
CIRCA: 1947 (date codes E 47 N 8 and F 47 N 8)
