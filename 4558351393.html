Description

Brass miner's safety lamp from Wales — Cambrian pattern, Clanny-Davy type, oil burning, serial No. 02398. The bonnet plaque is embossed with a Welsh dragon and reads "Miners Safety Lamp - Cambrian", and the base band is stamped "Cymru", Welsh for Wales.

Measurements:
• Height: 9 1/2"
• Base diameter: 3 1/2"

CONDITION
• Tarnish overall, with small green verdigris patches on the bonnet rim and around the ring of air holes
• Fine surface scratches on the brass chimney, most visible near the plaque
• A dull reddish patch of discoloration low on the chimney
• Oxidation and gray solder bloom on the underside of the base, pictured
• Punch-dot engraving on the underside of the base reads "Made in Taiwan"
• The wick is charred and there is soot at the burner collar - this lamp has been burned
• The glass cylinder is intact, with no cracks or chips
• The base unscrews cleanly and the lamp comes apart into its sections
• Bonnet, glass, burner, wick, wick pick, oil font and swing handle are all present
