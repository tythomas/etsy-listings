Listing is for a creamer & Sugar  ~ this turquoise blue glass is just gorgeous

This piece has beautiful turquoise blue coloring with a beaded design raised on piece surface ~ the beauty is hard to capture in photos and describe !

Manufacturer: Kemple Wheaton
Pattern: Lace and Dewdrop Blue 

Sugar bowl ~ 2 1/2" tall 
Creamer ~ 3 1/4" to the top of the spout 

Unsure of exact manufacturing date ~ I believe this is a 1970s reproduction since there are KW markings on this piece. ( The 1970s reproduction pieces have a KW imprinted on the bottom )

Kemple glass was in business from 1945 to 1970 . Wheaton bought this mould afterwards. This original pattern was made in the late 1800's and was used by Phoenix Glass

EXCELLENT vintage condition - no chips , cracks or rough spots. Pieces are heavy !
