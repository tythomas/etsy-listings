Description

Oversized vintage king-size patchwork quilt by Illinois quiltmaker Robb Gragg, measuring approximately 114 x 100 inches. Colorful scrap-fabric squares are arranged in blocks framed by patterned blue sashing, with a cream backing and thin batting for a relatively lightweight feel.

This quilt comes from an Illinois consignor who personally knows the maker, Robb Gragg. The colorful squares include older-looking fabrics, while the blue sashing appears newer than the block fabrics; specific fabric ages have not been verified.

The quilt is machine sewn. Contrasting red thread is particularly noticeable against the blue sashing on the front and the cream background on the back. Some areas use white thread ~ Stitching is uneven and irregular in places, and some loose thread ends and uneven edge finishing are visible in close-up photographs. Please examine all photos for these handmade construction characteristics.

SIZE: Approximately 114 x 100 inches (oversized king). Please measure your bed and desired drop before purchasing.

CONDITION: Sold unwashed. No stains, odors, tears, or open seams reported. The irregular stitching and finishing described above are visible; photos are part of the condition description.

A colorful, generously sized quilt for farmhouse, cottage, eclectic, or vintage-inspired bedding and display. You will receive the exact quilt photographed.
