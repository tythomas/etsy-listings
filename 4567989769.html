Description

A beautiful circa 1940s hand quilted scrap quilt filled with an incredible assortment of colorful period cotton prints. Large green and white pinwheel/star centers are surrounded by scrappy pieced star motifs, then framed with wide pink sashing for a wonderfully graphic pink, green and white color palette.

The quilt appears to date to approximately the late 1930s–1940s based on its construction and extensive assortment of period fabrics. The scraps include tiny florals, larger florals, plaids, checks, stripes, polka dots, geometric designs and novelty-style prints. Some fabrics may have originated from feed or flour sacks, while others appear consistent with period dressmaking and household sewing scraps; because the individual fabrics cannot be positively documented as feedsack, I have not identified this as a feedsack quilt.

The repeating block design is an unusual scrappy star and pinwheel variation. Each large block features a green and white rotating pinwheel/star center surrounded by smaller colorful pieced star forms and repeating green squares. Wide pink sashing with white cornerstones separates the blocks.

The quilt is hand quilted throughout. Decorative curved/scalloped quilting can be seen across the pink sashing, with additional quilting following and complementing the pieced design. It has thin cotton batting and a continuous white cotton backing, giving it a lightweight feel and lovely soft drape. The outer edge is finished with a substantial wide white cotton binding/edge with visible hand stitching.

MEASUREMENTS
Approximately 75" wide × 99" long.

The quilt is shown on a queen-size bed for scale. At 75" wide, it covers the top of a queen mattress with a shorter side drop. It would also work beautifully on a full/double bed or oversized twin depending on the amount of drape desired. Please compare the measurements with your own bed and preferred coverage.

CONDITION
Very good vintage condition for its age and very structurally sound overall.

Age-related details noted include:
- One small tear/area of fabric loss in the pink sashing
- Deterioration/thinning to one of the printed fabrics within a star
- Scattered areas of missing or broken hand-quilting stitches
- Localized yellow/tan soiling on the white backing
- One small tan/yellow spot on the white fabric on the front
- The pink fabric varies in shade throughout, with some sections lighter or deeper than others. This may be from different fabric pieces and/or age-related fading.

No other significant issues were observed. Please review the close-up photographs carefully, as they are an important part of the condition description.

The quilt has been gently hand washed and air dried.

Its lightweight construction makes it equally beautiful displayed folded at the foot of a bed, layered over bedding, or used as a vintage bed quilt.
