Description

Beautiful **28-piece vintage Bareuther Bavaria Harvest china set**, made in Germany and dating approximately to the **1940s–1950s**.

This coordinated porcelain set features six different colorful **fruit and nut motifs** on classic white porcelain with elegant gold trim. Apples, grapes, pears, peaches, cherries, pineapple, strawberries and nuts give the set a wonderful harvest-inspired look that is especially striking for fall entertaining, Thanksgiving tables, or a vintage china collection.

The coupe-style plates have a clean, simple shape that allows the detailed fruit designs to stand out. Cups and saucers coordinate with the same Harvest theme.
