Fun facts: 
The Harlequin Fiestaware line was sold exclusively at F.W. Woolworth Co. This glaze was developed and used by Homer Laughlin Pottery Company. The bowl and lid have the distinctive rings. Lid and side handles are triangular-shaped, art deco style. These covered sugar bowls, especially in excellent condition and color, are nearly impossible to find and highly desirable.
