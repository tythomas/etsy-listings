Description

1960s Notre Dame Fighting Irish Pendleton 100% virgin wool stadium blanket with original cushion and clear carrying case. Approx. 48" x 74" including fringe.

A hard-to-find vintage Notre Dame collectible combining two iconic names — the Fighting Irish and Pendleton Woolen Mills.

Dating to the 1960s, this Pendleton wool stadium blanket features the classic Notre Dame Fighting Irish football player with ND jersey over a green shamrock. Even better, this example remains with its original foam stadium cushion and clear carrying case, creating a much harder-to-find complete vintage stadium set.

Originally designed as a portable “Robe in a Bag” for football games and outdoor events, the blanket is made from 100% virgin wool in a rich navy blue with green, gold, and white graphics.

A wonderful piece for a Notre Dame collection, vintage college football display, sports room, office, alumni gift, tailgate setup, or Pendleton collection.

DETAILS

• Maker: Pendleton Woolen Mills, Pendleton, Oregon
• University: Notre Dame
• Design: Fighting Irish football player, ND jersey and green shamrock
• Era: 1960s
• Material: 100% virgin wool
• Color: Navy blue with green, gold and white design
• Style: Pendleton stadium blanket / Robe in a Bag
• Blanket size: Approx. 48" x 74" including fringe
• Fringe: Approx. 5" at each end
• Includes: Wool blanket, original foam seat cushion and original clear carrying case
• Quantity: One complete vintage set

CONDITION

This is a vintage set with the blanket, cushion and carrying case each showing different levels of age-related wear.

• Blanket: Very well preserved with strong navy color and soft wool. The printed ND design shows minor age-related crackling. No major damage noted.

• Cushion: Original foam stadium cushion is included. A few small holes are present from age/use.

• Carrying case: Original clear plastic case shows age-related staining and small tears near the handle area. It remains an important part of the original set but should be considered more fragile than the blanket itself.
