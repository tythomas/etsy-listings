Description

Authentic Anchor Hocking Manhattan depression glass pieces produced from 1938-1943. Choose from 10" dinner plates, 6" bread plates, or 10 oz tumblers.

MAKER: Anchor Hocking 
PATTERN: Manhattan
CIRCA: 1938 - 1943

This listing offers individual pieces from the Manhattan pattern, known for its distinctive concentric ring design that creates a modern look despite its Depression Era origins.

Available:
- 10" Dinner Plates (Quantity: 6)
- 6" Bread Plates
- 10 oz Tumblers, 5 1/4" tall (Quantity: 5)

The Manhattan pattern remains one of Anchor Hocking's most recognizable Depression glass designs and works equally well in vintage collections, everyday use, and table settings.

All pieces are in vintage condition with no chips or cracks noted. Glass remains clear and bright with only light signs of age-appropriate use. Please note there are lines in the glass as well as visible seams - these are not cracks but a result of manufacturing . Bread plates show these lines. 

Shipping Note:
Manhattan glass is surprisingly heavy for its size. Shipping costs are based on the weight of the glass, secure packaging, and your distance from Illinois.
