Description

Antique-style cream stoneware storage crock with a substantial rolled rim, glossy dark brown interior, and warm speckled glaze. Measures approx. 7 3/4" tall by 8" in outside diameter, with a 6 3/4" opening. A handsome piece for primitive farmhouse kitchen display, utensil storage, or a rustic collection.

Unmarked; exact maker and production date are unknown. The cream exterior and dark brown interior are consistent with older American utilitarian stoneware, possibly late 19th to early 20th century. Salt glazing and Albany-type interior glaze are possibilities, not verified identifications.

CONDITION: Vintage wear, scattered dark speckling, staining and glaze crazing. The thick rolled rim has no chips. A fine vertical line is visible on the exterior side and stops before the bottom; it may be a hairline crack, but this cannot be confirmed. The underside also has a distinct curved dark line with some apparent surface depth; it could be a firing-related separation or a later hairline crack. Neither line has been confirmed to extend through to the brown-glazed interior. Both areas are photographed closely. Sold as a decorative vintage piece; watertightness and food safety have not been tested or guaranteed. Please review all photographs for condition.
