Description

Antique Miller's Apple Vinegar bottle — aqua glass, complete original paper label, intact lightning stopper, 11 1/2" tall. The body is embossed THIS BOTTLE NOT TO BE SOLD, and the label names Miller Bottling Works of Canton, Illinois, with the 16 fluid ounce contents wording required after 1906.

Measurements:
• Height: 12" to the top of the stopper
• Base diameter:  3 1/4"

The label is paper and original to the bottle — keep it dry, and wipe the glass rather than washing the bottle.

CONDITION
• No chips or cracks on the lip, neck, shoulder, body or base
• The glass is clear, not cloudy
• Paper label is complete and every word is readable, with age toning and scattered small spots
• One lower corner of the label is lifting slightly away from the glass, pictured
• Small nick missing from the paper along that same lower edge
• The wire bail is heavily rusted and the rubber gasket is dried and hardened
• Light haze and a few dried specks inside the bottle
• Grey soil in the base recess and along the bottom edge
• Bubbles and wavy lines in the glass came from the original mold, not damage
