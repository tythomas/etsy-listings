Description

Beautiful mid-century ash splint trapper’s pack basket, ca. 1948–1955. Tight weave, canvas straps, brass-tacked rim, and warm patina—perfect for rustic display or Adirondack decor.
Step back into the golden age of American craftsmanship with this authentic ash splint trapper’s pack basket, dating from approximately 1948–1955. Handmade in the Adirondack or Maine style, this basket represents the post-WWII transition when traditional ash-splint weaving met surplus military materials—creating rugged yet beautiful field gear for trappers, hunters, and guides.

🧴 Care & Display
This vintage pack basket has been beautifully preserved for over seventy years and should continue to age gracefully with simple, gentle care:

~ Cleaning: Dust with a soft brush or dry microfiber cloth. Avoid damp cloths or cleaning products, which can soften or discolor the ash splints.

~ Conditioning: If the wood appears dry, apply a very light coat of natural beeswax or lemon oil using a soft cloth—never heavy oils or modern polyurethane.

~ Straps: Cotton straps can be lightly brushed or vacuumed; avoid washing or soaking, as water can shrink the webbing and loosen rivets.

~ Display: Best kept in a dry indoor setting away from direct sunlight or high humidity. Perfect for rustic wall display, entryway accent, or use as a decorative storage piece.

~ Preservation Tip: To maintain its original patina, do not sand or refinish the rim or splints—the aged surface is part of its authenticity and collector appeal.

🧭 Historical Context
This era of basketry followed wartime resource changes—leather harnesses gave way to strong cotton webbing, and craftspeople adapted field designs for durability and comfort. Baskets like this one were standard gear for Maine and Adirondack trappers and foragers, prized for their strength, balance, and breathable construction.

A superb piece for Adirondack lodge, cabin, or farmhouse décor. Ideal for hanging on a wall, displaying by a fireplace, or filling with foraged pinecones, wool blankets, or vintage outdoor tools. Equally at home as a rustic art statement in a modern setting.
