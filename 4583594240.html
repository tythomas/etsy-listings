Description

Antique Yellow Bird appliqué quilt with hand-appliquéd yellow birds, brown and white woven gingham, extensive hand quilting and documented Cambridge, Illinois family provenance. The attached collection label records the quilt at approximately 78" x 90".

This distinctive quilt is identified on its attached collection label as “Yellow Bird.” The label records it as coming from Jere Payne of Cambridge, Illinois and states that it was made by her grandmother. Research identifies Jere as Jerelyn Ann “Jere” Reed Payne (1931–2016). Based on the documented family provenance, fabrics, construction and comparable period bird appliqué quilts, I date this quilt to approximately the 1930s–1940s, with the early 1950s remaining possible.

The design is especially unusual. Pale yellow cotton birds are appliquéd across brown and white woven gingham blocks, with the birds shown in different flying positions. Their edges are secured with dense hand-worked buttonhole/blanket stitching, and the wings, tails, black eyes and beaks have additional embroidered details. The quilt is extensively hand quilted with geometric designs and graceful figural motifs, including additional birds quilted into the cream areas.

PROVENANCE
The original attached collection label reads “Yellow Bird,” records the size as 78" x 90", identifies the quilt as from Jere Payne of Cambridge, Illinois, and states “Made by her Grandmother.” It also records a July 24, 2016 purchase date from the collection of Joyce Koller, Cambridge, Illinois. The 2016 date is the collection/purchase date, not the date the quilt was made.
