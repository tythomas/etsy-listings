Description

A large 16-inch oval serving platter in the Woodland pattern, English, with pink cabbage roses around a scalloped, embossed rim.

The reverse carries the pattern name WOODLAND printed in red, and a faint green wreath mark where only the words and Co, Ltd and ENGLAND are still readable, so the pottery that made it stays unnamed. ENGLAND without Made in, plus and Co Ltd, dates it between roughly 1891 and the 1920s.

The body is a light semi-porcelain rather than heavy ironstone. Pink cabbage-rose sprays with pale green ferns ring a scalloped rim moulded with small daisy rosettes, scrollwork and a beaded inner band, and one smaller spray sits in the centre of the well. Gold was laid along the scalloped edge and brushed into the end scrolls; much of it has since worn away.
