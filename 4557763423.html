Description

Antique Art Nouveau tea tray — ebonized wood frame, glass over a dark mahogany panel with a printed medallion and border, nickel bail handles with turned wood grips.

The decoration is printed, not inlaid: the lines are all the same weight and the wood grain runs straight through underneath. No maker's mark.

Measurements:
• Width: 11 1/4"
• Length: 17 3/8"
• Height including the gallery: 1 1/8"
• Overall length across the handles: 19 1/2"

Details:
• STYLE: Art Nouveau — central palmette medallion with whiplash scrolls and a small orange oval, plus a printed arrow-and-lens border on all four sides
• CIRCA: early 1900s to 1930s
• FRAME: ebonized wood, mitered and nailed, with a raised gallery edge
• PANEL: dark mahogany-grained wood under a sheet of glass
• HANDLES: nickel-plated wire bails with ebonized turned wood grips, mounted on pierced plates with slotted screws through the gallery
• BACK: covered in its original grey-green wool felt
• MARK: none

Care: wipe the glass with a damp cloth. Do not soak the wood or the felt back.

CONDITION
• Glass is intact — no cracks, chips or breaks
• Both handles present and firmly mounted; one mounting screw is missing from one of the plates
• Printed medallion and border are complete, with no flaking or loss
• Frame corners are tight and the panel is flat, with no warping
• Broad pale streaking and blotching across the panel under the glass, pictured — it is under the sealed glass, so it cannot be cleaned off
