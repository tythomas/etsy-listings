Description

Vintage Hanson Way Rite household kitchen scale with a red metal weighing platform, cream-painted body, and pale blue-gray circular dial. Made by Hanson Scale Co. in Chicago, USA, this 25-pound-capacity scale has the timeworn character of an old farmhouse kitchen piece.

The needle responds when weight is placed on the platform, and the scale can be adjusted to zero. Weighing accuracy has not been verified, so it is offered as vintage decor and a collectible household scale rather than a calibrated measuring instrument.

CONDITION
Authentic vintage condition with extensive surface rust/oxidation, scratches, finish wear, paint loss, and age discoloration. Rust and staining are especially evident across the dial face and underside. The printed numerals remain readable. An old pencil is completely enclosed inside the weighing platform, apparently inserted through an opening sometime in the past; it has been left in place. The needle responds to weight and the zero setting is adjustable. Please examine all photographs closely as they form part of the condition description.
