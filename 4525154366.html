Description

Handmade circa 1940s appliqué quilt, hand quilted with original quiltmaker's guide marks still visible. Measures 80 x 63 inches.

This handmade American appliqué quilt dates to approximately the 1940s and features a beautiful sage green and muted pumpkin orange color palette against a softly aged ivory background. Hand appliqué, detailed hand quilting, and an exceptional overall presentation make this a wonderful addition to a quilt collection or a timeless statement piece for cottage, farmhouse, folk art, or vintage-inspired interiors.

Details
- Circa 1940s
- Handmade American quilt
- Measures approximately 80" x 63"
- Machine-pieced quilt top
- Hand blanket-stitched appliqué
- Hand quilted throughout
- Hand-finished binding
- Lightweight construction with minimal batting for a soft, beautiful drape

One of this quilt's most interesting features is that the original quiltmaker's quilting guide marks remain visible in several areas beneath the hand quilting. These original layout marks provide a fascinating glimpse into the maker's design process and are part of the quilt's handmade authenticity.
