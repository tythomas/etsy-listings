Description

One vintage Portsmouth 563 EPNS water pitcher with a flared rim, scroll handle, and broad pedestal foot.

Made from electroplated nickel silver, this substantial silverplate pitcher has a bulbous body, banded shoulder, asymmetrical flared rim, large handle with a scroll design, and pedestal footed form.
