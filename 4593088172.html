Description

A beautifully preserved vintage 48 State Flowers quilt featuring 48 different floral blocks, each representing a U.S. state, with hand appliqué, colorful embroidery, and exceptionally detailed hand quilting. Measures approximately 73 x 82 inches.

Each cream-colored block features a distinct state flower and an embroidered state name or abbreviation in a small circular motif. The flowers are framed by vibrant coral-pink sashing, with an intricately quilted coral border and coordinating solid coral backing. The coral fabric has a smooth feel and subtle sheen; its precise fiber content has not been verified. Pink hand-quilting stitches form decorative floral and geometric patterns across the quilt.

HISTORY & AGE
Believed to date to the 1930s–1940s based on its design and construction. The current owner, age 84, reports that the quilt was made by her great-grandmother, who was from Kentucky.  The 48-state design reflects the era before Alaska and Hawaii became states. A connection to a published State Flowers pattern series is possible but has not been confirmed.

MEASUREMENTS
Approximately 73 x 82 inches.

CONSTRUCTION
Hand appliquéd and embroidered floral blocks; extensive hand quilting. It feels as though it may contain very thin batting, but this cannot be confirmed without opening a seam, which has not been done.

CONDITION
Exceptionally well preserved according to careful inspection. No stains, tears, open seams, noticeable fading, or odors reported. Colors remain vibrant. The quilt has not been washed. As an older handmade textile, it has natural variations in stitching and surface texture; please examine all photographs.

This is one original vintage quilt, not a reproduction. Suitable for a collector, textile display, or carefully used bed covering. Please handle and clean antique textiles with appropriate care.
