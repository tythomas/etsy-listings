Description

Red-stained woven wood splint picnic basket with a cream diamond band, a hinged embossed lid and two bentwood swing handles.

A torn foil maker's sticker survives under the loose bottom liner, and the owner's label inside reads Marie Ferguson, Towanda, Illinois.

MAKER: attributed to Red-Man (W.C. Redmon Sons & Co., Peru, Indiana) on the diamond weave and the construction. The foil sticker is torn and only part of the name still reads.
CIRCA: 1940s-1950s

Measurements:
• Length: 18 1/4"
• Width: 12"
• Height with the lid closed: 9 3/4"
• Height with the handles up: 15"
• Interior depth: 9"

Construction:
• Red-dyed flat wood splint woven over splint stakes
• Natural tan diamond band across both faces
• Bentwood top rim and bottom band, stapled
• One-piece hardboard lid, embossed all over with a pyramid grid, scalloped edge
• Two stamped brass butt hinges at the back
• Two bentwood swing handles on domed rivets
• Coated paper lining with a loose fiberboard bottom liner

Wiped clean and aired.

CONDITION
• No missing weavers, no broken or split stakes
• Hinges are tight and all the screws are there
• The paper lining has a wide water stain and an open tear, pictured
• Fading of the red portion at the bottom - pictured
• A second smaller lifted spot in the lining, plus creases and scuffs
• Paint loss and scuffing on both handle grips, bare wood at one handle top
• Dark speckling in the splint near the top rows, and the red is unevenly sun-faded
• Nail holes and dents along the bottom band
• Underside is dry and grey with a splintery corner; the base panel is stained
• Lid is mottled underneath, and chipped along the scalloped edge
• Brass hinges are tarnished, one leaf pitted, and the rivets have darkened
• Empty — no pie shelf, plates or picnic fittings
• There is no latch; this style closes by weight alone
