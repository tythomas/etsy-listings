Description

Set of 8 vintage Sasaki Coronation Light Blue champagne coupes, with pale blue bowls on clear twisted stems. Two sets of 8 available 

Sasaki made this pattern in Japan through the 1960s and 70s, and it is long discontinued. The bowls are hand blown and thin, so the blue is soft rather than strong — it shows up best when the glasses are grouped together or held to the light. Light blue is the hardest of the Coronation colors to find, and the champagne coupe is the shape that disappears first.

Each stem is a solid clear twist that flares into a swirled star where it meets the bowl, so you see a little rosette in the bottom of every glass. The feet are plain, thin discs with a smooth polished center. There is no etched mark on the base — Sasaki sold this line with paper labels, which almost never survive. All eight match.
