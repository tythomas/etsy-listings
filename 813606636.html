FUN FACTS ~ 

**Laurel Potteries of California - Dinnerware
Laurel Potteries began around 1940 and was one of the first of the great California potteries to go out of business. That was in the 1960s. They had offices in San Francisco and Stockton, California. The company was known for its speckled patterns such as California Living, Holiday and Seaside. The dinnerware reached its popularity in the 1950s. The look of the dinnerware is somewhat similar to that of Bauer.

**California Life
The California Life pattern by Laurel was introduced in the early 1950's. Like Brusche's Al Fresco patterns, Laurel's California Life pattern was a solid color with darker specks throughout.

** The Cerama-Stone stoneware line was produced for a short time in the late 1950s.
