Description

Antique pink and white Tree of Life / Pine Tree quilt, hand pieced and beautifully hand quilted. Measures approximately 88" x 81".

A beautiful antique pink and white quilt featuring a striking Tree of Life / Pine Tree variation, with approximately 30 pieced tree motifs set on point within a geometric pink lattice.

The quilt measures approximately 88" x 81" and is shown photographed on a queen-size bed. Its two-color design is made entirely in solid pink and white cotton, giving the quilt an especially bold, graphic appearance.

Each tree is constructed from numerous small pink and white triangles with a solid pink trunk. The trees are surrounded by pink latticework with small square intersections, creating the wonderful secondary geometric pattern visible when the quilt is viewed as a whole.

The quilt is hand pieced and extensively hand quilted. Quilting measures approximately 7–9 stitches per inch in the areas checked. The white fields feature diagonal crosshatch quilting, with outline quilting around the pieced design and closely spaced diagonal quilting through portions of the border. Additional curved quilting motifs can be seen along the outer areas.

Construction details include a narrow white outer border with pink inner bar, thin cotton batting, a narrow hand-applied binding turned to the back, and a plain white cotton backing constructed with a vertical seam.

The Tree of Life / Pine Tree belongs to a longstanding family of American pieced tree patterns that appeared under several related historic names. Based on the pattern, fabrics and construction, this quilt appears consistent with an early 20th-century example, conservatively estimated circa 1900–1930. No maker's name, signature or date was found.
