Description

Vintage Scotch Brand No. 600 cellophane tape storage tin featuring bold red, black, and yellow tartan plaid graphics. This tall, cylindrical metal advertising container measures approx. 6 1/2" high and 5" in diameter. Empty tin with its original removable lid, which lifts off and fits back on properly. Props used in photos not included. 

Manufactured in the USA by Minnesota Mining & Manufacturing Co., Saint Paul, Minnesota (3M). The original lid label reads “Scotch Brand Cellophane Tape,” No. 600, and specifies 12 rolls of 1/2" × 2592" transparent pressure-sensitive adhesive tape. The tape rolls are not included. Mid-century vintage styling; an exact production year has not been verified.

Condition: Original vintage condition with scratches, scuffs, areas of lithographed color loss and surface wear. Some rust is present near the bottom. The lid has visible rust, residue, scratches and discoloration, including wear to its original paper label on bottom. Please inspect the photographs closely for the extent and placement of wear. Sold empty as a decorative collectible storage tin, not with tape.
