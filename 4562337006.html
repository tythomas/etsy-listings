Description

Regal by Sango covered sugar bowl — cream porcelain with pink roses, a tan scrollwork border and gold trim, made in Japan in the 1950s.

The base is marked SANGO CHINA MADE IN JAPAN in a red and gold cartouche. The gold band, acorn finial and handle scrollwork are all still bright.

Measurements:

Hand wash only — the gold trim is not dishwasher or microwave safe.
