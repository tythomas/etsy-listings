This beautiful turquoise blue glass lidded vegetable bowl with lid is just gorgeous ~ I am not sure the lid actually goes with the bowl - it does fit, so it is yours :)

Manufacturer: Kemple 
Pattern: Lace and Dewdrop Blue 

Unsure of exact manufacturing date ~ I believe this is an original piece and not a 1970s reproduction since there are no markings on this piece. ( The 1970s reproduction pieces have a KW imprinted on the bottom )

Kemple glass was in business from 1945 to 1970 . Wheaton bought this mould afterwards. This original pattern was made in the late 1800's and was used by Phoenix Glass
