Description

Vintage brown Taperlite overnight train case with brass-tone hardware and a salmon satin interior. Measures approximately 16½" wide × 11½" tall × 7½" deep.

This small hard-sided suitcase is from the Taperlite line by Sardis Luggage Co., dating approximately to the late 1950s–early 1960s. The cognac/rust-brown vinyl covering has contrasting stitching, a Taperlite badge, brass-tone hardware, and a molded handle. The hinge straps are stamped “Sardis Luggage.”

Inside is the original salmon-colored satin lining, a gathered fabric divider, and a zippered pocket. Its compact size works well for vintage storage, travel-themed decor, or a photography prop.

CONDITION
Good vintage condition with visible age and use wear. The exterior has scuffs, scratches, scattered marks and spotting, and edge/corner wear. The brass-tone hardware shows tarnish and finish wear. The interior has marks/staining and some loose or frayed threads around the divider. There is a slight musty storage odor.

Both latches open and close properly, and the interior zipper operates properly. No key is included. Please review all photos for the finish and condition details.
