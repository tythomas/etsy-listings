Description

Homer Laughlin Cavalier Eggshell 16-piece dinnerware set for four, made in October 1954. The wide teal turquoise bands, ivory centers and platinum trim give this early mid-century American china a clean, tailored look.

This listing includes all 16 pieces shown as the four place settings. 
Serving pieces for this pattern are listed separately.

SET INCLUDES:
• 4 dinner plates — 9 1/4 inches
• 4 dessert or bread plates — 6 1/4 inches
• 4 saucers — 6 inches
• 4 cups — 2 3/4 inches tall × 3 5/8 inches diameter; 6-ounce capacity

MAKER AND DATE:
The backstamp reads Cavalier Eggshell by Homer Laughlin, USA, with production code J 54 N 6. The code dates these pieces to October 1954 at Homer Laughlin's Newell, West Virginia operation. Cavalier Eggshell was introduced in 1953 and was the company's final eggshell-weight dinnerware shape.

The decoration features a solid teal or turquoise band with platinum trim. No unverified pattern name has been assigned; similarly colored Cavalier patterns such as Romance, Lupine and Turquoise Melody include floral decoration that is not present here.

CONDITION:
All 16 pieces are free of chips, cracks, repairs and glaze crackling. No visible surface scratching is noted. The platinum trim has very little wear overall. The cups show light wear along the inner platinum line from stacking. Please review the photographs closely for the complete condition and appearance.

Hand washing is recommended to protect the vintage platinum trim. Not microwave safe because of the metallic decoration.

A striking service for four for a mid-century table, intimate dinner, special occasion or Homer Laughlin collection.
