Description

Vintage Way Rite 25 lb kitchen scale with a distressed red 5½-inch square platform, tan metal body, and 6½-inch round dial. Measures 9¼ inches tall.

This vintage Way Rite countertop kitchen scale was made in Chicago, USA. Its square red metal weighing platform, warm tan painted body, and large numbered dial make it a striking piece of farmhouse kitchen decor or an industrial-style display. Estimated mid-20th century; exact production year has not been verified.
