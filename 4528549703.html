Vintage Noritake Progression Bright Side 4-piece place setting with coral poppy design, circa 1976–1982.

Complete 4-piece Noritake Progression "Bright Side" place setting in the cheerful 9079 poppy pattern, produced from 1976 to 1982.

Each place setting includes:
