Description

Antique Erphila set of eight Czech porcelain dinner plates, in a Dresden-style floral pattern with a purple iris bouquet, black-and-gilt scrollwork borders, and gold rims. Each plate measures 10 3/4 inches.

Erphila is the import mark of Ebeling & Reuss of Philadelphia, one of America's premier importers of fine European porcelain; the 'Czecho-Slovakia' backstamp dates this set to roughly the 1920s to early 1930s, making it genuinely antique. As with most Erphila imports, the underlying Czech factory is not named on the porcelain, part of the intrigue of these early American-market wares. A complete matched set of eight dinner plates from this period is uncommon.

Each plate centers on a beautifully shaded bouquet ~ a large purple iris with roses, primula, and blue forget-me-nots ~ framed by an elaborate black-and-gilt scrollwork border scattered with smaller flower sprays, and finished with a softly scalloped, paneled rim and bright gold trim.
