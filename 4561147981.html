Description

Antique c.1850-1865 tulip applique quilt with deep red floral applique and elaborate hand-quilted feather wreaths. Measures approximately 83" x 82".

A striking mid-19th-century American applique quilt combining bold tulip and vine motifs with exceptionally detailed hand quilting.

The quilt is constructed on a white cotton ground with repeating angular tulip blossoms worked in deep red and contrasting stem, leaf and calyx applique that now appears tan/brown. Large open areas between the applique motifs are filled with elaborate hand-quilted feather wreaths and other curving feather and foliate designs. Additional decorative quilting fills the wide outer border.

The narrow red inner border and red edge binding create a strong frame around the white ground and floral design.

DATE
• Based on its applique style, construction, quilting motifs and comparison with documented museum examples, this quilt appears to date to approximately 1850-1865.

• Red-and-green floral applique quilts were especially associated with American quiltmaking during the middle decades of the 19th century. The stem and leaf fabric on this quilt now appears tan/brown. Because of the age and deterioration of this particular fabric, its original color may have changed over time. to tan; its original color cannot be confirmed.

• The deep red cotton is consistent in appearance with the strong reds used in mid-19th-century quilts. Turkey red was commonly used during this period, but the dye used in this quilt has not been tested and is therefore not identified as Turkey red with certainty.

CONDITION
This is an approximately 160+ year-old textile with significant age-related wear, particularly to the tan/brown stem, leaf and calyx applique. Please review all photographs closely.

• The tan/brown applique fabric has deteriorated in numerous areas, with splitting, fraying and fabric loss. Some areas have worn through enough to expose the batting beneath.
• The deep red and white cotton fabrics are substantially more intact, although age-related wear is present.
• A broad tan discoloration is present across one quilted medallion.
• Several smaller tan/rust-colored stains are scattered across the white ground and border.
• General age-related toning/soiling is present on the white cotton.
• The reverse has an overall cream/aged cast with two rust-colored stains and a pale gray discolored area.
• The red binding has faded lighter than the interior red and has a frayed area along a fold.
• One medallion has what appears to be guide marks from the maker - pictured
• Hand quilting remains intact throughout overall.
• No patches or obvious later repairs were observed.
• Quilt has not been laundered.

Because of its age and areas of deteriorated applique, this quilt is best suited to collecting, display or light decorative use rather than everyday bedding.
