Description

A visually striking antique red and white Irish Chain quilt, generously sized at 92 x 94". Its bold geometric patchwork, visible hand quilting, and beautifully timeworn character make it a wonderful farmhouse, country cottage, or antique textile display piece.

An unusual full-width cream-colored border finishes one end of the quilt, with two centrally placed pink floral fabric appliqués and green embroidered stems and leaves. The other edges feature red fabric binding finished with consistent white machine zigzag stitching. One white square bears the handwritten inscription “Mrs. E. F. Rowe”; the identity and connection of this name to the quilt are unknown.

DETAILS
• Size: approximately 92" x 94"
• Pattern: red and white Irish Chain variation
• Construction: hand-quilted, thin and lightweight, with no batting
• Binding: red fabric with white zigzag stitching along the bound edges; contrasting cream floral border across one end
• Estimated age: early-to-mid 20th century,
