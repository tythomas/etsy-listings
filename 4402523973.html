FULL DESCRIPTION
This exquisite Noritake dinner plate was produced between 1915 and 1923 during the early Art Deco transition. Each piece features hand-applied raised gold scrollwork, floral cartouches, and a pearl-white porcelain body finished with a bright gold verge line. The pattern is No. 16034, confirmed by the green “M” wreath mark used by Noritake during its first export era.

These plates were created in small-run artisan batches and were often imported into the United States through early 20th-century department stores. The craftsmanship is unmistakable: heavy raised gilt, intricate detailing, and a glowing white base typical of Noritake’s premium pre-war porcelain.

All four plates are in beautiful antique condition for their age. Three have no damage. One plate has a tiny nick in the gold on the rim (very common on raised-gilt 100-year-old pieces). All display beautifully.
