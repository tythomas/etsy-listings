Description

Set of four pink Depression glass sherbet dishes in Federal Glass's Patrician pattern, also called Spoke. Each stands 2 7/8 inches tall and measures 3 3/4 inches across.

Federal Glass made Patrician between 1933 and 1937, and pink was the colour they made least of — amber turns up everywhere, pink far less often. Finding four that have stayed together is the harder part.

The pattern is pressed right into the glass, not etched or cut. Under the rim there is a row of little arches, then a zigzag band and a line of beading, and below that a wide band of scrolls and fleur-de-lis on a frosted, pebbly ground. A ring of scallops finishes it just above the short stem. The colour is in the glass itself, not sprayed on. Federal almost never marked this line, so there is no backstamp to look for — the pattern is the signature.
