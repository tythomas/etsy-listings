Description

Listing cost is for ONE Vintage blue salt glaze large mixing bowl

Measures: 10 1/4"  across the top of the bowl x 5 1/4" tall x 1 3/4" band 

VERY GOOD vintage condition: No chips - NO cracks  - NO hairline ~ NO crackling of the glaze.
The glaze is bright with a nice shine - NOTE- there are some inconsistencies with the coloring of the glaze on the outside of bowl, near the bottom - there are two small circle areas where the glaze is a bit darker. Bowl's rim is smooth to the touch with no rough areas- rim is slightly discolored from use over the years with some light pitting in areas. The outer portion of the bowl on sides and bottom have some raised pin point bumps - this is most likely from the firing process. 
NOTE- I have added photos to show the areas where the raw pottery is exposed from either a small flake in the glaze or glaze pop that occurred during firing. A large glaze pop is noted on the side and also in the inside bottom of bowl - see photos grid. The bottom of base of bowl has two area where the glaze has flaked off as well - these areas are larger than the ones on the side. This does not affect how the bowl sits. 
One is on the side of the outer band near rim and the other on the inside of bowl near rim. 

Coloring was also hard to capture - it varies in intensity depending on lighting~  Gorgeous coloring ~ large sized bowl. 

A  beautiful picket fence pattern encircles the bowl!

Nice addition for your country farmhouse ~ I love using these bowls to serve cut up fruit or pasta salad in!
