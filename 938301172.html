Description

Vintage Nikko Colorstone Interplay stoneware made in Japan, circa 1970s. Choose 10 1/2" dinner plates, 7 1/2" salad plates, cup and saucer sets, extra saucers or the lidded sugar bowl.

Vintage Color Stone by Nikko stoneware dinnerware made in Japan, featuring a bold retro concentric-ring design in orange, mustard yellow and brown against a lightly speckled neutral background.

This discontinued Colorstone design is commonly identified in the vintage dinnerware market as the Interplay pattern. Pieces are sold by variation, making this listing ideal for replacing missing pieces, adding to an existing Nikko service or building a set.

AVAILABLE:

• Set of 4 Dinner Plates — 10 1/2" diameter
• Set of 4 Salad Plates — 7 1/2" diameter
• 2 Cups + 2 Saucers — cups measure 3" tall and hold approximately 6–8 oz; saucers measure 6 1/2" diameter
• Set of 2 Extra Saucers — 6 1/2" diameter
• Sugar Bowl + Lid

Pieces are marked Color Stone by Nikko — Made in Japan. Backstamps also identify the dinnerware as freezer-to-oven safe, dishwasher safe and microwave oven safe.

CONDITION

Very good vintage condition overall.

No chips or cracks noted. Glaze remains bright and glossy, and edges are smooth to the touch without noted nicks or rough spots.

Some small manufacturing bumps or irregularities are visible on some plate surfaces and are part of the original production. Please review photos for examples.
