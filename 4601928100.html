Description

Set of four vintage Christmas ornaments featuring two posable Japanese felt elves and two miniature flocked bottle-brush wreaths. The elves measure just under 7" from hat tip to toes with their legs extended; each wreath measures approx. 3 1/4" across.

The pair of charming pixie elves features molded expressive faces, pointed ears, and green and white felt outfits. Their bendable arms and legs hold their positions for hanging or seated display. The white elf retains a JAPAN label, and the green elf retains an ALL NEW MATERIAL JAPAN label. Maker is unknown. Likely mid-20th century, approximately 1950s–1960s.

The two small wreaths feature green and peach-colored bottle-brush foliage with white flocking, ribbons, bells, and berry-like decorations. Both wreaths retain their bells and decorations.

Vintage condition with age-related wear to the felt and flocked surfaces, including visible fuzziness, fading and fraying along portions of the elf clothing.
