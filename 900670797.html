Description

Colony Harvest Grape milk glass punch bowl set by Indiana Glass — a 14-inch footed bowl on a gold base with 14 matching cups, in the classic raised white grape-and-leaf pattern of the 1960s–70s.

Indiana Glass produced the Harvest pattern under its Colony brand, and the white milk glass version has stayed a shower-and-wedding favorite for decades. This is a complete set: one large pedestal bowl and a full fourteen cups, all in the same grape motif. The glass is heavy and thick, with the crisp molded grape clusters and leaves the pattern is known for.

Measurements:
• Punch bowl: 14" diameter x 8" tall (includes gold base)
• Cups: 2.5" tall x 3.5" diameter at the rim, 6 oz each
• Cups included: 14

Maker & pattern:
• Maker: Indiana Glass (Colony brand)
• Pattern: Harvest / Harvest Grape, white milk glass
• Circa: 1960s–1970s

Very good vintage condition. No chips, cracks, or discoloration on the glass, and no staining on the white milk glass. The rims of the bowl and cups are free of nicks and chips. The gold-tone base shows some loss of the gold finish in areas — please see the photos, which show this accurately.
