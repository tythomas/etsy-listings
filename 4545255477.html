Description

Set of six Noritake "Provincial" water goblets in moss green — heavy pressed glass with deep vertical panels and a band of scalloped arches circling the upper bowl, each standing about 5 3/4” tall.

Noritake produced the Provincial line in Japan from roughly 1974 into the late 1980s. It was pressed rather than blown, which is why the panels come up so crisp and why the glass has real weight in the hand. This is the deeper olive-toned moss green.

The bowl is a flared tulip shape with vertical panels running up from the base.

There is no backstamp. Noritake marked its 1970s pressed glassware with paper foil labels rather than moulded marks, so a bare base is expected on this line rather than a gap — the attribution here rests on the panel-and-arch moulding, the knop stem, and the water goblet's height, not on a mark.
