Description

Haas & Czjzek 1926 Art Deco porcelain tea service for eight, complete with 27 pale green and gold pieces.

Exceptionally thin, delicate porcelain has a soft satin or matte pale green finish, contrasting white cup interiors, faceted Art Deco forms and restrained geometric gold decoration. The handles, finials, rims and accents are richly gilded.

The green crowned H&C Schlaggenwald Czechoslovakia backstamp identifies Haas & Czjzek, a historic Bohemian porcelain manufacturer whose factory was founded in 1792. The printed 1792 is the factory's founding year. A separate inscription reads “Nina England 1926” with a painter's monogram, indicating that the service was decorated and signed in 1926. No documented factory pattern name has been established.

THE 27-PIECE SERVICE INCLUDES
• 8 cups: 2 7/8 inches tall × 3 3/8 inches across the rim
• 8 saucers: 5 1/2 inches across
• 8 plates: 8 inches across
• 1 teapot with lid: 10 inches from spout to handle × 5 1/2 inches tall to the finial; holds approximately 3 cups or 24 fluid ounces
• 1 covered sugar bowl: 4 1/4 inches tall to the finial
• 1 creamer: 3 3/8 inches tall; holds approximately 1 cup or 8 fluid ounces

CONDITION
Very good antique condition with no cracks or repairs and no heavy utensil scratching. 

One of the two interchangeable lids has a small, shallow chip on its underside rim - captured in last photo. The chip is not prominent when the lid is in place and does not affect its fit or use; the sugar-bowl and teapot lids are the same size and fit either piece. 

Minor manufacturing irregularities include a small white glaze/color skip on several plates, a tiny dark firing speck on one saucer, and several pinpoint glaze holes on the side of the teapot - all pictured. 

The creamer has very light scratching on its gilded handle, and the sugar bowl and tea pot have localized gold loss inside where the lid rests.

 These details are shown in the close-up photographs. The remaining gilding is bright and exceptionally well preserved. The soft matte appearance is the original satin-finished surface rather than dullness from wear.

The complete set has been carefully hand washed to remove storage dust. Because of its age, delicate porcelain and gold decoration, continued gentle hand washing is recommended. Not suitable for a dishwasher or microwave.

A distinctive signed Art Deco service for afternoon tea, elegant entertaining, a wedding gift or a collector of early Czechoslovakian and Bohemian porcelain.
