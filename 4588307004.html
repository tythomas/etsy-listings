Description

Vintage 1930s–1940s Nine Patch tied quilt featuring scrappy patchwork blocks set between a repeating green floral cotton print. The varied period fabrics include checks, plaids, stripes and small-scale prints in blue, pink, lavender, green, burgundy, black, brown and cream. Mixed orange, yellow and ivory ties add wonderful handmade folk character.

Measures approximately 69 x 74 inches.

Construction: machine-pieced cotton Nine Patch top with large green floral alternate blocks, thin batting, and a one-piece plain cotton backing. The quilt is hand tied with a mix of yarn and heavier thread - three different colors. The batting appears to be an older thin cotton. 

Dating: Based on the fabrics, construction and overall design, this quilt dates to approximately the 1930s–1940s.

Condition: Unwashed vintage condition. The quilt has a light musty/storage smell that remains after airing. There are a couple areas of discoloration on the front and back, along with localized seam opening/fraying as shown in the photos. The backing is one piece with scattered age-related discoloration. Please review all photos closely for condition.

A colorful, highly decorative vintage quilt with a soft farmhouse and cottage look. Its 69 x 74 inch size works beautifully displayed across a bed, folded at the foot of a bed, or used as a large throw.
