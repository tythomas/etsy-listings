Description

One antique Metlox Poppytrail Provincial Fruit Green covered sugar bowl with fitted lid, approx. 5 1/2" high with an 8-ounce catalog capacity.

Strongly attributed to the Provincial Fruit Green pattern by Metlox Poppytrail, this two-handled pedestal sugar bowl was made in California. The matching pattern was produced from 1960 to 1982, placing this example circa the 1970s. Its cream-speckled body, olive-green lid and foot bands, colorful fruit-and-leaf decoration, loop handles, and three-lobed finial correspond to cataloged examples of this form.

The glazed ceramic california pottery combines warm cream, olive green, burgundy-red, yellow-orange, and natural leaf tones. This speckled pottery pedestal bowl works equally well as replacement china for the Provincial Fruit pattern or as colorful tableware among olive green dishes for a coffee or tea service.

Price is for one complete sugar bowl consisting of the bowl and its fitted lid.
