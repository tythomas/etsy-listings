Description

This striking antique quilt features the historic Crown of Thorns / Rocky Mountain Road design worked primarily in deep green and cream, framed by gold/tan, dark, green and rich burgundy border strips.

Dating to approximately the turn of the 20th century, likely circa 1890–1915 based on its fabrics and construction. The dating is based on the solid-color plain-weave cottons, hand piecing, hand quilting, hand-seamed backing, edge construction and aging characteristics of the fabrics rather than on the pattern name alone.

The hand piecing has wonderful early folk-made character, with noticeably irregular points, unevenly sized triangles and variations in the piecing that are original to the quilt's construction rather than later damage. The quilting is also hand worked.

MEASUREMENTS
Approximately 80 x 82 inches.

CONSTRUCTION & MATERIALS
- Hand pieced
- Hand quilted
- Cotton fabrics
- Cotton batting
- Cream/off-white plain-weave cotton backing assembled with hand-sewn seams
- No separate applied binding; the edge is finished as part of the quilt's original hand construction

CONDITION
Please review all photographs closely. This is an antique textile with age and condition issues and is not a perfect quilt.

There are numerous open/separated piecing seams throughout the quilt, including several openings where the batting can be seen. These are primarily failures of the seam stitching. The fabrics themselves remain intact without widespread fabric deterioration or fabric loss.

There is age-related fading and color change, particularly in portions of the dark border fabric, along with scattered spotting/discoloration. The different solid fabrics have aged and faded differently, which is visible in the photographs.

The uneven points, variable triangle sizes and irregular piecing are original handmade characteristics and should not be confused with damage.

A beautiful survivor with bold graphic color and unmistakable handmade character, especially appealing for an antique quilt collection, historic textile display, layered bedding or period-inspired decor. Because of the open seams, gentle use and appropriate care are recommended.
