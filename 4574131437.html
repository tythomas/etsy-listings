Description

Four Seasons Early Summer floral plates and coordinating Japanese stoneware bowls, sold as a single piece or a set of 4; plates measure approx. 6 1/2" and bowls approx. 6" across the rim.

The floral plates are strongly identified as the Four Seasons Collection “Early Summer” pattern by the photographed backstamp and matching replacement pieces catalog entry. The bowls share the cream speckled glaze and dark brown rim band, but their photographed mark reads “GENUINE STONEWARE JAPAN,” so they are described as coordinating rather than definitively assigned to the pattern. Period advertising documents the Four Seasons collection and Early Summer design in 1985; other specialist references describe the line as 1970s to early 1980s production.

These substantial pieces of japanese stoneware have a warm cream ground and dark brown rim bands. The plates feature white, orange, and yellow flowers, green foliage, brown stems, and turquoise-blue accents. This speckled dinnerware works easily with existing Early Summer place settings, an informal mixed vintage table, or a replacement china collection. Both photographed marks identify the pieces as imported from japan. The bowls are useful as ice cream dishes or for serving cereal and fruit.
