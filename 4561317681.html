Description

Antique English transferware plates in lilac purple — a branching seaweed and coral sprig at each panel of the twelve-sided rim.  Set of four plates, 7" across.

Each back carries the printed mark PORCELAINE OPAQUE over SEVRES with EDWARDS below — James Edwards of Dale Hall, Burslem, 1842 to 1882.

Measurements:
• Diameter: 7"

Hand wash only.

CONDITION
• No chips, cracks or repairs
• Crazing throughout the glaze, front and back — the fine web you can see in the close-up photos. This is normal on earthenware of this age. Not all plates show this. 
• A short brown stain along one crazing line on a rim, pictured
• Small dark flecks in the wells, from the original firing
• One base is tea-toned inside the foot ring, with old paper label residue
• Tiny glaze pits and one small raised glaze bubble, from the original making
• The purple print is strong and unworn, and the depth of colour varies a little from plate to plate
