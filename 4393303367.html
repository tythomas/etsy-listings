Description

Vintage mid-century wool plaid camp blanket, red-green-black-cream stripes, 45x70, excellent condition. Authentic 1950s Esmond style.

This authentic mid-century wool camp blanket features a bold plaid of red, green, black, cream, and gray — a true classic of the 1940s–1950s American outdoor era.
Likely produced by Esmond Mills or a comparable U.S. woolen mill, this blanket is crafted of 100% wool with a brushed nap that’s soft yet substantial.

Measures approximately 45" x 70", perfect as a throw, stadium blanket, or cabin accent. The edges are finished in traditional wool blanket stitching in red yarn — an original factory finish typical of postwar production.
Each corner shows a small wool-yarn tie-off knot, where the stitching run was completed and secured — this is original to manufacture, not a flaw, and common in mid-century Esmond and Nashua mill blankets.
