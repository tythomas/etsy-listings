Description

Vintage Blue Ridge dinner plates in the Green Eyes pattern by Southern Potteries.

These hand painted plates likely date to the 1940s, when Blue Ridge / Southern Potteries was known for its colorful underglaze floral dinnerware. Green Eyes is a documented Blue Ridge pattern and has the brushwork variation expected with hand painted pottery.

Details:
- Maker: Blue Ridge / Southern Potteries
- Pattern: Green Eyes
- Likely 1940s production
- Hand painted underglaze decoration
- Approx. 10 1/4” diameter
- Made in USA
- Discontinued  pattern

Condition:
 All plates are in excellent condition with no chips or cracks noted. Minor hand painted variation and small production characteristics may be present, as expected with Blue Ridge pottery. These appear as tiny raised firing bumps on plate surface and edging. No crackling of the glaze - nice shine. Plate are free from nicking and rough edges.
