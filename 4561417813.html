Description

Blue Ridge Green Briar teacup and saucer - hand-painted green and brown flowers on ivory earthenware, Piecrust shape, 1940s. Neither piece is backstamped, which is common on Blue Ridge; the pattern is named by the green feathered rim and the chartreuse-and-brown flowers on the Piecrust blank.

MAKER: Southern Potteries, Erwin, Tennessee
PATTERN: Green Briar
SHAPE: Piecrust
CIRCA: 1938-1957

Measurements:
• Saucer: 6 1/8" diameter
• Cup: 3 7/8" diameter, 2 1/4" tall

Hand wash only.
