Description

Vintage American-made gold-tone double candle wall sconce with two curved candle arms, three decorative lower hooks, a circular backplate and a four-petal floral accent. Measures 10 inches tall, 7 inches wide and projects approximately 4 1/4 inches from the wall.

This single wall-mounted piece has a reflective brass-tone finish over magnetic metal (not solid brass). The original paper label on the back reads A-138406 and MADE IN THE U.S.A.; the maker is not identified. The design has a traditional, Hollywood Regency-inspired look. Estimated circa 1970s–1980s based on styling, not a confirmed production date.

MEASUREMENTS
Height: 10 inches
Width: 7 inches
Projection: approximately 4 1/4 inches
Quantity: one sconce with two candle cups and three hooks. Candles and mounting hardware are not included.

CONDITION
Vintage condition with visible surface scratching, mottled discoloration, and areas of darkening and wear to the gold-tone finish, especially on the circular backplate. Both candle arms and all three decorative hooks are sturdy and secure. No structural damage noted. The shaped area on the backplate near the attachment point is part of the original mounting construction, not a dent. See close-up photos for condition.

MOUNTING
The reverse has an original three-prong mounting arrangement. The original label instructs the user to slip the prongs over a suitably secured nail head to lock the backplate against the wall. Mounting hardware is not included. Please select wall hardware appropriate for your wall and the item's weight; the original instructions do not establish suitability for every wall type.

A charming vintage accent for a hallway, entryway or traditional gallery wall. For safe use, keep candles away from flammable materials and never leave burning candles unattended.
