Description

One vintage Colony Harvest white milk-glass pedestal cake stand features raised grape-and-leaf decoration and a scalloped edge.

This pressed milk glass stand is strongly attributed to the Colony Harvest pattern made by Indiana Glass for the Colony brand. Historical documentation dates the pattern’s creation to the 1950s, with production continuing after Indiana Glass acquired the molds in 1957. No maker mark is visible.

The glossy opaque white milk glass, round platform, embossed grape clusters, leafy border, and decorated pedestal create a substantial serving and display piece. As a wedding cake stand, it works especially well for wedding desserts, cakes, cupcakes, and pastries, or as part of a vintage milk-glass collection.
