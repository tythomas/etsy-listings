5 COLONIAL TULIP AMBER / HONEY YELLOW  FLAT 6 ounce Juice glasses  

Made by Anchor Hocking - pattern discontinued and difficult to find in this size and color tumbler !! 

VERY GOOD vintage condition - no chips or cracks - great shine -  rims smooth to the touch.  Glass is delicate -  air bubbles can be seen in  all the glasses ( not seen in the photos very well) . Glasses have  visible  mold lines and mold imperfections here and there. Some have slight surface scratches that can be seen when held to the light 

Each glass measures approximately  3 3/4" tall  with a 2 1/2" diameter 
Holds approximately 6 ounces 

Glasses are faintly marked with  the "anchor over H" ( marking used from 1937 until 1968) along with  the lettering  USA.
