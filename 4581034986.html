Description

Set of 6 vintage Preisner Pewter 2304 water or wine goblets, each approximately 6 7/8 inches tall with a 12-ounce capacity.

This matched set of tall pewter goblets has softly rounded bowls, slender traditional stems, and a warm silver-tone finish. Each is marked Preisner Pewter 2304 on the underside. Their substantial 12 oz size works well for water, wine, or a distinctive vintage table setting.

Preisner produced classic American pewter tableware and giftware for much of the 20th century. Based on the styling, spun construction, brushed finish, and maker's mark, these goblets are believed to date from approximately the 1960s–1970s. The 2304 number identifies the model.
