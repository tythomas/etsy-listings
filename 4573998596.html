Description

Noritake YBRY pink rose porcelain bowls, circa 1921, are offered in two sizes - 7 3/8" and 5 1/5" 

The red M-in-wreath mark reads Noritake, Made in Japan, YBRY, and U.S. Design Patented 76832. Independent pattern references identify YBRY as an early Noritake design introduced around 1921.
These glazed white porcelain bowls have a pale cream border decorated with pink roses, blue geometric floral panels, green foliage, gold-tone scrollwork, and gold-tone rim lines. The larger and smaller bowls share the same decoration, making them useful replacement pieces for an existing YBRY service.
