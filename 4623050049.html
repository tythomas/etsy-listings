Description

A distinctive vintage brass Hessian soldier doorstop with a marching pose, detailed military-style uniform and tall loop-shaped hat. Attributed to Virginia Metalcrafters' Colonial Williamsburg reproduction line, this handsome piece is well suited to a traditional entryway, mantel or Colonial-style collection.

DETAILS
• One brass soldier doorstop; sold individually.
• Height: approx. 8.5" overall, including the rectangular pedestal.
• Hollow-backed cast-brass figure with a separate brass base secured by slotted screws.
• The raised marking on the back is read as “W 8-7”; matching examples have been identified as Virginia Metalcrafters model CW 8-7.
• Estimated production period: mid-to-late 20th century. Exact year unknown.

CONDITION
Vintage condition with age-related patina and surface wear. There is noticeable uneven dark discoloration/tarnishing across the FRONT FACE of the rectangular pedestal and a small greenish area of oxidation near one end, as shown in the photographs. The unfinished back and underside retain rough casting texture. Please examine the photographs for the appearance of the actual piece; no restoration or polishing is represented.
