Description

Vintage Sunbonnet Sue appliqué quilt featuring 30 individually decorated figures in a blue-and-cream setting. Measures approximately 71 x 85 inches; each Sunbonnet Sue block measures approximately 10 x 10 inches. Medium weight and very soft to the touch. Offered as a complete quilt for careful display, textile preservation, restoration, or a cutter/craft project.

Hand-appliquéd figures feature contrasting black blanket stitching and decorative embroidery. The quilt appears hand-quilted, with visible quilting across the blocks, blue sashing, and border. Pastel and patterned fabrics create a charming traditional Sunbonnet Sue design. Estimated 1930s–1940s based on style and construction; exact date and maker are not documented. From an Illinois family quilt collection.

IMPORTANT CONDITION: This is a heavily distressed, fragile vintage quilt with extensive fabric deterioration. Of the 30 Sunbonnet Sue figures, 19 are mostly intact (some with slight fraying); the other 11 have varying degrees of fabric loss. The blue border has numerous tears and areas of deteriorated fabric. There is additional edge wear/fraying, slight yellowing, and some visible discoloration. It has not been washed and no odors are present. The fabric is very soft, but its strength should not be assumed. Please review all photographs closely. Not recommended for everyday bedding use; handle gently. Sold as found, without repairs or cleaning.

Approximate dimensions: 71 x 85 inches. Vintage textile sold as one complete quilt, not as individual blocks. Colors may vary slightly by screen.
