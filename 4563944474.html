Vintage Carico Renaissance crystal water goblets with hand-cut leaf decoration and paneled stems. Choose a single goblet, set of 2 or set of 4.
Vintage Carico Renaissance crystal water goblets featuring the pattern's distinctive hand-cut leaf design and elegant paneled stems. Choose a SINGLE GOBLET, SET OF 2 or SET OF 4 from the variation menu.

These clear crystal goblets are from Carico's discontinued Renaissance pattern, identified by its repeating elongated leaf cuts above a band of smaller intersecting leaf-shaped cuts around the lower bowl. A faceted, paneled stem completes the design and gives the glasses a beautiful sparkle in the light.

Carico describes its crystal as handcrafted, mouth blown and hand cut by skilled craftsmen. The Renaissance pattern is identified in replacement references as a cut-bowl design with a paneled stem.

These are the WATER GOBLETS in the Renaissance stemware line rather than the smaller wine glass. At 7 1/8" tall, they match the documented size of the Renaissance water goblet.

A wonderful opportunity for someone replacing a missing or damaged Carico Renaissance glass, adding additional stems to an existing service, or putting together a matching set of vintage crystal for entertaining.
