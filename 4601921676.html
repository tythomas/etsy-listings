Description

Vintage Northmore 7-light indoor Christmas light string in its original red and green Santa Claus illustrated cardboard box, believed circa 1950s. One complete boxed strand per purchase, with seven original multicolored incandescent bulbs plus three additional vintage replacement bulbs (ten bulbs total).

Each strand has seven original flame-shaped bulbs in assorted red, blue, green, orange and white. Each includes at least one of every color, but the number and arrangement of colors vary from set to set. Twisted red-and-green wiring and black sockets. All seven bulbs in each set illuminated when tested at the time of listing. Incandescent bulbs become warm in use.

Each box measures -  11.75"  x 5.5" x 1.5" 

Four boxed sets are available and are sold individually, NOT as a lot of four. The best-preserved box will be shipped first. Original Santa and holly box graphics are especially charming for vintage holiday displays.

HISTORY AND MARKINGS: Northmore is printed on the illustrated boxes. Two box backs identify Raylite Electric Corp., 305–315 Rider Avenue, Bronx 51, N.Y.; three strands retain round Paramount No. 109 UL tags. Manufacturer markings differ among sets. A precise production year and the relationship between these labels have not been independently verified. Dating to circa 1950s is an estimate based on packaging and construction.

CONDITION: The four original cardboard boxes vary in condition, with edge wear, creases, tears and missing or damaged cellophane windows; none has intact original cellophane. One box has previously torn cardboard portions glued back into place, as pictured. A couple of bulbs show age-related crackling in the colored surface coating; this is not represented as cracked glass. Please see photos for examples of box and bulb condition. Exact box, bulb sequence, and markings received will vary.

BULBS AND SAFETY: All seven original bulbs illuminated during testing at the time of listing, but because these are fragile vintage incandescent bulbs, bulb functionality upon delivery and continued operation cannot be guaranteed. Two additional vintage replacement bulbs are included with each box at no extra charge; their colors vary and specific colors cannot be selected or guaranteed. Vintage wiring has not been professionally inspected or certified for electrical safety. Recommended as a collectible/display piece unless inspected by a qualified electrician. Bulbs should not be left operating unattended.
