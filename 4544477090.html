Description

A 1930s hand-pieced Pinwheel quilt in rose pink, teal, and red print cottons set on a crisp white ground, hand quilted throughout in a fine clamshell design.

The pinwheel blocks are built from dress and feed sack cottons of the period — small florals, checks, and novelty prints in pink, teal, red, and blue — each one framed by wide white sashing that lets the color do the work. The whole white ground is covered in closely worked clamshell hand quilting, stitched by hand in small even runs. It is the kind of quilting that took a winter to finish and still reads beautifully in raking light.

Dating comes from the fabrics themselves: the small-scale novelty prints, the pink and teal palette, and the feed sack cottons all place it in the 1930s to early 1940s. The piecing and the quilting are both done by hand.

This size works as a twin coverlet, a generous throw over a sofa or chair, or hung as a textile panel.
