NOTE- This is a recollection piece from Indiana Glass manufactured a line in the 70's & 80's that was called Recollection.  This collection featured pieces that replicated the early Federal Glass Madrid depression era pieces. 

ALL pieces are in VERY GOOD vintage condition - no chips or cracks, glass is bright with no clouding. Small air bubbles are noted on a few of the pieces - edges are smooth and free from nicking - edges of pieces are scalloped. 

Rims of the pieces are thick and have a beautiful scallop shape. Pieces have a raised embossed scroll design. 

Gorgeous dinner plate, soup bowl  & cup / saucer…. great addition for anyone who has a collection of Madrid pieces !
