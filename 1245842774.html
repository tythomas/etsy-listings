Description

Vintage Jaska of California Cascade Ware nine-piece turquoise-and-brown pottery set with a 112-ounce pitcher, seven tumblers in two sizes, and a 14¼-inch round platter.

A striking matched collection of postwar California pottery, with turquoise blue glaze and contrasting brown drip-style accents. The generously sized pitcher, coordinating tumblers, and round platter make an appealing mid-century display together.

INCLUDED — 9 PIECES TOTAL
• 1 large pitcher — approximately 112 fl oz capacity
• 7 ceramic tumblers — in two sizes
• 1 round platter — approximately 14¼ inches across

Sold together as one nine-piece set, not individually.

MAKER & APPROXIMATE AGE
Jaska of California, associated with Los Angeles pottery. Contemporary advertising documented the Cascade Ware line by 1946, and a 1947 advertisement promoted the line as handmade open-stock dinnerware. The set is dated approximately to the late 1940s–1950s; an exact year of manufacture cannot be confirmed.

CONDITION
This is vintage pottery with glaze crazing and visible fine crack lines. As disclosed in the earlier listing, some of the lines appear to extend into the pottery body rather than being limited to the surface glaze, and become more apparent when wet. Please examine the listing photographs for the glaze and condition details.

Because of these condition issues, the set is offered primarily for collecting and decorative display. It is not represented as watertight or suitable for serving food and beverages. All nine pieces described above are included.
