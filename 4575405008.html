Description

Colony Color Crown Blue pressed-glass water goblets, approx. 5 3/4" tall with a 7-ounce capacity.

This matching water goblet set is strongly attributed to Colony Glass's discontinued Color Crown Blue pattern, also collected under the King's Crown and Thumbprint names. 
Manufactured from 1976–1980 and are discontinued; an independent 1976 trade reference documents the introduction of this blue color during the Bicentennial period.

The turquoise blue pressed glass features flared rims, rounded bowls, a horizontal oval thumbprint pattern, short ornamental molded stems, and round feet. Each footed water glass has the saturated color and distinctive pressed glass design that works beautifully for a coordinated table, bar cart, or replacement stemware collection.
