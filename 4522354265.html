Description

Hand quilted Dresden Plate quilt featuring colorful vintage cotton scrap fabrics, black blanket-stitch appliqué, and bright yellow and white setting blocks.

This vintage Dresden Plate quilt measures approximately 65" x 86" and was handmade using a wide assortment of vintage cotton scrap fabrics. The quilt features colorful floral prints, ginghams, novelty prints, and feedsack-era fabrics arranged into traditional Dresden Plate blocks set against cheerful yellow and white backgrounds.

Each Dresden Plate blade is finished with a distinctive black blanket stitch that outlines the individual fabrics and creates a striking visual contrast. The quilt is hand quilted throughout with floral and geometric quilting patterns visible across both the front and back.
