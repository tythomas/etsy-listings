Description

This beauty is straight out of a 100+ year old cow barn that we tore down.  Its worn edges tell the story of MANY bales of hay being lowered from the loft to the cattle below. The groove portion of the wood is smooth. Some cracks can be seen in the wood, but none go all the way through the piece. The metal shows rust and some corrosion ( see photos) 
Piece is  solid and secure - wooden pulley rotates freely on the metal piece

The wooden pulley measures approximately 5 3/4" in diameter and the metal portion measures approximately 7" long ( including the top ring).  The diameter of the top ring is 2 1/4" . 

Rustic, primitive and industrial!  Great for display or to be repurposed  ~
