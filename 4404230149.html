Description

A beautiful 1930s Luce travel suitcase in warm butterscotch tones with brass hardware and gingham lining, perfect for décor or photo styling. * NOTE- items are not included in the listing- this listing is for luggage only

This vintage Luce suitcase dates to the late 1930s–1940s and features its original brass nameplate, stitched leather trim, classic micro-gingham interior lining, and working latches. The warm caramel exterior and aged brass patina give it the perfect farmhouse, cottage, or vintage-decor look. This piece shows authentic wear throughout the corners, edges, and binding, consistent with age, but remains structurally sound and excellent for display, prop work, or storing lightweight linens or keepsakes.
