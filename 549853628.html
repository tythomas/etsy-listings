Full Description:
Beautiful vintage pressed-glass goblets from the Park Lane pattern by Indiana Glass, produced in the mid-1960s. These elegant stemmed goblets feature the classic Park Lane hallmarks: a square radiating foot, a single faceted stem ball, elongated diamond panels, and a crosshatch band around the upper bowl.

These are frequently misidentified online as “Cape Cod” due to surface pattern similarities, but the square foot and stem design confirm the correct pattern as Park Lane. This set of four is bright, clear, and excellent for table settings, vintage barware displays, or mid-century stemware collections.

Each goblet holds approximately 8 ounces and stands just over five inches tall, making them perfect as water goblets, small wine glasses, or specialty cocktail service.
