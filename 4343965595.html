Description

Listing is for ONE gorgeous Redwing Sponge band Cookie Jar from Redwing's Gray line kitchenware that was introduced in the late 1920's

This cookie jar is almost 100 years old and is in EXCELLENT vintage condition ~ no chips or cracks (no hairline or other cracking). Glaze is intact and shows no visible crackling. There is a nice shine on the glaze still. Lid shows no chipping or rough spots. Some pin point holes are noted in the pottery on the lid top, outside and inside the piece - this is from the firing process. The outer band has an area where a glaze bubble popped during firing leaving an open hole to the raw pottery - see photos.  A tiny spot has a raised piece of pottery that was not smoothed prior to firing. It presents as a tiny raised bump ( near the front left side of the jar). There is one small darkened spot on the inside of the jar - see photos.  Lid has one area where the rust paint is outside the band -see photo grid. The hand painted cursive wording "COOKIES" is legible and show no in consistencies. Lid fits securely.

FUN FACTS: 
The Red Wing Union Stoneware Company was formed in 1906 when Red Wing Stoneware Company merged with the Minnesota Stoneware Company. The Red Wing Stoneware Company originally started in Red Wing, Minnesota, in 1861 by German immigrant John Paul. The name changed again in 1936 when the name "Red Wing Potteries, Inc." was finally decided upon. Red Wing Potteries, Inc. operated until 1967. The name was changed again in 1996 this time to "Red Wing Pottery" as the company was reborn and then closed again in 2019.
