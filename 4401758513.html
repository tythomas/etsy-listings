Gorgeous hand-blown ruby red optic goblets with rich mid-century color. Heavy, vibrant, and full of studio-made character.

DESCRIPTION:  
These vintage ruby red goblets are hand-blown mid-century pieces featuring bold optic ribbing, a thick rounded foot, and a sculpted stacked stem. Made in the Blenko/Kanawha tradition of West Virginia studio glass, each goblet is wonderfully heavy with the deep gold-ruby color associated with true mid-century ruby production. These display beautifully on a holiday table, bar cart, open shelving, or as year-round statement glassware.

As is typical with authentic hand-blown ruby glass, each goblet shows subtle variations in tone, depth, and clarity of color. One goblet has a visible internal ruby streak when held to strong light. These are natural results of the glassmaking process and are not flaws; they simply highlight the individuality of studio-made glass. All pieces are free of chips, cracks, or repairs.
