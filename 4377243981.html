Description

Listing cost is for one gorgeous antique pink and white hand stitched quilt in cotton fabrics estimated to be from the early 20's - 30's

Measures approximately : 86" x 75" 

The quilt is hand pieced and hand quilted. It is beautifully and expertly quilted throughout with tiny, even diagonal stitches, all done by hand.

Each 12" x 12" block features a small pink pinwheel pattern inside a white Mayflower pattern with pink background. There are 30 of these large blocks framed in a 2" white fabric border and finished with a narrow white binding that has a subtle floral patten. 

This lightweight quilt measures approximately 86" long and 75" wide. Quilt is thin, with no batting in between the top and bottom (that I can tell). It appears  thin in spots on the back. It is in nice antique condition, with the exception of one very small yellowed spot. Some slight fading of the pink pinwheel design on is noted in places. There are no large tears or rips visible, but 4 blocks have fabric that is thinning and shows wear - see photo grid. Quilt has no strong odors & comes from a smoke free home.  The piece has been hand washed and sun dried.  Coloring was hard to capture in photos - it is more of a soft muted pink than a vivid pink coloring.

I am happy to answer any questions about the quilt and to send additional photos upon request. :)
