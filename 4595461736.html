Description

Antique Lancaster Glass covered biscuit jar in the No. 917 Twin Crescents pattern, featuring bold swirling pinwheel medallions, diamond-textured panels, a decorative matching lid, and a subtle lavender tint. approx. 9" tall with lid in place.

This substantial early American pressed glass jar is attributed to Lancaster Glass Company’s No. 917 pattern, commonly called Twin Crescents, documented around 1910. The glass appears predominantly clear with a faint lavender/amethyst cast, especially in thicker areas. This coloration may be consistent with sunlight-related solarization of manganese-containing glass, but its cause has not been independently verified.
