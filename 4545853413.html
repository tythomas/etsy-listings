Description

Six pink Depression glass sherbets in Jeannette Glass Company's Cube pattern, each 3 1/2 inches across the rim and standing 2 3/4 inches tall.

Jeannette made Cube between 1929 and 1933, when a pressed pattern that threw light like cut crystal could be sold for pennies at the five-and-dime. The tumbling-block facets run the whole lower bowl and down over the foot, and the soft pink glass turns warm rather than cold in the sun.

The pattern is pressed, not cut - you can see it in the rounded edges of each facet and the faint mold line down the bowl. Above the cubes the rim is left plain and smooth, and the base underside carries a radiating star. There is no maker's mark on any of the six, and none is expected: Jeannette did not mark this pattern, so it is identified by form and pattern alone.

MAKER: Jeannette Glass Company, Jeannette, Pennsylvania
