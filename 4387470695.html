Description

Listing cost is for TWO Antique Indiana Glass “Juno” Double Pinwheel Goblets (circa 1910) — a stunning pair of authentic Early American Pattern Glass (EAPG) stemware pieces.

These vintage goblets feature a deeply pressed pinwheel and starburst pattern that catches and reflects light beautifully. The base displays a radiant sunburst motif, a signature of early 20th-century pressed glass craftsmanship.

Produced by the Indiana Glass Company in Dunkirk, Indiana, the “Juno” pattern was introduced around 1910, during the transitional era from hand-pressed to machine-pressed glassmaking. Each goblet shows faint mold seams and small air bubbles — authentic signs of early manufacturing and proof that these are true antique originals, not later reproductions.
