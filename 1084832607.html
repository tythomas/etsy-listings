Pieces vary by maker, pattern, age, size, weight, and capacity. Approximate measurements and known pattern information are listed by option below. Most glasses range from about 3 1/2" to 8 1/4" tall and hold approximately 4 to 16 ounces, depending on the style.

Condition:
Each piece is in very good vintage condition unless noted in the individual option description. No chips or cracks unless specifically disclosed. As expected with vintage glass, pieces may show small manufacturing bubbles, mold marks, light surface wear, or minor signs of age and use.

Please review photos and option descriptions carefully before selecting. If more than one of the same option is available, the best condition piece ships first.

Care:
Hand washing is recommended to preserve the clarity and finish of vintage glassware.
