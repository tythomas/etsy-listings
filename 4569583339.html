Description

One vintage 14" Theodore Haviland Luanda oval serving platter in white china with blue leaf decoration and a dark silver-tone inner line.

The backstamp identifies the Luanda pattern by Theodore Haviland New York and states “Made in America.” The Haviland Collectors Internationale Foundation dates this American mark to 1937–1956, placing the platter most plausibly circa the 1940s.

Measurements: 
14" x 10 3/4" x 1 1/4" tall 

The glazed porcelain china has a white ground, a blue leafy branch at the center, scattered blue leaves around the rim, and a narrow dark silver-tone line. Its restrained botanical design works naturally for replacement service, family meals, or holiday serving.

This listing is for one oval 14" serving platter.
