Description

Beautiful set of ELEVEN vintage Homer Laughlin Eggshell Georgian bread and butter plates in the Cashmere pattern.

Cashmere features delicate sprays of pink, blue and yellow flowers accented with gold trim, a thin gold circle near the center and a softly colored pale yellow/ivory border surrounding the lighter center.

A lovely set for collectors of Homer Laughlin dinnerware, replacement pieces for an existing Cashmere service, or for creating a vintage floral table setting.

DETAILS:

• Maker: Homer Laughlin China Company
• Line/Shape: Eggshell Georgian
• Pattern: Cashmere
• Quantity: 11 plates
• Size: Approximately 6 1/4" diameter each
• Type: Bread & butter / dessert / side plates
• Made in USA
• Vintage early 1950s

The photographed backstamp reads E51N6, a Homer Laughlin production code dating that plate to May 1951 at the Newell, West Virginia plant.
