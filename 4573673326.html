Description

1930s Akro Agate miniature nesting mixing bowls in your choice of cobalt blue or green. Each color is a complete graduated set of four child-size pressed-glass bowls, made as miniature play kitchen / housekeeping glassware. Sold by the four-piece color set.

The bowls have a fluted, paneled exterior with a substantial rounded rim and nest neatly inside one another. Their tiny scale is intentional: these were made as children’s toy dishes rather than full-size kitchen mixing bowls.

MEASUREMENTS — EACH 4-PIECE SET

• Next: 3 5/8 in diameter × 1 3/8 in high
• Next: 3 in diameter × 1 1/4 in high

VARIATIONS
• Cobalt Blue — complete set of 4
• Green — complete set of 4

The green set has been tested under black light and does not glow; it is not being represented as uranium glass. No maker marks are present.

Small air bubbles, flow/striations and other slight irregularities can be seen in the glass and are consistent with the mold-pressed manufacturing process. Please review all photographs closely for the condition and individual characteristics of each vintage set.

A scarce miniature form for an Akro Agate or vintage children’s glass collection, and especially appealing displayed nested or graduated together.
