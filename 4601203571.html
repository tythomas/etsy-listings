Description

Vintage Spode blue and white transferware plates, offered individually in three collectible patterns. Choose one plate from the variation menu.

OPTIONS & MEASUREMENTS
•  Greek Plate — Blue Room Collection, Greek pattern; octagonal, 9 3/4" across. 
•  Indian Sporting — Engravers’ Archive Collection, Indian Sporting pattern; round, 10 1/4" diameter. 
•  Italian Church — Engravers’ Archive Collection, Italian Church pattern; round, 10 1/4" diameter.

Each option purchases ONE plate, not the set of three. One of each is available.

The backs identify the historic designs as first introduced circa 1806 (Greek), circa 1815 (Indian Sporting), and circa 1820 (Italian Church). These are design-origin dates, NOT the manufacturing dates of the plates offered here. The backs also describe the imagery as underglaze prints from hand-engraved copper plates. Exact manufacturing dates have not been established.

CONDITION
All three have no chips, cracks, or glaze crazing. Edges are free of nicks and rough spots, and the glaze retains a nice shine. Vintage items may show minor signs of handling or age; please review the photographs.

Beautiful for blue-and-white displays, gallery walls, collections, or replacing a favorite Spode plate. Photographs show the three available patterns and their backstamps.
