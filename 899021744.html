Circa  1923 - 1933

USED vintage condition - metal wiring shows light wear with some corrosion and rust noted, but is functional and holds the lids securely onto the jars.  Note there are no lids with these jars Glass is clean and free from cracks or chipping. 
The remaining jars vary in coloring - one has light clouding. Small air bubbles in the glass of jars; this is very common with the glass used for older mason jars. Jars have mold seams and straw marks present in glass. Straw marks are dents and mold marks in the glass and are the result of the manufacturing process and are not post-production damage. The underside of the jars have a ringed base and are marked with a number in the center of the jar base. ( # 5, 10) 

So many possibilities for this beautiful piece ~  add to a collection of canning jars, use as a floral vase for fresh cut flowers or simply make it functional handout in it the bathroom for storage ~  keep your cotton balls or Q tips in it!

NOTE - Jars are intended for display only! Please do not can food in these jars. Canning food in vintage jars may cause jars to shatter and could cause injury or damage.
