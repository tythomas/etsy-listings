Description

Large vintage 1970s hand-crocheted chevron afghan in chocolate brown, tan, cream, green, burgundy and red. Approx. 76" x 54".

This large vintage crochet afghan is a wonderful example of the warm, bold color combinations associated with 1970s handmade textiles. Alternating chevron and ripple-style bands create a graphic zigzag design in chocolate brown, warm tan and beige, cream, shades of green, burgundy and bright red.

At approximately 76" long x 54" wide, this is a generously sized vintage afghan that works beautifully across the back of a sofa, layered over a chair, across the foot of a bed or simply used as a cozy throw.

The mix of earthy neutrals with deep green and pops of red gives it a distinctive retro look without being limited to fall decorating. It would work especially well with 1970s, mid-century, granny chic, cabin, cottage and collected vintage interiors.

Good vintage preowned condition with no stains, tears or significant damage noted.
