Description

Vintage International Silver Company silverplate coffee pot marked I.S. Co. on one foot.

This beautiful old coffee server has an especially striking silhouette, with a full pear-shaped body, tall sweeping spout, ornate openwork handle, four decorative feet, hinged domed lid and sculptural finial. Raised scroll and foliate details decorate the handle, spout and base.

International Silver Company was formed in Meriden, Connecticut in 1898 and became one of America's major silverware manufacturers. This piece is marked I.S. Co. and is silverplate rather than sterling silver.

The elaborate traditional design has strong Victorian and Rococo Revival influence. I have not found sufficient documentation to confidently assign an exact International Silver pattern name, so no specific pattern attribution is being made.

CONDITION
Vintage condition with substantial age-related patina, tarnish, surface wear, scratching and areas of uneven coloration/plating wear. The lower portion of the body shows particularly noticeable mottling and wear. There is a visible dent near the bottom footing - pictured.  
Please look closely at the photographs, which are part of the condition description.

The pot has intentionally not been heavily polished. Its aged surface has been left intact rather than risk further wear to the remaining silverplate.

The hinged lid, ornate feet, graceful spout and dramatic darkened silver finish give this piece wonderful character as vintage display decor. Beautiful styled with antique books, on a sideboard or buffet, in an old-world kitchen, or incorporated into Victorian Revival, traditional or dark-academia decor.
