Description

Blue Ridge hand painted plates by Southern Potteries — six different 1940s florals, sold one pattern at a time, from 6 1/4" bread and butter up to a 9 1/4" luncheon plate.  Most, not all, are marked on the back Blue Ridge, Hand Painted, Underglaze, Southern Potteries, Inc., Made in U.S.A.

Choose your pattern from the drop-down menu:
• Option A — blue single blossom, green leaves, fluted Colonial rim, 9 1/4" luncheon plate
• Option B — two maroon tulips with maroon rim bands, beaded Candlewick rim, 7" salad plate
• Option C — pink and yellow roses with pale blue feathering, fluted Colonial rim, 6 1/4"
• Option D — marigold orange blossom with red vein strokes, fluted Colonial rim, 6 1/4"
• Option E — cluster of pale yellow buds, fluted Colonial rim, 6 1/4"
• Option F — yellow daisy with a brown stippled center, fluted Colonial rim, 6 1/4"

Options can be combined in one order. Best condition ships first with options have have multiples.

Measurements:
• Option A: 9 1/4" across
• Option B: 7" across
• Options C, D, E and F: 6 1/4" across

Hand wash only.
