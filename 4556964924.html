Description

8 pieces of Antique Homer Laughlin flow blue dishes — cobalt rims with rust and blue florals and gold trim, about 1900. The dinner plate and oval platter carry Homer Laughlin's eagle backstamp with the Colonial shape name; the round platter is marked American Beauty.

Included in the listing pricing:
• 1 Oval platter — 13.5" x 9.5"
• 1 Round platter — 11 1/8"
• 1 Dinner plate — 10 1/8"
• 6 Luncheon plates — 9 1/8"

Gently hand washed and air dried. Hand wash only.

CONDITION
• Crazing throughout, front and back — the fine web of lines in the glaze that semi-vitreous china of this age almost always has. Pictured.
• The gold rim line is worn and broken in places
• Brown discoloration and grey staining in the wells of the luncheon plates, pictured
• Fine utensil scratching across the plate wells
• Small dark specks in the glaze are original firing flecks, not damage
• No chips, cracks or hairlines
• No repairs
• A piece of tape with owners name is still stuck to one plate back 
• Because of the crazing these are for display and serving, not everyday food use
