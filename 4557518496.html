Description

Antique Tirschenreuth dinner plates from Bavaria — pale yellow rim, heavy gold filigree, floral center, 10 3/4". Each plate is marked on the back with the green crowned PT shield, BAVARIA TIRSCHENREUTH, and a red DECORATED IN OWN STUDIOS stamp, so this is a studio-decorated blank rather than a named pattern.

Choose your set:

Measurements:

MAKER: Tirschenreuth (PT), Bavaria, Germany
DECORATION: transfer-printed and burnished gold filigree in two bands on a pale yellow ground, with a rose-filled basket motif repeating around the rim
CENTER: transfer floral bouquet, ringed in gold
CIRCA: 1930s
