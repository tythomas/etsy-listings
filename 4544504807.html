Description

A classic vintage picnic hamper in the traditional suitcase style, hand woven from golden willow with a fitted lid, a twisted willow carry handle, and twin leather straps closing on solid brass swing clasps, measuring approximately 18 inches wide.

It has a full hinged lid rather than a split top, a heavy rope twist trim around the rim and base, and a warm honey color to the willow that sits well against white or painted furniture. Closed, it works as year round storage that does not look like storage.

The hardware is what dates it. The lid tabs are set with aluminum pop rivets, the hinge plates are stamped steel, and the lid stays are plastic coated wire, all of which place this in the later twentieth century rather than the prewar era, most likely the 1970s or 1980s. The weaving itself is done the old way, with a woven willow base, paired rod uprights, and hand twisted rope borders. There is no maker's mark, label, or stamp anywhere on the basket, inside or out, so it is offered simply as an unmarked vintage willow hamper.
