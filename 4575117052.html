Description

Two matching Jeannette Windsor Pink pressed-glass pieces—a footed creamer and two-handled open sugar bowl ~ Sold in a pair 

Attributed to Jeannette Glass Company’s Windsor Pink pattern, produced from 1936–1946. Both pieces share the pattern’s transparent rose-pink glass, molded diamond texture, angular handles, faceted pedestal stems, and square sunburst feet.

These pieces of depression glassware and pink glassware include a footed creamer and sugar bowl. The open sugar bowl is a two-handled pressed glass bowl. Collectors seeking 1940s glassware may use either piece as replacement glass for a Windsor collection.

The geometric texture and warm pink color make these useful replacement pieces and an inviting addition to a coffee, tea, brunch, or dessert table.
