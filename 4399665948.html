Description

Vintage Midwinter Rural England blue transferware, circa 1935–1948. Multiple countryside scenes. Sold individually by size. No large chips or cracks.

Beautiful vintage Rural England blue transferware by Midwinter Ltd., Burslem, England, produced circa 1935–1948.
Creamy ironstone bodies feature richly detailed cobalt blue scenes framed by ornate floral borders — a classic English country style popular during the interwar and wartime years.

🌿 Pattern Details:
This pattern includes several different countryside scenes, which vary from piece to piece:
~ Village Bridge Scene – stone bridge, river, cottages
~ Boating Scene – rowers on a river below a domed church
~ Watermill Scene – mill beside a stream, trees in background
~ Farmstead Scene – farmer with animals near a rustic homestead


All pieces share the same floral border, so the set displays beautifully together.

🏺 Maker & Date
~ Maker: Midwinter Ltd.
~ Location: Burslem, England
~ Pattern: Rural England
~ Date Range: c. 1935–1948
~ Backstamp: Crown mark “Midwinter Ltd Burslem England” (used before “Made in England” was added in the 1950s)
~ Material: Ironstone-type earthenware, cobalt blue underglaze transfer

Collector’s Note: The mark used on these pieces predates “Made in England,” confirming true prewar to immediate postwar production.

✨ Condition
All pieces are free of large chips and cracks.
Some pieces show age-related glaze crackling, most noticeable on the backs of certain plates, some on the front. Salad plates have visible darkened crackling on front and back. 
A few areas show darkening in the crazing where the glaze reaches the clay body  — typical patina for prewar English ironstone. Some slight nicking is noted on the underside of a few pieces.  Rich blue transfer remains clear and vibrant.
