Description

Antique yellow ware 7" mixing bowl with a wide brown slip band and narrow pinstripes, molded in an embossed arch and fan pattern.

The base is plain glazed yellow ware with no maker's mark or size number — common on kitchen bowls of this type, which were sold unmarked by several American potteries.

MAKER — unmarked
PATTERN — embossed arch and fan relief, wide brown slip band with pinstripes above and below
CIRCA — early to mid 20th century

Measurements:
• Diameter across the rim 7"
• Height: 4 1/4"

Hand wash only.

CONDITION
• Glaze flake below the rim, about 3/4" across, showing the buff clay underneath — pictured
• A smaller glaze nick in the arch pattern just below it
• The top edge of the rim is dry and darkened all the way around
• Small dark pit in the glaze on the inside wall
• Allover fine crazing, inside and out, with light brown staining in the lines
