Description

Vintage Morgantown Glassware Guild Crinkle #1962 glasses in transparent pink, offered by size and quantity. Choose short tumblers, tall 10 oz tumblers, or footed sherbet glasses.

Morgantown's Crinkle pattern is known for its wonderfully irregular, hand-worked appearance. The textured surfaces, free-form uneven rims, and slight differences in shape from glass to glass are characteristic of the pattern and manufacturing process rather than damage.

AVAILABLE SIZES
• Short Tumbler – approx. 3 1/4" tall × 2 3/4" diameter; approx. 6 oz
• Tall Tumbler – approx. 5 1/8" tall × 2 3/4" diameter; approx. 10 oz
• Sherbet – : approx. 3" tall × 3 1/2" diameter with 2 3/4" base; approx. 6 oz


MAKER & PATTERN
Morgantown Glass Works / Morgantown Glassware Guild
Pattern: Crinkle #1962
Color: transparent pink
Circa: late 1930s to early 1960s

Crinkle #1962 is closely related to Morgantown's earlier El Mexicano design. The #1962 designation is the pattern/design number and should not be read as the year of manufacture.

CONDITION
All pieces are clean and free of chips, cracks, and other damage. Only normal light vintage wear is present. Because of the intentionally free-form construction, individual glasses vary slightly in rim shape, texture, and dimensions.
