Description

Antique Wooden Butter Mold with Wheat Sheaf Stamp | Large 19th Century Plunger Press

Antique wooden plunger-style butter mold dating to the 19th century, likely the late 1800s. This large lathe-turned wooden mold includes its plunger and a deeply carved recessed wheat sheaf stamp.

The decorative disc at the base of the plunger features a detailed wheat sheaf surrounded by carved accents. When pressed into butter, the recessed design would have created a raised decorative wheat motif.

A wonderful piece of early kitchen and dairy history with the warm patina and character that make antique wooden kitchen tools so appealing for primitive and country displays.

MEASUREMENTS
• 8" tall with plunger extended
• 6" tall with plunger lowered
• 4 1/2" across outside edges of opening
• Carved stamp measures 3 3/8" in diameter
• Stamp is approximately 1" thick

CONDITION
Good antique display condition with age-appropriate wear and patina.

A long crack runs down one side of the wooden mold. The area remains solid and stable. It is possible that an older repair was made to this area, although I cannot confirm that.

Additional age-related checking/fissures are visible around the upper portion of the mold. The carved wheat stamp itself is in very good condition with no visible cracks or significant damage.

The wood shows expected darkening, variation, surface wear and signs of use consistent with an antique piece of this age.
