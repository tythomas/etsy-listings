Description

Antique Periwinkle / Hummingbird four-point star quilt, 74 x 64", featuring colorful patchwork, a creamy white background, and dense hand quilting.

A charming vintage patchwork quilt with colorful four-pointed stars nestled between generous cream-colored, rounded white shapes. Its closely spaced hand quilting creates a wonderfully textured surface, and the mix of tiny florals, plaids, dots, stripes, and geometric prints brings a cheerful, timeworn farmhouse character.

The design is most consistent with the historical Periwinkle / Hummingbird four-point star pattern family, also associated with names such as Kite. The exact historical variant is not definitively verified. Based on the fabrics and construction, we estimate this quilt dates to approximately the 1930s–1940s.
