Description

A pair of Washington Colonial dessert plates, 7 1/4 inches across, with a 22K gold spatter rim and a rose bouquet at the center. Two plates, sold together.

Washington Colonial was Vogue's embossed plate shape, made in the USA in the 1940s and decorated in dozens of variations. This is one of the gold ones: the raised lattice rim was flooded with gold spatter so the white relief shows through in a mottled, lacy way. That mottling is the decoration, not wear.

The back is stamped WASHINGTON COLONIAL / MADE IN U.S.A. in green, which names the shape line rather than the decoration number. Inside the spatter rim sit two finer gold bands, a hanging fern-and-bead swag and a narrow palmette ribbon. The center bouquet of pink and yellow roses is identical on both plates, in the same position, so it is a transfer print rather than hand painting.
