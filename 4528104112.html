Description

Antique McKee Ray EAPG cake stand with a dramatic swirl optic pattern, beaded rim, and tall pedestal. Measures 9" across and 7 3/8" tall.

A beautiful example of Early American Pattern Glass, this antique McKee Brothers cake stand is recognized by collectors as the **Ray** pattern, also commonly referred to as **Ball and Swirl**. The elegant optic swirl design flows continuously through the plate, twisted pedestal, and matching foot, while the distinctive ball edge frames both the top and base.

This substantial pedestal stand is ideal for displaying cakes, pastries, candles, seasonal décor, or as a centerpiece on a dining table or buffet.

Measurements:

* 7 3/8" tall

Condition:
* Very good antique condition.
* No chips, cracks, or repairs found.
* Normal manufacturing characteristics of antique pressed glass are present, including scattered seed bubbles and mold seams from production. These are original to the piece and typical of late 19th-century glassmaking.

Pattern: Ray (also known as Ball and Swirl)
Maker: McKee Brothers Glass Works
Circa: 1894–early 1900s
Material: Pressed clear glass (EAPG)

This striking Victorian-era cake stand is both highly decorative and functional, making it a wonderful addition to an Early American Pattern Glass collection or a beautiful display piece for entertaining.
