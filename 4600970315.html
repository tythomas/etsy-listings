Description

Vintage Myott-Meakin Tonquin Blue transferware dinnerware, made in Staffordshire, England. Choose from four variations: individual bowls, plates, complete cup-and-saucer sets, or extra individual saucers. Multiple pieces are available.

The classic blue-and-white Tonquin design features a scenic country-house landscape with a richly detailed floral border. These pieces bear the combined Myott-Meakin Staffordshire, Made in England backstamp, rather than the earlier Myott-only mark—an important distinction for collectors and replacement buyers. The combined name follows the companies’ 1976 merger. Circa 1980s is a working manufacturing estimate, not an exact documented production date; the Tonquin design itself is older.

AVAILABLE  PIECES—
• Cereal Bowl — 6 5/8" diameter x 1 3/4" tall.
• Dessert Plate — 7" diameter.
• Cup & Saucer — one handled cup (2 5/8" tall x 3 5/8" diameter at top x 1 3/4" diameter at base) together with one matching saucer (5 7/8" diameter).
• Extra Saucer — one saucer, 5 7/8" diameter.

CONDITION: All pieces are free of chips, cracks, crazing, and discoloration. These are vintage dishes; minor manufacturing differences may be present.
