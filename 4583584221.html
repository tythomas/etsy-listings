Description

Antique pink and white scrappy Nine Patch variation quilt, hand quilted and dating approximately 1935–1945. Measuring 84” x 84”, this striking square quilt combines a large rose-pink ground with white setting areas and dozens of colorful period cotton prints.

The pieced blocks are arranged on point and incorporate florals, dots, checks, geometrics and other Depression-era dress-print and feedsack-type cottons. The varied scraps and repeated fabrics give each block its own character while the expansive pink setting creates a bold, graphic overall design.

The quilt is hand quilted throughout. Decorative geometric and curving quilting fills the large pink areas, white sections and border, and the stitching is especially visible from the plain white cotton back. Large pink sections were pieced from narrower lengths of fabric as part of the original construction. A narrow light-colored binding finishes the edges.

DATE
Circa 1935–1945. The dating is based on the assortment of period cotton prints, color palette, scrap construction, setting and hand-quilting style. Some prints are consistent with 1930s–1940s feedsack and dress-print cottons; individual fabrics have not been authenticated as specific feed sacks.

MEASUREMENTS
Approximately 84” x 84”.

MATERIALS & CONSTRUCTION
• Cotton quilt top and backing
• Scrappy Nine Patch variation / Nine Patch on point design
• Hand quilted
• Rose pink, white and multicolor printed cottons
• Plain white cotton backing
• Narrow light-colored binding

CONDITION
A beautiful antique quilt with strong color and an overall sound appearance for its age. There are scattered age/storage marks and discoloration, including yellow-brown staining on some white areas, faint pinkish color transfer/discoloration on portions of the white backing, and small scattered spots. Please review the photo grid with close-up photographs for these areas. No fabric deterioration, no holes, no open seams or extensive binding loss were observed in the photographs.

The long joined areas visible in the pink fabric are original construction seams where narrower lengths of pink cotton were pieced together, not tears.

A visually striking piece of American quiltmaking with an especially appealing rose-pink ground, extensive period scrap fabrics and decorative hand quilting. Its 84-inch square size gives it wonderful presence displayed on a bed, folded over a bench or used as a textile focal point.
