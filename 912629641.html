Unsure of exact manufacturing date ~ I believe these small bowls are original pieces and not a 1970s reproduction since there are no markings on this piece.   (The 1970s reproduction pieces have a KW imprinted on the bottom )

Kemple glass was in business from 1945 to 1970 . Wheaton bought this mould afterwards. This original pattern was made in the late 1800's and was used by Phoenix Glass
