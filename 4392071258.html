Description

Charming red and white Churn Dash quilt measuring 62” x 78”. Machine quilted in a figure-eight pattern with cotton muslin backing. One small tear on binding and light pink hue from gentle color bleed. Beautiful as a display or cozy farmhouse accent.

This vintage red and white Churn Dash quilt blends traditional design with 1980s machine craftsmanship. The classic Churn Dash (also known as Monkey Wrench) pattern features bold red piecing against mixed white and cream cotton fabrics — some from the same muslin used on the backing. The quilting is machine done in a continuous figure-eight motif, and the double-stitched binding adds durability and charm.
