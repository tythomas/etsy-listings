Description

A boxed set of six Everbrite steak knives from Italy, each one a single piece of mirror-polished stainless steel, still in the black presentation box they were sold in.

Everbrite was an Italian export cutlery name, and these date to roughly the 1950s or early 1960s — the blades are stamped simply ITALY rather than MADE IN ITALY, which is the earlier form. The lid is lined in cream satin printed with the Everbrite name in gold script, and gold scrollwork frames each corner of the box.

There are no rivets and no separate handle pieces: the handle and blade are one continuous length of steel, tapering from a flat, wide grip to an upswept clip point. The edges are plain rather than serrated, which is why a set like this stays useful — a serrated vintage knife can't really be sharpened again. All six sit in the original rose fabric tray
