Description

Vintage Anchor Hocking Monarch Royal Ruby water goblets in a rich, deep ruby red.

These mid-century goblets feature the distinctive stacked ball stem associated with the Monarch Royal Ruby pattern. The glass is an exceptionally deep red that can appear almost black in lower light and glows a vivid ruby-garnet color when illuminated.

A beautiful choice for a vintage table, holiday place settings, Christmas glassware, replacement stemware, or a collection of classic Anchor Hocking Royal Ruby glass.
