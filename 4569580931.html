Description

Antique A.E. Hull Daisy & Waffle stoneware crock with beautiful blue glaze, raised daisy decoration and molded waffle pattern. Measures approximately 6 1/2" diameter × 4 1/2" tall.

A beautiful piece of early American stoneware, this antique crock is attributed to A.E. Hull Pottery and features the collectible Daisy & Waffle design.

The cylindrical crock is decorated with large raised daisy blossoms against a molded waffle or trellis background, with a distinctive raised diagonal border encircling the top. The glaze is especially appealing, ranging from deep cobalt and periwinkle blue to softer blue-green tones as it settles over the raised decoration.

Research into this distinctive mold identifies it with A.E. Hull Pottery's Daisy & Waffle stoneware. Comparable examples are also described as Daisy & Trellis. The pattern is documented on early-20th-century butter and pastry crocks and related stoneware forms. This example is believed to date to approximately the early 1900s–1920s.

Originally made as a stoneware crock rather than a flower pot, it has a thick pottery body, glazed interior, unglazed tan rim and unglazed clay base. 

Today it makes a striking piece of antique American pottery for display and can also be repurposed as a utensil crock, flower vessel, brush holder, cachepot or decorative container. The rich blue coloring and dimensional floral design display particularly well.
