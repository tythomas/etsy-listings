Description

Vintage Homer Laughlin Eggshell Nautilus N1829 dinnerware set — 24 pieces providing a four-piece place setting for six.

Made by Homer Laughlin in Newell, West Virginia, circa 1952, this discontinued Eggshell Nautilus pattern features graceful blue-gray leaves, tiny white blossoms and a delicate silver-colored rim on an ivory/cream ground.

Replacements identifies the pattern as N1829 — Eggshell Nautilus, Leaf & Tiny Flowers Rim.

24 PIECES / SERVICE FOR 6

• 6 dinner plates — approximately 10" diameter
• 6 fruit/dessert bowls — approximately 5 1/2" diameter
• 6 saucers — approximately 5 3/4" diameter
• 6 footed cups — approximately 2 5/8" tall; 8 oz capacity

This is a particularly pretty mid-century pattern with a soft, understated color palette that works beautifully with vintage, traditional and grandmillennial table settings.

CONDITION
No chips, cracks, discoloration or crazing noted.

The silver-colored trim remains attractive overall with light wear/loss consistent with age and use. All pieces show light surface scratching, with more visible utensil scratching on the dinner plates. Please review the photographs closely for examples of the condition.

Backstamp is shown in the photographs.

Because of the metallic trim, these pieces should not be used in the microwave. Hand washing with mild dish soap is recommended to help preserve the decorative trim.
