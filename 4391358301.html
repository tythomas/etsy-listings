Description

Authentic 1960s Harry C. Hedberg Co. oak veneer dovetail recipe boxes. Choose Smooth Grain, Textured Grain, or Both. Fits 3x5 cards.

Beautiful vintage dovetailed wooden recipe boxes made by the Harry C. Hedberg Company of Marion, Wisconsin (Stock No. 583).
Crafted between the mid-1960s and early 1970s, these pieces feature oak veneer over hardwood, machine-cut dovetail joinery, and brass hinges.
Originally designed for 3x5 recipe or index cards, they now serve beautifully as rustic kitchen storage or mid-century desk organizers.

*The textured grain box with 2 corner veneer chips retains its original Hedberg maker’s label on the base and shows the warm patina that only time can create.

• Dimensions: 8 ⅞″ W × 6″ H × 4 ¾″ D (4 ½″ back / 4″ front slope)
