Description

Antique 19th-century red and green applique quilt in a rose wreath variation with a scalloped swag border. Hand-appliqued and hand-quilted in Turkey red and green on a white background. Measures approximately 82" x 68" inches.

A genuine antique applique quilt that brings authentic 19th-century character to a farmhouse, cottage, cabin, or cottagecore interior. Six rose-wreath medallions of red rosettes and green leaves are framed by an undulating red     (pink hue) and green swag-and-bud border with a gently scalloped edge, a coordinated hand-planned design rather than a simple repeat. Beautiful draped over a quilt rack or blanket ladder, layered at the foot of a bed, hung as textile art, or displayed in a collection of early American quilts.

Dating to approximately 1890 to 1915, this quilt is entirely hand-stitched: needle-turn applique with fine, dense hand-quilting (parallel-line and grid background fill) over a cotton batting. The red is a classic colorfast Turkey red, and the green is a period over dyed green - one leaf has softened to a natural tan, exactly the fading these early greens are known for. These fabrics and techniques are what distinguish a true antique from a later revival quilt.
