Description

Set of 4 Cambridge Caprice Clear Stem 300 low sherbet glasses, each measuring 4 1/4" tall and holding approximately 6 ounces. Made by Cambridge Glass from 1936–1958.

These elegant low sherbets feature the flowing optic drape / swirl design of Cambridge's classic Caprice pattern. Their wide, shallow bowl also makes them beautiful vintage champagne or cocktail coupes.

CONDITION
Excellent vintage condition with no chips or cracks. The glass is bright and clear with no clouding or discoloration. Rims are smooth to the touch and free from rough spots.

Minor manufacturing variations may be present, as expected in vintage glass of this age. Please review all photographs as part of the condition description.

A wonderful replacement set for an existing Cambridge Caprice service, or use these shallow stemmed glasses as champagne coupes, cocktail glasses or elegant dessert sherbets.
