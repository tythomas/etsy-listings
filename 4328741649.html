Description

Set of 4 mismatched vintage clear pressed-glass water/wine goblets, approximately 5 1/2–6 1/2" tall and 8–12 oz each.

Choose from four curated sets of mismatched vintage clear glass goblets. Each variation includes 4 different pressed-glass water/wine goblets with complementary shapes and patterns.

These substantial vintage glasses have the chunky, textured look that works especially well for eclectic table settings, weddings, bridal showers, dinner parties and collected vintage glassware.

SIZE

CONDITION
Very good vintage condition with no chips or cracks. Glass is clear without clouding or heavy water scaling, and rims are smooth without noted nicks or roughness. Light surface scratching may be visible when held to direct light.

Please note that the Whitehall cube tumbler included in Sets 1, 2 and 4 has more visible scratching than the other glasses.

Patterns and makers represented among the available sets include Anchor Hocking Wexford, Indiana Glass Mt. Vernon and Diamond Point, Jeannette Thumbprint, Colony Whitehall, Imperial Cape Cod, Duratuff, Tiara Sandwich and Hazel Atlas Gothic.

Each set is photographed separately—choose Set 1, Set 2, Set 3 or Set 4 from the variation menu.
