Description

Sixteen pieces of J&G Meakin Classic White ironstone — four complete place settings of the ribbed English china that has been turning up on farmhouse tables for sixty years.

Made in Hanley, Staffordshire, this is the all-white swirl pattern Meakin is known for: deep fluting radiating out from a smooth center, finished with a soft scalloped edge. No border, no gold, no pattern. It goes with everything already in your cupboard.

You get four dinner plates, four salad plates, four teacups and four saucers. The backs are marked — the larger plates carry the older ironstone stamp with the J and G monogram, the smaller plates and saucers carry the script Classic White version, a second stamp from a different production run. The glaze is still glossy and the plate wells have none of the grey knife marks that usually give away a set this age.

MAKER: J. & G. Meakin, Hanley, Staffordshire, England
