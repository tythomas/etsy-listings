Description

One vintage Bordallo Pinheiro Leaf with Large Bowl serving platter features a molded green leaf with an integral dip bowl.

This marked Portuguese serving piece is strongly attributed to Bordallo Pinheiro’s Cabbage collection. Its broad veined leaf, raised integral bowl, three-footed base, green glaze, and pale stem details match the factory’s documented form. The piece is provisionally dated circa the 1990s; the exact production year remains uncertain because this long-running design is still produced.

Molded glazed earthenware creates the naturalistic leaf veins and softly scalloped perimeter. Variegated pale-to-medium green glaze is accented by cream-to-pale-beige veins and stem details. As a majolica plate with green majolica character, this green pottery piece brings sculptural interest to vintage serveware and makes an inviting holiday serving dish for appetizers, vegetables, fruit, crackers, garnishes, or a seasonal table.

The listing is for one integral serving piece. The small dip bowl is permanently incorporated into the leaf and is not a separate inventory piece.
