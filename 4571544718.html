Description

Antique wooden ice cream freezer bucket in wonderfully worn teal blue-green paint, with old metal bands, original-style wire bail and turned wooden handle - Circa 1930s .

Measuring approximately 13 1/2 inches tall and 11 inches across the top, this substantial old wood-stave bucket makes a striking primitive farmhouse display piece.

Originally the outer wooden bucket from a hand-crank ice cream freezer, it is now best suited for decorative use. Style it with dried hydrangeas, seasonal branches, kitchen linens or other lightweight decor for a country kitchen, pantry, porch or vintage shop display.

MEASUREMENTS
• Approximately 13 1/2 inches tall, not including the handle
• Approximately 11 inches diameter across the top

CONDITION
Authentic aged condition with extensive wear and loss to the teal paint, scratches, exposed wood, oxidation/rust and patina to the metal hardware, and age-related separation between some of the wooden staves. The bottom retaining wire/band is missing.

IMPORTANT: This is the OUTER WOODEN BUCKET ONLY and is being sold strictly as a decorative vintage piece. The original interior ice cream canister, dasher/paddle and other operating components are missing. It is not a complete or functioning ice cream maker/freezer.
