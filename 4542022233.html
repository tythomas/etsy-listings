Description

Set of 8 vintage Colony Color Crown Blue Riviera Blue sherbet glasses. Each measures 3 1/8" tall by 3 7/8" across the rim.


A beautiful matching set of eight vintage Colony Glass Color Crown Blue sherbet glasses in the sought-after Riviera Blue color. Produced circa 1976–1980s, these pressed glass sherbets feature Stem #77 with the distinctive oval thumbprint panels, decorative sawtooth bands, and wide pedestal foot.

Although officially marketed by Colony Glass as Color Crown Blue, this pattern is also widely known among collectors as King's Crown or Thumbprint glass, making it highly desirable for both replacement buyers and vintage glass collectors.

Complete matching sets of eight are becoming increasingly difficult to find, making this an excellent addition to a collection or a striking choice for entertaining.
