Description

Set of 4 vintage Frankoma Wagon Wheel dinner plates in the classic Prairie Green glaze, mold 94FL. Each plate measures approximately 10 1/4 inches in diameter.

The sculpted wagon-wheel design and variegated Prairie Green glaze give each plate its own mix of soft green and rich brown tones. Natural variation in the glaze is part of the character of Frankoma pottery, so the four plates are not identical in coloration.

CONDITION:
Very good vintage condition. No chips, cracks, crazing/crackling, or noticeable utensil scratching. One plate has two small manufacturing glaze skips/pops on the eating surface that expose the clay beneath; these are shown clearly in the photos.
