Description

Original 1931 University of Chicago commemorative plates by Copeland Spode, England, featuring detailed black-and-white architectural transferware scenes. Choose one of two historic campus buildings: Eckhart Hall or Swift Hall and Bond Chapel. Each selection purchases ONE plate, not both.

These collectible plates feature intricate Gothic-inspired borders, scalloped rims, and the University of Chicago seal on the reverse. They make distinctive display pieces for university alumni, Chicago history enthusiasts, and collectors of architectural transferware.
