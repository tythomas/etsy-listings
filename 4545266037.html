Description

Vintage Sexton "Sta-Kool" metal picnic cooler in its original green crinkle finish, with the yellow script logo complete on both sides and a sound galvanized liner inside. About 16 inches long.

Sexton Can Company built these in the United States through the 1950s, back when a cooler was a painted steel box with a galvanized liner and a drain plug rather than a moulded plastic shell. The green hammertone paint and the yellow Sta-Kool script are mid-century roadside America — the kind of box that rode in a trunk to ballgames and lake cabins.

Both long sides carry the full yellow "StaKool" script and the yellow SEXTON roundel, and both are complete and bright with no flaking through the lettering. That is the part collectors look for, and on this one it survived. The lid lifts off entirely, hooked over the body by wire clips, with a wire clasp that latches it down at the front and a heavy wire bail handle at each end. The yellow plastic drain plug is still in place in one end.

Inside, the galvanized steel liner is an even grey with age patina, unperforated and undented. It still holds ice and still drains.
