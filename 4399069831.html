Description

Classic mid-century wool plaid throw with navy base and orange, green, and cream tartan. Likely Faribault Woolen Mill, 1950s-60s. Excellent condition.

Cozy and timeless, this vintage wool plaid throw features a deep navy background woven with a bold orange hue, forest green, and cream stripes in a traditional tartan pattern. The dense twill weave and integrated twisted fringe edges are characteristic of Faribault Woolen Mill Co. (Minnesota) production from the 1950s–1960s.

The blanket measures approximately 68” x 53” including the 2¼” twisted fringe on both ends. Made of 100% wool, it has that authentic mid-century weight and slightly scratchy feel of coarser domestic wool — perfect for cabin, lodge, or farmhouse décor.

No holes, tears, or stains noted. The colors remain rich and bright with only light stray fibers consistent with age. This piece displays beautifully folded over a sofa, layered on a bed, or styled in a cozy reading nook.

While untagged, the weave density, color palette, and fringe construction closely match documented Faribault Woolen Mill designs from the era, sometimes described as “Hunt Plaid” or “Burnt Orange on Navy.”
