Description

Set of 8 antique Hand Painted Nippon dessert plates with pink flowers, green foliage and gold accents. Each plate measures 6 3/8 inches in diameter.

These matching Japanese export porcelain plates feature shaded pink blossoms, green and blue foliage, fine dark outlines, and gold seed-head motifs with flowing gold vine details. The gently scalloped edges are trimmed in gold. Small variations in the painted decoration give each plate its own character.

All eight plates carry the same green cherry blossom Hand Painted Nippon backstamp. They date to the Nippon export era, approximately 1891–1921. The specific manufacturer and pattern name are unidentified.

At 6 3/8 inches across, they are a lovely size for dessert, bread and butter, or a coordinated tea table.

Very good antique condition with light wear to the gold edging. No chips, cracks or repairs noted. The floral decoration remains bright and attractive. Please see the close-up photos for the gold edging and painted details.
