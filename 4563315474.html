Description

Set of four Bartlett Collins St Genevieve sherbet glasses in avocado green pressed glass.

Each has a flared bowl of bullseye and cane panels over fine flutes, a ball knop stem, and a wide foot with a sunray underside. The pattern dates from 1967. They serve as dessert dishes or champagne coupes.
