Description

This listing features one Imperial Glass purple slag glass Atterbury Lion, a Lion on Nest covered candy dish produced circa 1959-60.

Imperial’s circa-1960 issue uses Thomas B. Atterbury’s recumbent-lion dish-cover design, patented August 6, 1889. The lid is molded “PAT'D AUG 6 1889,” and the base carries an intertwined IG mark. The patent date belongs to the original design; this example dates to Imperial’s later 1959-60 production.

The molded purple slag glass combines plum purple, lavender, white, gray, and nearly black swirls. The pressed glass bowl is an openwork rim bowl with a raised foot, giving it the profile of an ornate pedestal bowl. Its fitted figural lid rests over the oval basin. The dramatic marbling and detailed reclining lion make this collectible glass a striking display piece suited to victorian decor while preserving its practical covered-dish form.
