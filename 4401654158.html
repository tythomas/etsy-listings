Vintage Park Lane Crystal Goblets – Set of 4 – Square Base – Often Sold as Cape Cod Pattern – Indiana Glass 1960s

Set of 4 vintage pressed crystal goblets with square radiating bases and textured diamond panels. Park Lane by Indiana Glass, made mid-1960s. Often sold as Cape Cod pattern. All excellent.

These sparkling pressed crystal goblets bring crisp mid-century texture to your table. Made by Indiana Glass in the mid-1960s, this design is part of the Park Lane pattern and features a sculpted square radiating foot, a single faceted stem knob, and a high-relief mix of crosshatch and elongated diamond panels.

Although these goblets appear on Replacements as “Cape Cod,” that categorization is based on bowl texture. The maker and pattern are Indiana Glass Park Lane. Listing both names helps collectors and shoppers find this exact style.

Each goblet is beautifully clear with strong pattern definition, a comfortable 8-ounce capacity, and the elegant proportions typical of Indiana’s mid-1960s pressed glass lines.
