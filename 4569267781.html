Description

One vintage Pepsi wooden bottle crate in yellow and navy blue with metal-bound corners and an open slatted bottom.

This commercial soda-delivery crate has large PEPSI block lettering on both long sides, elongated hand holes, solid wood side and end boards, spaced bottom slats, and metal edge and corner reinforcement secured with nails, rivets, and heavy staples. The block branding, construction, and documented comparable dated 1968 support a likely late-1960s to 1970s origin. A faint interior stamp appears to read STURDI-BILT, although the smaller wording, bottler, and location are no longer legible.

The worn yellow and navy finish gives the crate strong period advertising character for a beverage collection, bar display, shelf organizer, or rustic storage accent. The originality of every painted area has not been established, and the yellow may include working-life or later repainting.

You will receive one empty crate without bottle dividers. Price is for the single crate shown.
