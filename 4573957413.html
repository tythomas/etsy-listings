Description

This yellowware bowl pair is a nesting bowl set of two with mustard glaze, dark brown bands, and molded relief decoration. One marked USA and the other RRP Co

These substantial antique yellowware glazed earthenware bowls have broad rolled rims, glossy interiors, and repeating geometric and stylized foliate or wheat-like relief around the exteriors. Each brown band bowl is a rolled rim bowl with molded decoration. Their molded decoration and banded yellow-ware construction are consistent with early-20th-century kitchen pottery, likely circa the 1920s, although the production date remain unknown.

This vintage mixing bowl pair has a warm ochre-yellow glaze, dark brown stripes, and deeply molded surfaces that give the primitive pottery a strong kitchen presence. The bowls nest together for display and also work as coordinated vintage kitchenware or replacement bowls for a collected yellow-ware kitchen.

Sold together as one set of two bowls: one larger bowl and one smaller bowl. The bowls are not being offered separately.
