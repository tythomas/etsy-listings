Description

Set of four vintage golden amber pressed-glass sherbet dishes with flared bowls, graceful diagonal spiral ribs, and smooth circular pedestal feet. Each dish measures approx. 4 1/4" across the rim, 3" tall, and 2 3/4" across the foot, with a 4-ounce capacity. Sold together as one matching set of four.

The glass has a warm golden amber color. Raised spiral ribs decorate the bowls and end above the smooth pedestal bases. Vertical mold seams are visible from the rims through the bowls and down the bases, characteristic of their molded construction. No maker's marks were found.

The shape is similar to Federal Glass Diana sherbets and Hocking Spiral examples, but the exact maker and pattern have not been definitively established. A Depression-era attribution is possible, so these are offered as vintage amber spiral sherbet dishes rather than as a confirmed named pattern.

CONDITION: All four are free of chips, cracks, and roughness along the rims. Visible mold seams are manufacturing characteristics, not damage. Please review the photographs for the appearance of the glass and its vintage character.
