COLONIAL TULIP yellow 16 oz FLAT TUMBLERS  

4 are in VERY GOOD vintage condition - no chips or cracks - great shine -  rims smooth to the touch.  Glass is delicate -  air bubbles can be seen in  all the glasses ( not seen in the photos very well) . Glasses have  visible  mold lines and mold imperfections here and there. Some have slight surface scratches that can be seen when held to the light 

2 are in GOOD vintage condition and show light wear that can include water scaling and scratching. 

Each glass measures approximately  5 5/8" tall x 3 1/4" diameter at the top and holds 16 ounces when the glass is filled to the top of the rim! 

Made by Anchor Hocking - pattern discontinued and difficult to find in this size tumbler !! 

Circa 1960-80

Glasses are marked with anchor logo and USA 

Fantastic set of glasses for any retro glassware collector!
