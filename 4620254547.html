Description

Vintage leather suitcase with a distinctive fitted interior and warm caramel-brown exterior. This 21" travel case makes a striking decorative luggage display, photo prop, or accent for a vintage-inspired room.

Inside is brown-and-cream striped fabric lining, leather retaining straps with working snap fasteners, and metal organizational fittings. The red maker's badge reads 'Built by Boreitz N.Y.C.' A second emblem reads 'Fischer Fine Leather Ware, Walnut Hills.' Two metal keys are included; their operation in the locks has not been verified.

ESTIMATED AGE
Likely mid-20th century, approximately 1940s–1950s. 

CONDITION
This is an aged, used piece with visible exterior scuffs, scratches, and wear along the leather edges. One latch is seized and cannot engage with the brass-tone receiving notch. The lid consequently sits unevenly on that side and does not close flush, even with gentle pressure. The cause of the latch malfunction has not been verified and no working closure is promised. The interior has a musty odor and minimal staining. The applied exterior monogram appears upside down relative to the maker's metal label. Interior leather straps and snaps are intact and function properly. Please examine the photographs, especially those showing the latch and uneven closure.
