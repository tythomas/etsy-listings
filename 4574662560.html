Description

Antique circa-1930s butterfly appliqué cutter quilt featuring dozens of individually made butterflies in a wonderful assortment of colorful period scrap prints. Measures approximately 75 x 69 inches.

Each butterfly is appliquéd to a light cotton block and outlined with contrasting dark blanket/buttonhole stitching, with embroidered antennae adding character to each block. The butterflies include florals, ginghams, plaids, geometrics and solids consistent with 1930s feedsack-era and dress-print scrap fabrics. Soft green sashing with dusty pink cornerstones creates the grid, and the quilt has a solid green backing.

This quilt was found at an Illinois estate sale. The maker and exact history are unknown. It has not been washed by us.

MEASUREMENTS
Approximately 75 x 69 inches.

CONDITION — PLEASE READ
This is an old, well-loved quilt with significant age-related wear and is being sold as a cutter/repurpose quilt. There is fabric deterioration and loss to multiple butterfly appliqués, including areas where portions of appliqué fabric are missing; loose and missing blanket stitching; fraying and fabric loss along portions of the green sashing and pink corner blocks; exposed batting in some worn areas; and scattered staining/age discoloration. Please review all condition photographs closely.

Despite the deterioration, the quilt remains remarkably charming when displayed. Much of the butterfly appliqué remains intact, the colors and large assortment of vintage prints still have strong visual impact, and the quilt displays beautifully on a bed, over a chair, or as a decorative textile. It is not being represented as a structurally sound everyday-use bed quilt.

A wonderful survivor for a collector who appreciates timeworn textiles, or a source of period butterfly blocks and fabrics for careful repurposing, framing or textile art. Sold intact; any cutting or repurposing is left entirely to the buyer.
