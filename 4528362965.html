Description

Vintage pink Depression glass cake plate with a ribbed design and bubble foot pedestal. Measures 12¼ inches across.

A beautiful vintage pink Depression glass cake plate featuring an elegant ribbed pattern and low bubble foot pedestal. Measuring 12¼ inches in diameter, it works equally well as a cake stand, serving platter, centerpiece, or display piece.

Collectors often attribute this design to Hocking Glass (later Anchor Hocking) and refer to it as the AHC37 or Petticoat Ribbed pattern. Production is generally believed to date from the late 1930s into the early 1940s, spanning the end of the Depression glass era.
