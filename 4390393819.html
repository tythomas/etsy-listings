Description

GORGEOUS 1937 hand-appliquéd friendship quilt with 30 signed blocks from the Ward, Dodd & Barrier families of Houston, Texas. Hand quilted with soft pink sashing and original 1930s feedsack cottons — a true Depression-era treasure. Please see ALL photos as they capture the quilt's beauty and imperfections. 

This 1937 Friendship Signature Quilt is a rare and beautifully preserved example of Depression-era American quilting.
Each of its 30 blocks was lovingly hand-appliquéd and embroidered with the names of family and friends — many from the Ward and Dodd families, along with Barrier, Simpson, and Mitchell.
One block is dated “Bessie Barrier 1937,” providing an authentic time reference and confirming its Texas origin.

✨ Key Features:
*Date: Embroidered “1937” confirms Depression-era origin.
*Design: 30 unique hand-appliquéd blocks — tulips, butterflies, and floral motifs — each signed by its maker.
*Names Included: Predominantly Ward and Dodd families, with additional names such as Simpson, Barrier, and Mitchell — indicating a close-knit Houston, Texas community or extended family group.
*Construction: Entirely hand-appliquéd and hand-quilted with fine, even stitches.
*Fabric: Original 1930s cottons and feedsacks with pastel florals and solid fabrics.
*Color Palette: Pink and peach sashing, rose-pink binding, and pink calico feedsack backing.
*Condition: Very good vintage condition. A few small age spots, faint fold-line fading on back, as well as a small hole/tear ( see last four grid photos), One larger discoloration spot between blocks on the front noted, no holes or tears. Clean and well preserved. Hand soaked and air dried. 
*Size: 82" x 70"
*Provenance: Signed blocks, dated 1937, and embroidered “Houston, Texas” lend strong historical and regional authenticity.

This quilt beautifully reflects the creativity and unity of women in 1930s Texas — each name a personal contribution stitched with care. The dated blocks and regional surnames make it an exceptional genealogical and collectible textile.
