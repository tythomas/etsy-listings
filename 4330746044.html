These gorgeous glass decanters were issued in 1966 by Jim Beam as part of their collectible Kentucky Derby Decanters series.
Raised rings around the neck with a bohemian style pattern on main portion of bottle. Base and top of stopper have a sunburst design. Remnants of original stickers are attached. 

Measurements:
 10" tall
 11 1/4" with stopper in place
 4" wide across base. 

Bottom of bottle is embossed with the words "LIQUOR," "KY DRB 230

Both bottles are in VERY GOOD vintage condition - no chips or cracks, stopper fits tightly. 
NOTE- Green stopper has a part of the plastic washer missing - this does not affect its secure fit. see photos.
