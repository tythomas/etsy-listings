Description

An Oneida Community Bridal Wreath silverplate flatware service, still in its original fitted wood chest with red flocked lining and a knife rack in the lid.

Bridal Wreath came out in 1915 on Oneida's Par Plate line.

The mark on the back of a knife blade reads PAR PLATE / ONEIDA COMMUNITY, and that stamp is what dates it — the same pattern ran later under a different line. The knives are hollow-handled with wide blunt blades, the forks have four tines, and the spoons come in two sizes, all on the same handle. The chest stands the knives in a lid rack and holds the rest in felt-ribbed trays below.
