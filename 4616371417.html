Description

Vintage Wesson Oil advertising stoneware crock featuring cobalt-blue lettering, a bold blue band, and the slogan “For Making Good Things to Eat.” A substantial, heavy piece of vintage kitchen advertising with wonderful farmhouse display appeal.

This cream-colored stoneware crock has a thick rolled rim, thick rounded interior bottom, and an unglazed base.
