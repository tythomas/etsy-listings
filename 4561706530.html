Description

Six mismatched vintage Japanese teacups — cups only, no saucers. Floral, gold trimmed, mostly footed, 1950s.

Three are marked on the base: Noritake Grafton, Ucagco China Made in Japan, and Royal Sealy China Japan. A fourth reads Forget-me-not / Japan / 3002. Two are unmarked.

The six cups:
• Ucagco China, Japan — three gold claw feet, embossed swirl body, purple irises on one side and pink blossoms on the other, openwork gilt handle
• Pink lustre band with gold scrollwork, floral bouquet in the well, footed, double-loop gilt handle
• Ivory cup scattered with tiny pink and purple roses and blue forget-me-nots, gold lace rim, full gold handle
• Forget-me-not, Japan — embossed paneled body, scalloped gilt pedestal foot, pink roses and violets
• Royal Sealy China, Japan — swirl fluted body, tall gilt pedestal foot, magenta dahlia with blue and turquoise ferns
• Noritake Grafton — pale yellow band over white, gold filigree inside the rim, solid gold handle

Measurements:
• Rim diameter: 3 7/8"
• Height: 2 1/2"
• Capacity - Holds approximately 6 ounces

Gently hand washed and air dried. Hand wash only — the gold trim will not survive a dishwasher or a microwave.
