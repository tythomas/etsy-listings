Description

Set of four Noritake Spotlight Walnut Brown pressed-glass iced tea goblets, each approx. 6 1/2" tall.

Made in Japan and identified by surviving “Noritake Spotlight JAPAN” foil labels, these discontinued goblets feature flared bowls, raised geometric panels, short stems, and rounded-square feet. Replacement pieces catalogs the 6 1/2" iced tea goblet form and dates the line to 1974–1983.

This set of four goblets has transparent smoky amber-brown color and substantial molded texture. The amber / brown pressed glass brings warm character to brown stemware collections and works especially well for serving iced tea, water, or other cold beverages. Within the broader category of noritake glassware, these pieces offer useful replacement stemware for an existing Spotlight collection. Collectors often group pieces from this documented production period with 1970s 80s glassware.

Price is for one set of four matching goblets. Two goblets retain their foil labels.
