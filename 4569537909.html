Description

Antique Atterbury & Company “Atlas” milk glass figural compote, dating to approximately 1870–1890. This striking Victorian pressed-glass piece features the bearded Atlas figure forming the pedestal beneath a deep milk glass bowl with an elaborate pierced openwork rim.

Approximately 8 1/4" tall x 8 1/4" across the top, with a 5" diameter base. The bowl is approximately 2" deep.

Atterbury & Company operated in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, and the Atlas form is documented by both the Corning Museum of Glass and the Early American Pattern Glass Society. The Corning Museum dates Atlas to 1870–1890.

This particular compote has special multigenerational family provenance. It belonged to my grandmother, who was born in 1904, and was displayed for many years in her buffet. According to family history, it had been passed down to her from earlier family members and has remained in our family for generations.
