Description

Vintage iridescent luster water goblets, 7 inches tall, with a rainbow sheen on the bowl and a clear teardrop stem. 
12 available at the time of listing.

These are the larger water-goblet size, not the 6½-inch wine glass most iridescent lustre sets turn out to be, and the difference is obvious in the hand. The bowl is a deep tulip pressed with a soft diamond optic — rows of large rounded facets that break the light into pink, green and gold as the glass turns. Look at the rim: it is gently wavy and irregular rather than dead level, because it was fire-polished by hand rather than cut and ground.

The iridescence stops exactly at the bottom of the bowl. Stem and foot are left plain, clear and colorless — the lustre was sprayed onto a masked blank and fired, the mid-century method, and that hard stopping line is one of the things that dates these. The stem itself is a blown teardrop that swells in the middle and joins the pressed foot through a small stacked-ring collar; you can see the mould rings in it in the close-up photographs.
