Description

Large Victorian Charles Meakin Harvard lavender transferware platter measuring 15.5 inches wide.
Antique Charles Meakin Harvard oval serving platter in lavender transferware.

This large Victorian platter features an ornate floral transfer design in a soft lavender-purple color with a raised molded border and scalloped edge. Marked Harvard, Hanley, Charles Meakin, England on the underside.

Research indicates the Harvard pattern was produced during the late Victorian period, approximately 1885-1895.

Measurements:
- 15.5 inches wide
- 12.2 inches across
- 1.75 inches tall

Condition:
- No chips
- No cracks
- No hairlines
- No repairs
- Glaze retains a nice shine
- Light utensil wear consistent with age and use
- Minor manufacturing specks and transfer variations typical of Victorian Staffordshire pottery

This is a substantial, heavy platter with raised molded detailing around the rim. The lavender transfer decoration remains crisp and well preserved. Underside retains the Charles Meakin Harvard backstamp along with impressed factory mark "SP 22" and a small purple "17" mark.

A beautiful piece for transferware collectors, English country decor, cottage style decorating, or display with antique ironstone collections.
