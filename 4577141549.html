Description

Homer Laughlin Cavalier Eggshell serving pieces with teal green bands and platinum trim, made in the USA during the mid-1950s. Choose the large platter, creamer, covered sugar bowl, or salt and pepper set.

PLEASE NOTE: Pieces are sold individually. Select one serving piece from the variation menu. The group photograph shows all available choices; it is not one complete set.

AVAILABLE PIECES:

• Large Platter — 13 3/4 inches long x 11 1/2 inches wide. Minor platinum loss and very light surface scratching.
• Creamer — 4 1/4 inches tall. Minor platinum loss.
• Covered Sugar — 4 1/4 inches tall with the lid in place. Minor platinum loss.
• Salt & Pepper — Matching pair, each 2 3/4 inches tall. Both retain their cork stoppers. Visible platinum wear is present along the lower edges, with lighter wear around the perforated tops and scattered light surface wear.

The Cavalier Eggshell line was introduced by Homer Laughlin in 1953. Backstamp date codes visible among these pieces indicate 1954–1955 production at the Newell, West Virginia factory. The curved handles, pedestal feet, teal bands, and platinum accents give the serving pieces a distinctive mid-century formal look.

CONDITION:
All pieces are free of chips, cracks, and glaze crackling. Condition varies slightly by piece and is described above. Please review the close-up photographs. These are approximately 70-year-old vintage china pieces and show the light wear expected from age and previous use.

Hand washing is recommended to protect the platinum trim. Not suitable for microwave use.

A useful replacement for an existing Homer Laughlin Cavalier Eggshell service or an elegant teal and ivory accent for a mid-century table.
