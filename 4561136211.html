Description

American Tourister Escort three piece luggage set — two brown marbled vinyl hardside suitcases and a matching shoulder carry on bag, 1970s.

The original Escort hangtags are still on the handles, and the sewn label inside reads Escort, made in Japan for American Luggage.

MAKER: American Tourister
LINE: Escort
MADE: Japan
CIRCA: late 1960s - 1970s

Measurements:
• Large suitcase: 26 1/2" wide x 20" tall x 8" deep
• Medium suitcase: 23 1/2" wide x 16 1/2" tall x 6 3/4" deep — nests inside the large one
• Shoulder bag: 16" wide x 12" tall

Features:
• Molded hard shell under marbled vinyl, with chrome edge banding
• Three dial combination lock in the center of each suitcase, flanked by chrome latches
• Leather wrapped top handles on swivel posts
• Cream damask satin lining, elastic lid pocket, tie ribbons and zip pockets at both ends
• Shoulder bag has a metal tooth zipper with a lock catch, a front zip pocket, and an adjustable strap
• Period owner's ID tags still in the handle windows
• Metal AMERICAN plate on the front edge of each suitcase

CONDITION
• Scuffing and fine surface scratching throughout, heaviest on the shoulder bag
• Small yellow paint fleck on the front of the shoulder bag, pictured
• Short dark scuff on one end panel of the shoulder bag, pictured
• Long scratch across the top of the large suitcase, and dark smudge marks near one lid corner, pictured
• Chrome banding and latches are dull rather than bright, with rub through along the edge bands
• Leather handle wraps are creased and darkened
• Brown stain about the size of a fingertip on the padded lid roll inside, pictured
• Lining is cream with uneven yellowing toward the base
• Elastic lid pocket and both tie ribbons are intact
• The combination locks are set to unknown combinations, and no key is included for the shoulder bag's zipper lock
• Sold as a display and storage set rather than as working travel luggage
