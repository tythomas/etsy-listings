Description

XLarge antique American stoneware mixing bowl attributed to Red Wing, Minnesota, in the No. 2 milk pan / shoulder bowl form, dating approximately 1906–1920s.

This is an impressive, heavy utilitarian bowl with a broad straight shoulder, creamy off-white glaze, darker rim edge, and the firing character associated with early working stoneware. Research found documented Red Wing No. 2 milk pan / shoulder bowls at approximately 13 inches diameter x 6 inches tall, an exceptionally close match to this bowl's form and dimensions. This example is unmarked, so the Red Wing attribution is based on documented form, dimensions, glaze, and construction rather than a visible maker's stamp.

MEASUREMENTS
Approximately 13 1/4 inches diameter x 5 3/4 inches tall.

MATERIAL
Heavy glazed American stoneware with a cream/off-white zinc or Bristol-type glaze.

CONDITION
Antique condition with age and manufacturing character. A hairline is present on the underside of the bottom; it does not visibly continue through to the inside of the bowl. - pictured. There are numerous glaze pinholes, firing specks, glaze inconsistencies, and small surface irregularities inside and out. These early manufacturing characteristics are visible in the photos. No major rim chips are noted. Please review all photos closely, particularly the underside and glaze-detail images.
