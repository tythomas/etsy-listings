Description

Set of six vintage Brockway Glass American Concord amber water goblets, featuring raised starburst medallions, decorative scrollwork, and richly textured pressed glass. Each measures approx. 6 1/4" tall, 2 7/8" across the rim, and holds 9 ounces.

Made by Brockway Glass Co. in the American Concord pattern, documented by Replacements Ltd. as produced in 1977–1978. The warm golden amber glass features an ornate stippled background, starburst motifs, and decorated pedestal bases.

Includes six matching goblets, sold together as one set. All six are reported free of damage, with no chips or cracks. Vintage glass may show minor manufacturing characteristics or gentle signs of age. Please review photographs for details.
