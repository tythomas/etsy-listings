Description
Beautiful set of four vintage ruby red goblets featuring a dramatic swirl bowl and sculptural twisted stem. These pieces date to the mid-century West Virginia art glass era, most likely the 1960s (1950s–1970s range). The deep cranberry ruby tone and hand-blown construction are characteristic of makers such as Pilgrim Glass and Kanawha Glass.

Collectors frequently confuse this style with **Fostoria’s Jamestown Ruby** pattern. While these goblets are not part of the Fostoria line, they make an excellent visual companion to Jamestown pieces. The rich color blends beautifully with other mid-century ruby patterns and displays wonderfully in holiday tablescapes, cottage décor, and vintage barware collections.
