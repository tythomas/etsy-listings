GORGEOUS pattern and design !!!! 

The discontinued pattern was produced between 1964-1975 and copies the famous Schumann Empress Dresden Flowers pattern. The pattern features a scalloped edging with platinum trim, floral rim & center. Each piece has a grey tone detailed floral design. 

Made in Bavaria Germany in the mid 60's  

This 24 pc set is in fantastic vintage  condition - no chips, cracks or heavy scratching. Glaze is intact and pieces have a nice shine. Little to no surface scratching is noted on plate surfaces - not sure these were ever used.  Platinum edging is intact and shows no wear or loss.
