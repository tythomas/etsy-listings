Description

Antique Palmer Cox Brownies ABC plate — embossed tin, full alphabet border, patented 1896.

PATENTED 1896 is cast into the metal, and the verse around the Brownie scene reads: "Up the table see them climb / Ready for a jolly time / In the dishes on a run / Brownies flocking to the fun."

DESIGN: Palmer Cox Brownies
MARK: PATENTED 1896, raised in the metal
MATERIAL: unpainted pressed metal, tin or pewter type
BORDER: full raised A through Z with small animal figures between the letters
