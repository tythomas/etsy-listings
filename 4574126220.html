Description

This antique Shenango China Inca Ware bowl set includes two matching bowls with Jul’s Danish Farm script and burgundy striping on tan glazed china.  

These matching commercial china bowls carry the green Inca Ware / Shenango China / New Castle, Pa. / U.S.A. backstamp. The Jul’s Danish Farm lettering is a custom advertising for a 1940s restaurant in Rock Falls, Illinois. This restaurant had many scandals / lawsuits that eventually caused its closure in 1951. Google it for the whole story, it's fascinating! 

Each ceramic bowl has substantial footed construction, rounded rims, a beige tan brown color palette, a burgundy exterior stripe, and matching interior script. The footed bowl pair has distinctive American diner and hotel china character, while the burgundy accents complement vintage burgundy dinnerware collections. They make an inviting display pair and offer an unusual replacement or collecting opportunity for Shenango and American commercial china buyers.
